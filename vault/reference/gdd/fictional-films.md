# Fictional films

Rook quotes action films that exist only in his universe (owner 2026-10-09).

## Rules
- A title may parody a real film title.
- The line is always original: never a real film line, quoted or paraphrased, never a well-known catchphrase, even uncommon ones ([[dialogue-tone]] T3).
- The quote names the film: "Like in Cry Hard 2: …".
- Vesna may answer with a verdict on the film or on how the last attempt went.
- Only titles in this list may be quoted; new titles go through the owner.

## Catalogue
- Cry Hard (owner)
- Die Hardly (owner)
- Try Hard (owner)
- Armageddon't (provisional)
- Total Refund (provisional)
- Hard Vacuum 2 (provisional)

## Pool (owner 2026-10-10)
The six titles above are examples. The catalogue grows to about 50 parody titles of badass 80s and 90s action films (newer ones allowed if they keep the spirit), recognisable as parodies. The text agent draws from it and need not use every title. Status: to be generated (task A4 film pool).
