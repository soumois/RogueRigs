# Crew characters

Names and personalities from the owner (2026-10-09). Example lines and past jobs are provisional.

## Rook — the miner (player character)
- Cocky; waves the odds away; improvises first, thinks later, and defends a shaky plan with total confidence.
- Proud scrapper: loves his gear, loves it when things blow up, always counting his cut.
- Always in the wrong place at the wrong time; complains about it and keeps fighting.
- Under stress: punchlines, or a "quote" from a film in [[fictional-films]].

## Vesna — the colleague in orbit
- Speaks only through the drone ([[lore]] L2).
- Calm, precise, mission first; short dry sentences.
- Cuts Rook down when he gets carried away: a small joke, or the name of one of his plans that already failed.
- Cares about him; never says so.

## Dynamic
- A tight crew bickering; the jabs are affection.
- Typical exchange: Rook boasts or quotes a film, Vesna deflates it.
- Vesna teases Rook's plans and bravado, never the player's skill ([[dialogue-tone]] T2).

## Rigs jargon
Shop talk between people who have worked together for years: coolant, torque, drill bit, payload, burn window, hull patch, yield, redline.

## Past jobs (only these may be referenced)
- The Ceres job: Rook's plan; they lost the ship.
- The Pallas run: Rook cut through a debris field "for the shortcut".

## Example lines (reference only, never reused: T8)
- Rook: "Coolant's redlining. Drill harder."
- Rook: "Big robot. Bigger hole. Easy math."
- Vesna: "You said that before the Ceres job."
- Vesna: "Plan B is not shouting louder."
