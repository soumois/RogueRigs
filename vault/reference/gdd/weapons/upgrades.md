# Upgrades

- Offered on each level-up: 3 cards, pick 1 ([[xp-level-up]]).
- An upgrade applies to one weapon, or to all weapons at a lower value.
- Upgrades are never removed during a run.

## Stats (M1)
- Fire rate, damage, critical chance, critical damage.
- Stacking (approved): percentage bonuses add together (2 × +20 % damage = ×1.4; 3 × = ×1.6). Point bonuses add (2 × +5 crit chance points = +10 points).

## Effects
- Piercing projectiles (M2).
- 4-direction fire (M2): the main shot stays on the aim; the 3 extra shots are weaker, to protect pillar P2.
- EMP, stuns machines: provisional, M3 if time allows.
- Ice, burn, other elements: deferred ([[wishlist]]).

## Open
- Values per upgrade and per rarity; the all-weapons reduction factor.
- Drone dig speed as an upgrade.
- Per-weapon-type upgrades: deferred ([[wishlist]]).
