# Lore

## World
- Industrial sci-fi mining on an asteroid.
- The Kessler-Voss Mining Consortium claims rich deposits and guards them with automated security robots until its extraction crews arrive.

## The Rigs (player side)
- Independent miners who land in that window to strip the ore first. They steal a claim that is not theirs yet.
- Rook is the miner on the ground. Vesna stays in orbit and remote-controls the drone; all her dialogue comes through the drone ([[crew-characters]]).
- Everything on the Rigs' side is repaired, patched and bolted together, in contrast with the clean corporate robots.

## Rules (the critic cites these ids)
- L1 Every enemy on site is a robot; no company human is on the asteroid. Rook is the only person on the ground.
- L2 Vesna never sets foot on the asteroid; she speaks only through the drone.
- L3 The robots are only doing their job; jokes target the company, not robot cruelty.
- L4 The Rigs know the claim is not theirs yet: they are here first, not in the right.

## Lore behind each mechanic
| Mechanic | Lore meaning |
