# Company voice (Kessler-Voss)

Registers from the owner (2026-10-09). Example lines are provisional.

## To customers and the public: propaganda
- Upbeat, glossy, absolute confidence; "we" and "your future"; mining sold as a gift to humanity.
- Used on: billboards, loading tips, ads, public slogans.
- Examples: "Kessler-Voss. Every Rock a Promise." · "Your Future, Responsibly Extracted."

## To threats: bureaucracy
- Cold, impersonal, procedural: notice numbers, asset language, invoices; no emotion, no insult.
- Rook and Vesna are "unauthorized personnel" or "an extraction irregularity", never named.
- Used on: alert bar tiers, robot announcements, network messages the console intercepts.
- Examples: "Unauthorized extraction detected. Asset recovery initiated." · "Notice 7-B: trespassers will be invoiced."

## Rules
- The company never jokes; the humor comes from the gap between its tone and the situation.
- Robots carry the company voice; they never banter.
- Company text never uses Rigs jargon or Rigs slang.
- Example lines are reference only, never reused ([[dialogue-tone]] T8).
