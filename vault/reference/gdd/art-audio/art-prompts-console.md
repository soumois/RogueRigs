# Console C2 — turnaround prompt and Meshy mesh

Validated sheet (front, side, back): `vault/assets/console-c2-turnaround.webp`, design C2 "uplink mast", generated in ChatGPT by the owner on 2026-10-23. The Meshy mesh was generated by the owner from it (no credit spent by the crew). The owner may have adapted the object wording; the design paragraph is as proposed.

## Prompt (verbatim)
```
Turnaround sheet for 3D modeling of a field uplink console: front view, side view (exact 90 degrees) and back view, all three at the same size, same angle and same framing, subject centred with a margin, no ground.
Style: stylized flat-shaded low-poly game asset, chunky blocky volumes with large flat facets, facets clearly visible, no smoothing, no textures, clean hard-surface look. Rigid parts only: plates, pistons, bolts, no cloth, no organic forms. The silhouette must read clearly from a steep top-down camera.
Background and light: plain white background, even neutral lighting, no shadows, no effects, no text, no UI.
Design: a field uplink terminal on a heavy square base, with a tall central mast topped by a large upward-facing satellite dish that reads as a circle from above. A side console block with a slanted dark screen and one big round data socket where a drone plugs in, a coiled cable spool bolted beside it. Four stabilizer feet at the corners. Colors and wear: patched, mismatched white and teal plates with rust chips, bolts, one panel replaced by a different teal plate, improvised salvaged look; amber indicator lights only, no red, yellow, green, blue or violet. No logo.
```
