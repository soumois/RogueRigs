# Shooter, heavy, kamikaze, chaser v2 — Meshy prompt chains and sheets

Prompts written 2026-10-21 on the model of the chaser chain ([[chaser-meshy-pipeline]]). Sheets generated by the owner and stored 2026-10-22. Meshes made by the owner in Meshy 2026-10-22: [[robots-meshy-raw-2026-10-22]].

## Files
- Prompts: `Art/Source/{shooter,heavy,kamikaze}-meshy/prompts/` and `Art/Source/chaser-meshy/prompts-v2/`, each `sheet.txt`, `front.txt`, `left.txt`, `right.txt`, `back.txt` (verbatim, English).
- Sheets (owner): `vault/assets/shooter-turnaround.webp` (front, left, right, back), `heavy-turnaround.webp`, `kamikaze-turnaround.webp`, `chaser-turnaround-v2.webp` (front, side, back). `chaser-turnaround.png` stays the source of the mesh in game.

## Chain (ChatGPT / GPT Image 2)
1. `sheet.txt`: Image 1 = the robot cropped from `vault/assets/enemies-lineup.jpg` ([[art-prompts-robots]]) (chaser v2: `chaser-turnaround.png`), Image 2 = `chaser-turnaround.png` (style reference).
2. `front.txt`: Image 1 = front crop of the sheet, Image 2 = the sheet.
3. `left.txt`, `back.txt`: Image 1 = the generated front, Image 2 = the sheet, Image 3 = the crop of that view.
4. `right.txt`: Image 3 = the side crop mirrored (heavy, kamikaze, chaser v2); shooter: the right side crop, not mirrored.
5. The four views are the Meshy multi-view input.

## What the sheets show (differs from the prompts)
- Shooter: cannon on the robot's right forearm, red emitter; yellow hexagon logo on the head front and both shoulders; antenna on top.
- Heavy: hazard stripes on shoulders, forearms, knees and feet; big dark fists; red slot under the eye.
- Kamikaze: no fins: two upper arms and two lower legs around a faceted sphere core with glowing orange seams and a central hexagon; separate head with the red eye; two yellow-tipped vanes; no logo plate visible.
- Chaser v2: antenna on the robot's left (so the right view is not an exact mirror); no logo plate visible on the front.

## Design choices (owner to confirm)
- Heavy and kamikaze parts are `open` in their specs ([[enemy-heavy]], [[enemy-kamikaze]]): the sheets are proposals.
- Effects excluded from images (laser, flame, explosion): Meshy would model them.
- Background light grey, as in the chaser view prompts (the locked turnaround phrase says white).
