# Drone — turnaround prompt and Meshy mesh

Validated sheet (front, side, back): `vault/assets/drone-turnaround.webp`, generated in ChatGPT by the owner. The Meshy mesh was generated by the owner from this image on 2026-10-20 (no credit spent by the crew). The final image has two side pods, not four: the prompt below asks for four, the owner kept the two-pod result.

## Prompt (verbatim)
```
Character turnaround sheet for 3D modeling of a small floating mining drone, remote-controlled by a colleague in orbit: front view, side view (exact 90 degrees) and back view, all three at the same size, same angle and same framing, subject centred with a margin, neutral hover pose, no stand, no ground.
Style: stylized flat-shaded low-poly game asset, chunky blocky volumes with large flat facets, facets clearly visible, no smoothing, no textures, clean hard-surface look.
Design: compact, rigid mechanical body about the size of a miner's head and shoulders, with a clearly readable front and back so its facing direction is obvious from above. A boxy front nose with one round amber glowing camera eye. A short antenna with a small comms dish on top at the back. Four small chunky thruster pods at the corners, the two side pods wider than the body so the silhouette reads from a steep top-down view. A precise mining laser emitter mounted under the front of the body, dark metal, with no beam. Rigid parts only: plates, pistons, bolts, no cloth, no organic forms.
Colors and wear: patched, mismatched white and teal plates with rust chips, bolts, rivets and welded repairs, one panel visibly replaced by a different teal plate, a small bolted-on cable bundle, a repaired and improvised salvaged look. The amber eye is the only warm light; no red, no yellow, no green, no blue, no violet elsewhere.
Background and light: plain white background, even neutral lighting, no shadows, no effects, no laser beam, no text, no UI, no logo.
```

## Files
- Meshy download (owner, untouched): `Art/Source/drone-meshy/meshy/Meshy_AI_Rusty_Satellite_Drone_1020195641_texture.fbx` (22 MB, 5138 tris, no dish) with `..._texture.png` (base colour), `_normal.png`, `_metallic.png`, `_roughness.png`, `_metallic_roughness.png`.
- Game meshes (task-252-01): `Art/Meshes/drone/` (`SM_DroneBase.fbx`, `SM_DroneLaser.fbx`, `SM_Drone_assembled.fbx`, `drone_parts.report.json`, base colour, normal and metallic-roughness maps); scripts `Tools/art/blender/drone_game_ready.py`, `drone_cut_parts.py`, `drone_preview.py`.
