# Audio direction

- Sound effects: openly licensed sounds, found and scored against the style guide by the sound agent; listed in `sound_manifest.json`.
- Music: tracks per alert level, produced through the owner's existing Suno / kie.ai pipeline. Owner 2026-11-13 (task-519): « Une seule piste en jeu pour l'instant. »; tracks per alert level stay a later step (open). Three tracks, generated by him with Suno (instrumental): main menu, loading, in game; used as provided (no loop preparation), each restarting from its start when it ends, played through SC_Music (Music slider). Levels (provisional): menu 1.0, loading 1.0, in game 0.5. Transition: 1 s fade out of the old track and 1 s fade in of the new one. The in-game track keeps playing under pause, level-up, shop, level end and death screens, and restarts from its start at each level. The three tracks are placeholders, replaced before release.
- Sound palette (provisional, owner 2026-11-12, task-511 Q2): industrial, dry, metallic, little reverb; every candidate is scored against it. Owner 2026-11-13: the candidates in that palette were refused ("trouver une base de son plus orienté JV"; metallic impact "trop aigu"; "ça part vite dans l'aigu"); a new palette is not restated in his words: open.
- Sources (provisional, owner 2026-11-12, task-511 Q3): CC0 only; Kenney packs first, then OpenGameArt and freesound filtered CC0; Sonniss GDC excluded (not CC0, archive size). Author, link and licence of each kept sound in `sound_manifest.json`. Owner 2026-11-13 (by his picks): 7 of the 8 kept sounds generated by him with ElevenLabs Sound Effects, licence per his subscription; the level-up sound found by him, author and licence unknown; both to confirm by the owner. Manifest: `Art/Source/audio/sound_manifest.json`.
- Event list, slice 1 (provisional, owner 2026-11-12, task-511 Q1): player shot (one sound shared by all player weapons), player bullet impact (enemy or block), enemy death (all types; explosions not included), damage taken by the player, XP pickup, level up, button hover, button click (every enabled button on every screen). Adding or splitting an event is a line in the event table. Other events (slice 2) and music (slice 3): open.
- Mix rules (provisional, owner 2026-11-12, task-511 Q5): every effect plays through SC_Effects under SC_Master; at most 4 shots, 4 impacts and 3 XP pickups at once, the oldest stopped; ±5 % random pitch on shots, impacts and pickups; world sounds (shot, impact, death) positioned on screen, UI and player sounds centred; UI sounds also play while the game is paused.
- Levels (provisional, owner 2026-11-13, task-512 and task-513): player shot at Volume 0.5 (was 1.0), every player weapon. Player bullet impact on a wall (any hit that is not an enemy: blocks of every kind, map edge) is its own event: Volume 0.5 at the player, full up to 200 uu from the player, then fading linearly to silent at the screen edge distance; impact on an enemy unchanged. Distance = player (miner) to impact point. All values are lines of the event table, retouched by ear.
- Placeholders (owner 2026-11-13): every current sound is a placeholder, replaced before release; the licence of a placeholder does not block. Sounds are generated by the owner with ElevenLabs from English prompts written in the chat, then imported.
- Slice 1 additions (provisional, owner 2026-11-13, task-514 to task-516): enemy death fades with distance from the player like the wall impact (same distances, level at the player unchanged); ore pickup plays the XP pickup sound for now (own event); explosions (every explosion) have one placed event, distance measured from the explosion edge (0 inside the radius), full level up to the edge then linear fade to silent at the screen edge distance.
- Digging (provisional, owner 2026-11-13, task-517): laser mining, only the player mines; one centred looping sound while the player mines, fade in 0.15 s and fade out 0.15 s, no pitch jitter.
- Effects slider preview (provisional, owner 2026-11-13, task-520): moving the Effects slider plays the player shot sound, centred, at each 5 % step, at most one every 0.15 s, at the new level, paused screens included; Master and Music play nothing.
- Distance sounds (owner 2026-11-13, task-522): one generic distance sound (a point, full level then linear fade, one-shot or loop) carries every distance-faded sound; the explosion is a child of it measuring from its radius edge. Extractor rotors (provisional): one looped sound for the four rotors at the extractor centre, while its rotors spin in the level (arrival until it leaves the view, extraction from landing to the end of the takeoff behind the success screen), constant level, full up to 800 uu, linear to silent at 2150 uu.
- Robot impact (provisional, owner 2026-11-13, task-523): the player bullet impact on a robot plays at 0.6 of its level up to 150 uu from the player, rising linearly to its full level at 900 uu and beyond; the wall impact is unchanged.
- Zone objective complete (provisional, owner 2026-11-13, task-527): one one-shot sound when a zone objective is validated (bonus chest hack complete, console hack complete), the same sound for both for now, at the objective centre, full up to 200 uu, linear to silent at 1075 uu.
- Selection (provisional, owner 2026-11-12, task-511 Q4): 3 candidates per event, the owner picks by ear; rejected candidates are temporary (`Saved/Scratch/`), kept sounds have their source in `Art/Source/audio/`. Slice 1 sounds kept (owner 2026-11-13): assets `/Game/RogueRigs/Audio/SFX/SW_*`, levels equalized at import; built as [[sys-audio]] (event table, how to add a sound).
- Milestone: M3.
