# Visual style

- Flat-shaded low-poly, moving toward a more cartoon look (owner 2026-11-05, provisional, [[task-470-cartoon-floor-texture]]): the painted floor, the walls, the actor shadows and the miner light come first; nothing else changes without a new request.
- Camera: steep, near-vertical perspective; the miner is always centered.
- Camera shake mixing (provisional, owner 2026-11-02, [[task-416-camera-shake-mixing-falloff]]): shakes of different sources (hit, explosion, heavy slam, chest) add up; a new shake of the same source restarts it at the stronger of the two strengths; the total translation is capped at 12 uu. Translation only, no rotation. A shake at a world point is full strength at the point, falls linearly to 50 % at its max distance, and is 0 beyond.
- Miner light (owner 2026-11-04, provisional, [[task-425-miner-floor-wall-light]]): a point light carried by the miner lights only the floor and the terrain blocks (rock, ore, ore crystals); robots, miner, drone, pickups, barrels, chest, asteroids and the void receive none. It casts the shadows of the rock, ore and heal ore blocks only, nothing else casts from it; its height is the owner's own setting in BP_Miner (owner 2026-11-05, provisional, [[task-445-miner-light-wall-shadows]], replaces "no shadow"); sun and sky unchanged. Colour, intensity and pool size calibrated on `Art/Source/cartoon-look/reference/owner-2026-11-05-ingame-light-walls.webp`: warm orange at the centre fading to pink-violet on the floor (owner 2026-11-05, provisional, [[task-473-miner-light-calibration]]; was warm cream 255/194/158 and a pool of about 400 uu radius); floor under the miner never burnt (below about 205/255).
- Blocks are low, their top level with the top of the miner (owner 2026-11-06, provisional, [[task-475-wall-height-miner]]: raised slightly, was lower); a robot right behind a wall must still be spotted; the top face is the most visible face.
- Walls (owner 2026-11-05, provisional, [[task-471-wall-look-reference]]): raised violet plateaus with light-lilac tops, a thin glowing orange-yellow mineral border on a slight chamfer of the top edge, with slight intensity variations and a subtle glow on the top face near the edge, never thick, perfectly regular or neon-like, no post-process outline; sides in stylised low-poly rock: large clearly visible vertical facets, slightly tilted planes and subtle depth changes, irregular vertical edges, facet tints of dark violet, mid violet and a few lighter areas, lit and shaded facets alternating, a few subtle vertical reliefs; no cracks, no damaged look, no small noisy detail, facets neither too geometric nor repetitive; the top keeps its texture and stays continuous without lines between slabs; organic outline, proportions and heights unchanged (owner 2026-11-06, provisional, [[task-480-rock-slab-chamfer-facets]]; was dark sides with soft vertical shading and no visible cut between facets, as on `Art/Source/cartoon-look/reference/owner-2026-11-06-wall-section-target.png`, [[task-478-wall-lines-emissive-edge]]; before that dark stratified sides, as on `Art/Source/cartoon-look/reference/owner-2026-11-05-ingame-light-walls.webp`); damage cracks and tilted plates still read; void asteroids unchanged.
- Floor: the owner's hand-painted cartoon flagstone texture `Art/Source/floor-texture/owner-2026-11-05-floor-cartoon.webp` (magenta-purple flagstones, dark cracks with a pink rim highlight), used as is, no added relief, a flagstone about the size seen on `Art/Source/cartoon-look/reference/owner-2026-11-05-ingame-light-walls.webp`, no rubble (owner 2026-11-05, provisional, [[task-470-cartoon-floor-texture]]; replaces the task-273 cracked faceted plates, [[art-style-guide]] A13); arena edge is a cliff into space with stars and a planet.
- Void background (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-332-space-background]], [[task-335-space-texture-layers]]): layered: procedural stars and generated tileable cloud textures at depths, slow drift, never a single pasted image (replaces "procedural"). Unlit planes under the arena at real depths, parallax from the perspective camera: procedural stars Z -12000 (opaque, black base, slow twinkle), deep texture Z -8000 (additive, large visible shapes), cloud texture Z -5000 (additive, replaces the procedural nebula), sparse procedural dust Z -2000 (additive). Textures generated by the art agent through Kie.ai, owner-validated, square, tileable, repeated large; motion in the materials only (slow UV drift, no rotation); no Niagara dust. Bold, not timid (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, B1 to B4 of [[task-335-space-texture-layers]], replaces the 20 to 35 luma band of [[task-333-nebula-visible]]): cloud peak about 70 to 100 sRGB luma near an edge; bright cores may exceed the floor strip beside the edge, but the void mean stays below the floor mean so the floor still reads first; near-black share of the images about 20 to 25 percent, dense filaments, bright cores, dark dust lanes. Screen darkener (owner 2026-11-04, provisional, [[task-426-screen-radial-darkener]], replaces the miner-centred mask): black added to every pixel grows linearly in circles around the screen centre, from CenterDarkness at the centre to CornerDarkness at the corners (current owner-tuned values, 0.2 and 1 on 2026-11-04, live in [[mpp-player-light-mask]]; radius = centre-to-corner distance; pixel = colour x (1 - black)); it no longer depends on the miner position. Clear zone (owner 2026-11-04, provisional, [[task-427-darkener-clear-zone]]): no black inside ClearRadius 0.15 of the centre-to-corner distance, a soft fade from no black at ClearRadius to the full effect at FadeRadius 0.35, the circles unchanged beyond. Void pixels (world Z below -200) get at most 0.5 black. No planet yet (later request); no glow at the floor-void edge. Colours in [[color-code]].
- Nearer asteroids (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-334-near-asteroids]], replaces "no drifting debris"): the arena is an asteroid, and other asteroids float in the void around it: about 12 lit, flat-shaded faceted low-poly rocks, 200 to 1200 uu across, tops between Z -300 and -2500, over void only, 300 to 3000 uu outside the floor edge, all around the arena; slow self-rotation 1 to 5 deg/s, no drift; no collision, no shadow cast; the darkening mask applies. Colour in [[color-code]].
- The Rigs' side looks repaired and bolted together; corporate robots look clean.
- Actor shadows (owner 2026-11-05, provisional, [[task-472-actor-blob-shadows]]): the miner, the drone, every robot, the turrets, the barrels and the bonus chest each have a shadow directly beneath them that follows their world position and ignores every light; dark desaturated purple-grey, never pure black; opacity and scale adjustable per actor type and globally; slightly softened edge with a clearly readable silhouette, never very transparent or very blurred. These actors cast no light shadow (sun, sky, miner light); walls keep theirs. Replaces the task-264 blob discs. Colour in [[color-code]].
- No textures, no UI in reference art; strong directional light with lifted shadows; floor with no pebbles or rubble so robots, pickups and blocks stand out.
- Key art: `vault/assets/keyart.jpg` (readability reference, not a fidelity promise).
- M1 is grey box: plain cubes colored by block type after [[color-code]]; final assets come later ([[sys-grid]]).
