# Visual style

- Flat-shaded low-poly.
- Camera: steep, near-vertical perspective; the miner is always centered.
- Camera shake mixing (provisional, owner 2026-11-02, [[task-416-camera-shake-mixing-falloff]]): shakes of different sources (hit, explosion, heavy slam, chest) add up; a new shake of the same source restarts it at the stronger of the two strengths; the total translation is capped at 12 uu. Translation only, no rotation. A shake at a world point is full strength at the point, falls linearly to 50 % at its max distance, and is 0 beyond.
- Miner light (owner 2026-11-04, provisional, [[task-425-miner-floor-wall-light]]): a point light carried by the miner lights only the floor and the terrain blocks (rock, ore, ore crystals); robots, miner, drone, pickups, barrels, chest, asteroids and the void receive none. No shadow. Warm cream 255/194/158. Lit pool about 400 uu radius around the miner; floor under the miner never burnt (below about 205/255).
- Blocks are always low (no occlusion); the top face is the most visible face.
- Floor of cracked faceted plates, painted look, edges catching the light, no rubble (owner 2026-10-21, provisional, task-273; was "clean floor", [[art-style-guide]] A13); arena edge is a cliff into space with stars and a planet.
- Void background (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-332-space-background]], [[task-335-space-texture-layers]]): layered: procedural stars and generated tileable cloud textures at depths, slow drift, never a single pasted image (replaces "procedural"). Unlit planes under the arena at real depths, parallax from the perspective camera: procedural stars Z -12000 (opaque, black base, slow twinkle), deep texture Z -8000 (additive, large visible shapes), cloud texture Z -5000 (additive, replaces the procedural nebula), sparse procedural dust Z -2000 (additive). Textures generated by the art agent through Kie.ai, owner-validated, square, tileable, repeated large; motion in the materials only (slow UV drift, no rotation); no Niagara dust. Bold, not timid (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, B1 to B4 of [[task-335-space-texture-layers]], replaces the 20 to 35 luma band of [[task-333-nebula-visible]]): cloud peak about 70 to 100 sRGB luma near an edge; bright cores may exceed the floor strip beside the edge, but the void mean stays below the floor mean so the floor still reads first; near-black share of the images about 20 to 25 percent, dense filaments, bright cores, dark dust lanes. Screen darkener (owner 2026-11-04, provisional, [[task-426-screen-radial-darkener]], replaces the miner-centred mask): black added to every pixel grows linearly in circles around the screen centre, from CenterDarkness 0 at the centre to CornerDarkness 0.85 at the corners (radius = centre-to-corner distance; pixel = colour x (1 - black)); it no longer depends on the miner position. Clear zone (owner 2026-11-04, provisional, [[task-427-darkener-clear-zone]]): no black inside ClearRadius 0.15 of the centre-to-corner distance, a soft fade from no black at ClearRadius to the full effect at FadeRadius 0.35, the circles unchanged beyond. Void pixels (world Z below -200) get at most 0.5 black. No planet yet (later request); no glow at the floor-void edge. Colours in [[color-code]].
- Nearer asteroids (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-334-near-asteroids]], replaces "no drifting debris"): the arena is an asteroid, and other asteroids float in the void around it: about 12 lit, flat-shaded faceted low-poly rocks, 200 to 1200 uu across, tops between Z -300 and -2500, over void only, 300 to 3000 uu outside the floor edge, all around the arena; slow self-rotation 1 to 5 deg/s, no drift; no collision, no shadow cast; the darkening mask applies. Colour in [[color-code]].
- The Rigs' side looks repaired and bolted together; corporate robots look clean.
- No textures, no UI in reference art; strong directional light with lifted shadows; floor with no pebbles or rubble so robots, pickups and blocks stand out.
- Key art: `vault/assets/keyart.jpg` (readability reference, not a fidelity promise).
- M1 is grey box: plain cubes colored by block type after [[color-code]]; final assets come later ([[sys-grid]]).
