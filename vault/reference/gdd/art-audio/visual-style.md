# Visual style

- Flat-shaded low-poly.
- Camera: steep, near-vertical perspective; the miner is always centered.
- Blocks are always low (no occlusion); the top face is the most visible face.
- Floor of cracked faceted plates, painted look, edges catching the light, no rubble (owner 2026-10-21, provisional, task-273; was "clean floor", [[art-style-guide]] A13); arena edge is a cliff into space with stars and a planet.
- Void background (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-332-space-background]], [[task-335-space-texture-layers]]): layered: procedural stars and generated tileable cloud textures at depths, slow drift, never a single pasted image (replaces "procedural"). Unlit planes under the arena at real depths, parallax from the perspective camera: procedural stars Z -12000 (opaque, black base, slow twinkle), deep colour-wash texture Z -8000 (additive), cloud-wisp texture Z -5000 (additive, replaces the procedural nebula), sparse procedural dust Z -2000 (additive). Textures generated by the owner in ChatGPT from the art agent's prompts, square, tileable, repeated large; motion in the materials only (slow UV drift, no rotation); no Niagara dust. Darker than the floor beside it; the clouds are clearly visible, peak about 20 to 35 sRGB luma near an edge, above the far floor (about 10) but below the floor strip beside the edge (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-333-nebula-visible]]); stars stay the brightest points; the darkening mask applies to it like the rest of the screen. No planet yet (later request); no glow at the floor-void edge. Colours in [[color-code]].
- Nearer asteroids (owner 2026-10-27, provisional, [[task-334-near-asteroids]], replaces "no drifting debris"): the arena is an asteroid, and other asteroids float in the void around it: about 12 lit, flat-shaded faceted low-poly rocks, 200 to 1200 uu across, tops between Z -300 and -2500, over void only, 300 to 3000 uu outside the floor edge, all around the arena; slow self-rotation 1 to 5 deg/s, no drift; no collision, no shadow cast; the darkening mask applies. Colour in [[color-code]].
- The Rigs' side looks repaired and bolted together; corporate robots look clean.
- No textures, no UI in reference art; strong directional light with lifted shadows; floor with no pebbles or rubble so robots, pickups and blocks stand out.
- Key art: `vault/assets/keyart.jpg` (readability reference, not a fidelity promise).
- M1 is grey box: plain cubes colored by block type after [[color-code]]; final assets come later ([[sys-grid]]).
