# Floor texture: ChatGPT prompts (task-273-01)

Need: one square tileable painted floor texture, large cracked faceted plates, thin dark cracks, lighter bevelled plate edges, medium-dark violet-magenta ([[art-style-guide]] A13, locked floor phrase in [[art-prompts]]). Own design; the third-party frames were visual reference only and are never named or copied. No image was generated by the agent.

## Prompt chain
- Step 01, verbatim file `Art/Source/floor-texture/prompts/01-floor-texture.txt`. Core: "flat square 1:1 texture swatch ... straight from above (orthographic) ... about 6 to 10 large polygonal plates, some split by thinner secondary cracks ... fine thin dark crack lines ... plate edges slightly lighter ... MEDIUM-dark violet-magenta, plates #5A2A5C to #7A3A78, edges #9A58A0, cracks #2A1030 ... plates cross the borders ... no frame, no vignette ... flat uniform illumination, no baked light ... no pebbles, no rubble".
- Step 02, file `.../02-floor-texture-correction.txt`: A (light gradient, too dark or saturated) and B (border or seam), each "Keep this exact image: same ..." changing one thing, previous image attached.
- The tone is deliberately medium: the in-game albedo is darkened afterwards (far floor reads about RGB (20, 6, 20) after lighting); a near-black generation loses cracks and edges.

## Generation route
ChatGPT image generation, by the owner. Save the pick as `Art/Source/floor-texture/owner-<date>-floor-texture.png` (or the extension he gives).

## Seam method
`Tools/art/make_tileable.js --in <image> --out <tileable> --preview <3x3> --size 2048` ([[art-prompts-rock-top-texture]] describes the half-shift cross-fade), or the 3d agent's Blender step. Keep the raw file.

## Review checklist
- Straight top-down, no perspective; no baked light, shadow or gradient.
- No rubble, pebbles, crystals, objects, text.
- Crack lines and lighter plate edges visible; hue inside the stated hex range.
- No close resemblance to the references.
- 3 x 3 preview: no seam line, no strong repeating motif.

## Files
Owner image, 2026-10-21, "première version" (the owner may iterate): `Art/Source/floor-texture/owner-2026-10-21-floor-texture.webp` (1254 px, kept untouched) and lossless `.png` of it. Saturated magenta-violet, lighter than asked.
Baked by `Tools/art/blender/floor_texture_maps.py` into the same folder (2048 px): `T_Floor_Plates_D_asis.png`, `T_Floor_Plates_D.png` (material albedo), `T_Floor_Plates_N.png`, `T_Floor_Plates_H.png` (16-bit), `..._D_tiled3x3.png`, `..._N_tiled3x3.png` (1536).

## Bake method and numbers (levels of 255)
- Seam: source wrap step 3.42 (x) and 3.52 (y) against inner 1.21 and 1.36, above 1.5 x, so a correction: the border mismatch is split over 16 px each side (cosine ramp), no content shift; after 0.01 and 0.01. Then Lanczos-3 resample to 2048 with wrapped taps, no sharpening.
- D_asis: mean RGB (123, 52, 113), mean luminance 71.6, p1 25.0, p99 104.0.
- D: linear space, saturation x0.8, per-channel gain to mean RGB (95, 45, 95); luminance 59.3, p1 20.5, p99 86.2. Wrap vs inner: 0.13 vs 0.65 (x), 0.12 vs 0.73 (y).
- Height: luminance low-pass (sigma 20, faint), cracks as grooves (blur-8 minus luminance, depth 0.38), rims raised (0.12); mean 0.49.
- Normal: DirectX (green down, Unreal default), wrapped central differences, bake strength 12 (height in 0..1 per pixel step). Mean bytes (127.5, 127.6, 253.8), length 0.993 to 1.006, wrap vs inner 0.57 vs 1.34 (x), 0.50 vs 1.49 (y).

## Material values
Roughness constant 0.8, no map. Brightness (far floor 9-12/255, mid 39/255) is tuned in the material; NormalStrength starts at 1.
