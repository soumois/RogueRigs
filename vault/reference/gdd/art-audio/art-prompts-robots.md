# Corporate robots — prompt chain

Result: `vault/assets/enemies-lineup.jpg`. Enemy specs: [[enemies-index]].

## 1. Sheet
```
Same art style as the previous images. Character sheet on a plain light grey background, three-quarter top view, four security robots of the same mining corporation, all clean industrial yellow-orange panels, black hazard stripes, a corporate logo plate and glowing red eyes, rigid mechanical parts only:
1) fast melee chaser robot, light and agile;
2) kamikaze demolition drone with a visible glowing red-orange explosive core, clearly readable as "about to explode";
3) ranged robot firing red laser bolts;
4) heavy armored version of the melee robot, twice the size.
Flat-shaded low-poly, visible facets, no text.
```

## 2. Recolor and built-in cannon
```
Keep the exact same four robot shapes, details, poses and style. Only change colors and one weapon:
1) melee chaser: mostly bright industrial yellow, the lightest of the four;
2) kamikaze drone: dark gunmetal body with only small yellow parts, the glowing red-orange explosive core stays the main visual;
3) ranged robot: dark gunmetal body with thin yellow trims only. Replace the handheld rifle: its right forearm is a built-in laser cannon with a red emitter at the tip, integrated into the arm armor, not a held weapon;
4) heavy robot: yellow with wide black hazard stripes across its armor plates.
All keep the red eyes and the corporate logo plate. Flat-shaded low-poly, plain light grey background, no text.
```

## 3. Final colors and targeting laser
```
Keep the exact same four robot shapes, poses, details and style. All four keep the red eyes and a small yellow corporate logo plate. Only change colors:
1) melee chaser: bright industrial yellow, as now;
2) kamikaze drone: dark gunmetal, as now, with its glowing orange core;
3) ranged robot: acid green armor plates with dark gunmetal joints, keep the built-in arm laser cannon; add a thin red targeting laser beam projecting from the cannon;
4) heavy robot: steel blue armor plates with yellow and black hazard stripes.
Flat-shaded low-poly, plain light grey background, no text.
```

## 4. Arm cannon fix (ranged robot only)
```
Keep this exact image: same four robots, colors, poses, lighting and style. Only change the green ranged robot's right arm: from the elbow down there is no hand and no fingers. The whole forearm is a single chunky laser cannon barrel that starts at the elbow joint, with green armor plates and dark gunmetal parts, ending in the red emitter and its targeting laser beam. Flat-shaded low-poly, no text.
```
