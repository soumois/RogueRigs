# Art style guide

Derived from the four approved reference images and their prompts ([[art-prompts]]). Colors live in [[color-code]]; brightness and color-meaning rules live in [[readability-rules]]; do not restate them here.

## Shape language
- Chunky, blocky volumes with large flat facets; facets stay visible, no textures, no smoothing.
- Rigid mechanical parts only: plates, pistons, bolts, big gripping hands. No cloth, no organic forms.
- Every enemy role reads by silhouette alone: chaser = lanky biped, arms forward; kamikaze = round body with a glowing core and four limbs; shooter = bulky, one arm is a built-in cannon, thin antenna; heavy = chaser silhouette at twice the size, hunched.
- The drone is about the size of the miner's head and shoulders; the turret sits low and wide on a tripod base.

## Two factions, one contrast
- Corporate robots: clean panels, one big red eye in a boxy head, a small yellow logo plate, hazard stripes on heavy armor, no wear.
- The Rigs: patched, mismatched white and teal plates with rust chips, bolted-on pouches and a backpack, rivets and welded repairs; amber visor on the miner.
- Anything on the player side never uses enemy colors; anything corporate is never rusty.

## Effects
- Player fire: white-gold bolts, bright muzzle flash, small impact sparks on the hit target.
- Enemy fire and targeting: thin red lines.
- Explosions: orange core with debris; the kamikaze core must read as "about to explode".
- Nobody fires over a block: shooters and targets are positioned with a clear line.

## Scene composition
- Steep near-vertical camera, miner centered, strong directional light with lifted shadows.
- Clean flat floor with subtle large facets and no pebbles; the arena edge is a cliff into space with stars and a planet.

## Forbidden
- Textures, UI or text inside reference art, small scattered rubble on the floor, grey rock walls as arena borders.
- Handheld weapons on the corporate shooter (its cannon is part of the arm).

## Delivery formats
- Character or robot sheet: plain light grey background, three-quarter top view.
- 3D source: front, side and back T-pose on plain white, no weapon, no effects, even lighting, no shadows; front and back are used ([[asset-pipeline]]).
