# Ore deposit formations: owner prompts (task-234-01)

Owner 2026-10-19: a 2 x 2 deposit is one entity, so it is drawn as ONE crystal formation, 3 variants per type ([[ore-blocks]], [[heal-ore]]). Look reference: image C, `Art/Source/rock-look/20261017-131704-rock-look-2.png` (validated). Prompts are for the owner's ChatGPT image generation, pasted as is; nothing was generated by the crew. Images are for Blender modelling, so each is one isolated formation. Variants differ by structure, not colour. Colours: [[color-code]].

## Framing rules (inside every prompt)
- One formation, centred, three-quarter top-down view (about 55 degrees), square footprint of 2 x 2 cells on a thin flat lavender-grey stone pad; plain light grey background, no floor, no shadows, no text, no UI.
- Low-poly faceted, large flat facets, no smoothing, soft inner glow; silhouette readable at small size; crystals brighter than the stone.
- Duskite: glowing violet. Heal: glowing red only, no orange, no pink, no yellow.
