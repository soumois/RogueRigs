# Ore deposit formations: owner prompts (task-234-01)

Owner 2026-10-19: a 2 x 2 deposit is one entity, so it is drawn as ONE crystal formation, 3 variants per type ([[ore-blocks]], [[heal-ore]]). Look reference: image C, `Art/Source/rock-look/20261017-131704-rock-look-2.png` (validated). Prompts are for the owner's ChatGPT image generation, pasted as is; nothing was generated by the crew. Images are for Blender modelling, so each is one isolated formation. Variants differ by structure, not colour. Colours: [[color-code]].

## Framing rules (inside every prompt)
- One formation, centred, three-quarter top-down view (about 55 degrees), square footprint of 2 x 2 cells on a thin flat lavender-grey stone pad; plain light grey background, no floor, no shadows, no text, no UI.
- Low-poly faceted, large flat facets, no smoothing, soft inner glow; silhouette readable at small size; crystals brighter than the stone.
- Duskite: glowing violet. Heal: glowing red only, no orange, no pink, no yellow.

### Self-review (version 2)
- Distinctness vs the Duskite sheet: Duskite = crystal is the whole mass, 1.5 to 2 times taller than the pad is wide, grey pad nearly bare, violet. Version 2 = rock is the mass, red under a third of pad width, pad merged with rock; silhouettes are domed mound, flat slab, dished mound, none spire, ridge or crown. At far camera the heal reads as a red-spotted grey blob, Duskite as a violet spike. Colour alone is not relied on.
- Style: same low-poly facets, three-quarter 55 degrees, light grey background, 2 x 2 square pad as the Duskite prompts; [[art-style-guide]] A1, A12, A13, A14, A16 as in version 1 pass.
- [[color-code]]: red only, orange, pink, yellow, violet excluded; glow kept; stone lavender-grey. [[readability-rules]]: red clearly brighter than stone; low profile keeps it readable in the top-down view.
- Gaps to check on return: ChatGPT may still grow tall crystals, then correct with "Keep this exact image: same ..., but crystals no taller than a third of the pad width". Shard height in E2 may be ignored. Red could drift to pink in glow. Nothing generated yet.

## Self-review
- A1 pass: large flat facets, no smoothing requested. A12 pass: steep three-quarter view, flat light grey backdrop. A13/A14 pass: no pebbles, text or UI. A16 pass: plain light grey, three-quarter top view.
