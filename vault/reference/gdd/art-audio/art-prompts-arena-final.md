## 1. Full scene
```
Same art style as all previous images. Flat-shaded low-poly 3D game screenshot, steep top-down camera (almost vertical, slight perspective), 16:9.
Setting: a flat plateau on an asteroid. The arena edge is a sharp faceted cliff dropping into dark space, with a starfield and part of a large planet visible in the void. No grey rock walls anywhere.
Floor: clean, flat, slightly dark, subtle large facets, no pebbles.
Terrain: low knee-high blocks of light grey rock with a violet tint and visible cracks (destructible), some with glowing violet crystals (ore).
Player side, in white, teal and rust salvaged gear: the miner in the center firing white-gold laser bolts; the small floating drone next to a block, cutting it with a thin white-gold beam; a deployed scrap auto-turret firing white-gold bolts.
Enemies, all with red eyes and a small yellow logo plate: several bright yellow melee robots charging; one dark gunmetal kamikaze drone with a pulsing orange core; two acid green ranged robots with arm cannons, one projecting a thin red targeting laser toward the player, red laser bolts in flight; one large steel blue heavy robot with yellow and black hazard stripes.
Also: a red explosive barrel, a few cyan glowing XP cubes on the floor.
Strong directional light with lifted shadows so everything stays readable. No UI, no text.
```
