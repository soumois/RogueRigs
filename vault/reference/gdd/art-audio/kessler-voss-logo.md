# Kessler-Voss logo

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-11 among four variations of one idea (task-102).

- Look: a yellow bolted plate with a charcoal faceted asteroid carrying a "KV" monogram, then "KESSLER-VOSS" in large letters and "MINING CONSORTIUM" below. Flat colours: company yellow, charcoal, white.
- File: `Art/Source/kessler-voss-logo/20261011-201828-kessler-voss-logo-1.png` (variation 1), its prompt `prompts/01-plate-full-name.txt` and run log. Other variations were never saved to the repo: 2 plate with the short name, 3 round "KV" badge (best for small sizes), 4 emblem only; on 2026-10-17 none of them was found on the owner's disk. Variation 4 (emblem only) was regenerated on 2026-10-17 from variation 1 with `prompts/04-emblem-only.txt`: `20261017-kessler-voss-emblem-4.webp` (white background) and its cut-out `20261017-kessler-voss-emblem-4-cutout.png` (RGBA); used on the shield chaser shield on a #1A1F25 rounded plate ([[art-prompts-shield]]).
- Legibility: the name stays legible only at large sizes. "KESSLER-VOSS" alone survives about 64 px; "MINING CONSORTIUM" is for large signs and the lid sticker. For tiny logo plates use the round badge or the emblem.
- Colours: company yellow is allowed; never orange (reserved for explosions), acid green or steel blue (enemy-only).
- Text on logos and signs is allowed ([[art-style-guide]] A14 as amended). Slogans come only from [[company-voice]]; never invent a name.
- Used: the sticker on the lid of the [[barrel-asset]]. Intended: robot logo plates and advertising panels.
