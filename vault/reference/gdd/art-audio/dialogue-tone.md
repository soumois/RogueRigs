# Dialogue tone

Each rule has an id that the critic cites ([[agent-style-guide]]). Owner update 2026-10-09.

- T1 Combat barks: 3 to 8 words.
- T2 Jokes target the company or the robots (owner 2026-10-13). Self-mockery is fine. Vesna teases Rook's plans and bravado, never the player's skill or performance.
- T3 No real film line word for word. A real line twisted into the Rigs' world is allowed when clearly modified: the wink is the joke (owner 2026-10-20, was: no paraphrase either); the critic judges whether it is a twist or still the same line. Rook names a film only from [[fictional-films]], with original lines.
- T4 Rook: a cocky, odds-dismissing smuggler crossed with a tired, sarcastic blue-collar worker; punchlines and Rigs jargon under stress ([[crew-characters]]).
- T5 Vesna: calm, dry, short; deflates Rook; may name only a past job listed in [[crew-characters]].
- T6 Kessler-Voss: propaganda toward the public, cold bureaucracy toward threats, never jokes ([[company-voice]]).
- T7 Every on-screen text is in English ([[readability-rules]]).
- T8 Never reuse a vault example line verbatim in generated content.
- T9 Company text never orders the player to stop or do something that contradicts the objective; it states what the company does (owner 2026-10-10, found in a playtest: the shuttle countdown line "Cease all extraction" was misread).

- Examples (reference only, T8): "Send the invoice to corporate." · "They noticed. Great. Love it when they notice." · "Of course it's on the other side of the rock."
- Barks are nice-to-have; milestone M3. Pulled in (owner 2026-11-10, provisional, [[task-497-barks-in-game]]): rare, one line at a time; only on the key events horde_incoming, console_dropped, console_hacked, bonus_chest_opened, shuttle_landed; global cooldown 30 s; never while a Kessler-Voss alert line is shown (the alert keeps priority: a bark is dropped, an alert replaces a bark at once); a random line of the trigger, never the same line twice in a row; text only in the subtitle widget with the speaker name; Vesna's lines are her voice through the drone (L2).
