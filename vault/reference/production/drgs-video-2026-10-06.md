## Mining
- Contact mining: walking into the rock mass digs a cone in front of the dwarf; the hole grows in width and in depth together (several slabs deep at once), the mass crumbling into small shards (0:10–0:13, 1:39–1:46).
- Speed: the dig front advances about one body width every ~0.7 s; a hollow about 1.5 body widths deep and 2 wide forms in about 2 s (0:10.2 → 0:12.4).
- Rogue Rigs differs: our arc reaches up to 4 blocks in width, one row deep.

## Ore drip
- Gold nuggets are embedded throughout the rock mass, not in a separate vein block; each slab that breaks pops its nugget, so ore comes during the whole dig.
- Timing (1:39.5–1:46): the "+N Or" toast rises +1, +2, +3, +5, +6, +8, +9, +10 … +19 in about 6.5 s, about 3 chunks per second while digging; each chunk arcs out, lands, then is pulled in (counter rises about 0.5–1 s after the pop).
- Rogue Rigs differs: one chunk per broken ore block, ore blocks grouped in 3 × 3 veins that break together.
