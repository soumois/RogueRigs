# DRGS reference analysis

Source: owner run videos in `Reference/DRGS/` (not committed; level starts at 0:35). Times below are level time.

- **Pacing:** 3:58 level. Level-ups at 0:28, 0:52, 1:22, 1:37, ~1:55, 2:16–2:22 (three in a row), 3:07, ~3:28, 3:40; heal +12 (7.5 %) per level. Zone hold 1:06 (5 s, ground ring gauge), horde banner 1:34 (horde 4 s later), elite 2:55 (own health bar), capsule with a 30 s countdown and floor chevrons.
- **Arrival and oppression:** 10 s calm, 2–3 isolated enemies, first group of 8–10 from one side at 22 s, then a rising flow from several sides; lull before each announced horde; horde = a front of 50–80 from one side, big ones in front, a carpet of small ones behind; flyers come in snaking files. Enemies emerge from darkness outside a light halo of about 4–5 body widths; a red full-screen vignette marks each hit.
- **Packs:** one type per pack; packs advance as a crescent facing the player, sliding against each other; the player is pushed to walls and digs to escape.
- **Contact damage:** no attack animation; 8 per contact, about one hit per 0.5–1 s despite 5–6 enemies touching (i-frames or cooldown).
- **Mining:** rock is a continuous mass of irregular slabs 2–3 times smaller than our 200 uu cells; standing against a wall mines it, several slabs at a time in an arc, about 5 times faster than our drone; weapons keep firing.
- **Pickups:** a kill bursts many small cyan cubes in short arcs; they rest; inside the pickup radius they accelerate into a stream toward the player; ore pops one chunk per pick hit, which arcs, lands and is pulled in about 1 s later; heal crystals pop red chunks and a green ring shows the heal.
- **Upgrades:** five tiers grey, green, blue, purple, red (ours ends in yellow); values roughly twice ours at the time (fire rate +10/+15/+25/+40 %); piercing as rare/epic stat; weapon upgrades raise the weapon level; paid unlimited reroll.
- **Weapons (wiki):** Deepcore GK2 35 dmg, 4.62/s, bursts of 3, clip 30, reload 4 s, pierce 4; Boomstick 7 pellets × 60, 2/s, clip 2, reload 4 s, pierce 3, knockback; LMG Gun Platform 20 dmg, 3.33/s, pierce 4, 2 turrets of 10 s, placed automatically when standing still. DRGS weapons aim themselves; Rogue Rigs keeps manual aim (P2).
- **Enemies:** green tanks walk in groups and push the mass (owner: the model for our heavy); orange bloated ones explode on proximity after swelling (our kamikaze); red swarmers form the carpet (owner: a shield chaser).
