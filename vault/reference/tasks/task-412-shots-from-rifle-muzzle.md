## Questions
Decided 2026-10-31 (owner answer in request): Q1, Q2, Q3, Q7 defaults; Q4 default widened to a principle (every other player weapon shoots from the shown weapon Muzzle); Q5 no capture; Q6 default. Values provisional, owner 2026-10-31.
- Q1 Capsule bottom: Default: 20 uu above the floor, its top at the Muzzle height or above (sized at each shot from the real Muzzle height, so it always covers the 0-100 uu rock band from 20 up and every robot); radius 14 kept. Or: another clearance (say a value; it must stay between 0 and 100).
- Q2 Direction: Default: flat, parallel to the aim as today and as the ground chevrons, starting from the Muzzle. Or: from the Muzzle toward the mouse cursor point (with the stick, parallel).
- Q3 Check between the miner and the Muzzle: Default: kept, one sweep of the same capsule from the miner centre to the Muzzle before the shot: a rock stops it, a robot is hit. Reason: against a wall the Muzzle is inside the rock and the engine pushes a bullet born inside a rock out, sometimes on the far side; a robot pressed between the miner and the Muzzle would be skipped. Or: no check (simpler, those two cases can happen).
- Q4 Shotgun: Default: its 7 pellets leave from the burst rifle Muzzle with the same capsule (your task-400 words: "les autres tirs partiront du même endroit"); without a held rifle, today's point. Or: the shotgun keeps today's point and sphere. Turret bullets and robot shots unchanged. Decided: only the first player weapon (burst rifle) is shown; every other player weapon shoots from its Muzzle.
- Q5 Visual check: Default: a -game capture subtask: C1 bullets appear at the barrel tip, C2 miner against a rock, no bullet beyond it, C3 a robot close to the miner is hit. Or: no capture, you check in PIE. Decided: no capture.
- Q6 Muzzle placement: Default: no change to BP_BurstRifle; your uncommitted edit (rifle scale 1.5 in the log) stays yours, you check the Muzzle sits on the barrel tip and move it by hand if not. Or: a bp subtask places the Muzzle on the SM_BurstRifle tip.
- Q7 Projectile Blueprints: the collision root changes from sphere to capsule in the C++ base of all shots, so BP_Projectile, BP_ShotgunPellet, BP_TurretBullet and BP_ShooterProjectile must be recompiled, checked (ShotMesh attached, no collision) and saved. Default: one bp subtask does the four (no edit, only check and save), an exception to the one-asset-per-bp-subtask rule. Or: four bp subtasks, one per Blueprint.
