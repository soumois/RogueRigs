# Burst rifle turnaround prompt and reference images for Meshy

- request: Owner, 2026-10-30, verbatim: "Cette discussion a pour but de travailler l'arme dans les mains du personnage (l'arme de base). Il me faudrait le prompts et les images de ref pour cette arme. L'image servira ensuite à meshy." Meaning (Planner reading, to confirm in Q1): "l'arme de base" = the default weapon, the burst rifle (working name). Deliverables of this task: (1) the image prompt of a turnaround sheet of the rifle for 3D, (2) the reference images to attach to it. The resulting image goes to Meshy later; the Meshy run, the import, the hand socket and the attach to the miner are not part of this task (a later task once the owner validates the image). Draft prompt proposed to the owner (paragraph markers P1 to P5 are not part of the prompt): P1: Turnaround sheet for 3D modeling of the default weapon of a stylized space miner, a salvaged two-handed burst rifle, same weapon design as the rifle held by the miner in the attached images: four views on one sheet, left side view (exact profile), right side view (exact profile), front view (looking into the muzzle) and back view (looking at the stock), all four at the same scale, same height and same framing, no perspective distortion, subject centred with a margin, the rifle alone, no hands, no character, no stand, no ground. P2: Style: stylized flat-shaded low-poly game asset, chunky blocky volumes with large flat facets, facets clearly visible, no smoothing, no textures, clean hard-surface look. Rigid parts only: plates, bolts, rails, no cloth, no organic forms. Slightly oversized, chunky proportions so the rifle reads from a steep top-down camera. P3: Design: a compact two-handed rifle assembled from salvaged parts: boxy receiver, short shoulder stock, pistol grip and front grip that big armored gloves can hold, a top rail with a small sight, a magazine under the receiver, a short barrel ending in a slotted muzzle brake. A clearly readable front (muzzle) and back (stock). P4: Colors and wear: patched, mismatched white and teal plates with rust chips, bolts, rivets and welded repairs, dark gunmetal grip, barrel and muzzle, a repaired and improvised salvaged look, same palette as the miner. No red, no yellow, no green, no blue, no violet, no glow. P5: Background and light: plain white background, even neutral lighting, no shadows, no muzzle flash, no projectiles, no effects, no text, no UI, no logo. Owner answer 2026-10-30 (relayed by the main session, verbatim): "ok" = the plan and the defaults of Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5 accepted.
