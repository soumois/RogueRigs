# Verification levels per global task

- request: Owner, verbatim: "Ok, comme est-ce que tu proposes de mettre en place ce dernier point ?" (the point: proportion verification to the size of the change, and group small steps), then, on the proposal of the main session: "Oui, planifie". Accepted proposal, task A: the Planner sets a verification level per global task, shown in the plan the owner validates; the owner can change it with a word. Starting values: light = a setting on an existing system, no new asset, no new system, 3-4 acceptance points, 1 after launch if a before image exists, 1 sheet, indicative executor budget 40 turns, 0.6 M units; standard = new behaviour or new asset, verification as today, 90 turns, 1.5 M units; exploratory = look to find, several loops likely, capture loop with a fixed number of rounds, 150 turns, 3 M units. The owner rule of 2026-10-16 stays (a visible change ends with a -game capture compared with the look before); only its size changes. Shorter acceptance at the light level, example on 474: scale read back in the Blueprint rather than height measured in Blender at 1 %; rifle in hand and laser at the muzzle seen on one frame rather than the full list of attachments (keep this check on purpose: the scale change really did affect the rifle attach); one line gameplay unchanged through the capsule read-back and targeted tests rather than camera, drone, light and animations checked one by one; 1 launch and 1 sheet with the owner reference as before rather than 2 launches, 3 angles, 4 sheets. Executor rules: group reads and commands in one message when possible, as the planner already does; on reaching the budget it reviews what remains and writes the cause in result, without stopping (exceeding is not a failure). Why, cost analysis of tasks 460-480 without cancelled 466 (agent-usage.js): 20 tasks = 37.52 M units, executors 92 %, cache reads 69 % of executor cost. task-474 (miner 10 % smaller) cost 1.49 M, 91 turns, peak context 171k, for one Blueprint value and one C++ line; 475 and 476 are similar (1.43 M each). Nothing brakes an executor today. Known limits: the expected gain is modest (about 2.4 M of 37.5 M, 6 %, on 474, 475, 476 at about 0.8 M each, an estimate); the risk is a defect reaching owner validation; budget figures are starting values. A later comparison on about ten tasks, reference 474, 475, 476 (1.33 to 1.41 M executor), is NOT a task unless the owner asks. 
- goal: Every new global task carries a verification level the owner saw and can change, and the Executor applies the verification size and the indicative budget of that level.
- acceptance: A1 task-create.js takes a level per global (light, standard or exploratory), writes it on the global node, and refuses a new global without a level or with another value; existing nodes without a level stay valid for lint and the closing scripts, and task-confirm.js keeps the level. A2 The planning rules say the Planner sets the level from the criteria of the request, shows it per global in the plan it reports, takes standard when in doubt, and a one-word owner answer (leger, standard, exploratoire) changes it. A3 The table of levels (criteria, verification size, budget: 40 turns 0.6 M, 90 turns 1.5 M, 150 turns 3 M, provisional owner 2026-11-06) is written once in the crew nodes, the others link to it, and it says the figures are starting values and the budget is indicative. A4 The light level says how to shorten acceptance, with the 474 example from request including the deliberate rifle and laser check; a light task without a before image adds one before launch (2 launches, 1 sheet). A5 orchestrator-lessons keeps the owner rule of 2026-10-16 and says only its size varies with the level. A6 A change of screen or widget layout is never light: the four resolutions of umg-layout stay the default. A7 The exploratory level fixes the capture loop at 3 rounds unless the plan sets another number; at the limit the Executor shows the owner what it has and waits for him. A8 agent-executor says: group reads and commands in one message when possible; follow its consumption through the executor_usage line that closing a subtask writes on the global node, and its own turn count between closures; on reaching the budget review what remains, write the cause in result, do not stop; exceeding is not a failure. Its token_ceiling is no longer open and points to the budgets. A9 The known limits of request (modest estimated gain, risk of a defect reaching owner validation) are recorded with the rule. A10 Lint clean, committed by path, no game code, Blueprint or asset changed, no capture (nothing visible changes).

## Questions
Asked 2026-11-06, each with a default; open until the owner answers.
- Q1 Niveaux et budgets : on garde les valeurs proposees (leger 40 tours et 0,6 M ; standard 90 tours et 1,5 M ; exploratoire 150 tours et 3 M), indicatives et non bloquantes, le Planner prenant standard en cas de doute ? Elles remplacent le token_ceiling de l'Executor, aujourd'hui open. Default: oui, ces valeurs, provisoires.
- Q2 Une tache legere sans image "avant" : la regle du 2026-10-16 demande une comparaison avec l'aspect d'avant, que fait-on ? Default: un lancement "avant" en plus, soit 2 lancements et 1 planche.
- Q3 Un changement de mise en page d'ecran ou de widget peut-il etre leger ? La regle actuelle verifie les quatre resolutions sauf si tu choisis moins. Default: jamais leger, les quatre resolutions restent ; tu peux en demander moins dans le plan.
- Q4 Combien de tours pour la boucle de capture au niveau exploratoire ? La proposition demande un nombre fixe sans le donner. Default: 3 tours, sauf si le plan en fixe un autre ; a la limite l'Executor te montre ou il en est et attend ta reponse.
- Q5 Cette tache doit modifier orchestration-protocol et execution-protocol, des regles que l'Executor n'a normalement pas le droit de changer. Tu l'y autorises ? Default: oui, pour les taches 482 et 483 seulement.
