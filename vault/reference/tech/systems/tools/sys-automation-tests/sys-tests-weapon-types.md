# Automation tests — weapon types

Part of [[sys-automation-tests]] (harness, command and gotchas live there).

- `RogueRigs.Turret.*` (task-024-03; `BP_Miner` weapons stopped + native `ARRTurretLauncher` via `AddWeapon`, frozen native chasers; [[sys-turret]]): `LauncherStats` (not in the default loadout; CSV values, 2/2 charges, 12 s recharge (task-034-01), 10 s lifetime, 1500 range; nothing placed after 2 s idle); `ChargesAndRecharge` (button path `PlaceTurret`: 2 → 1 → 0, third press null; one charge at a time, 0.1 s leftover carried; broadcasts (1, 2, 12) … (2, 2, 0); 11.9 s none, 12.1 s one, 24.1 s two; `ReloadSpeed` 2 → 6 s; `MagazineSize` 3 starts a recharge); `Placement` (miner (32, 42) aiming +X: floor cell 200 uu ahead, centered, owner launcher, instigator miner; miner (35, 40) aiming −X at rock (34, 40): cell (35, 40), never behind the rock); `StatCopy` (damage 12 and pierce 6 copied, constants, `ARRTurretBullet`, life span 10, no magazine, no collision, not a miner weapon; a later launcher change reaches only the next turret); `LineOfSight` (turret (35, 40): robot (33, 40) behind rock ignored for one at (39, 40); none after that one goes; locked once the rock is destroyed); `FireRangeLifetime` (no grid, launcher alone: nearest robot locked, 1600 uu out of range, lock kept when a nearer robot appears, 4 bullets in the first second, `MaxHits` 4, 6 damage per hit, retarget on loss, alive at 9.9 s, gone at 10 s).
- `RogueRigs.Shotgun.*` (task-024-02 pellet model; `BP_Miner` with its M1 loadout, weapons stopped, native `ARRChaser` frozen (actor and movement ticks off, so a pending launch can be read) placed on pellet rays from the shot origin; [[sys-weapons]]): `StatsAndLoadout` (weapons `BP_BurstRifle` then the shotgun (`BP_Shotgun_C`); CSV row Shotgun, pierce 3, display name "Fusil à pompe"; accumulator alone: shots at 0 and 0.5 s, magazine 0 and 2.5 s reload, then 3.0, 3.5, 6.0 s, 7 pellets each); `PelletFan` (7 `ARRShotgunPellet` at the shot origin, flat, yaw offsets −15 to +15 by 5, `MaxHits` 3, knockback 250, owner the shotgun, instigator the miner, lifetime range / speed, gone after it; `Pierce` 5 reaches the next shot's pellets); `Pierce` (robots on the center ray at 460, 515, 570, 625 uu: first three −6, fourth untouched); `RockStopsPellets` (miner (36, 40) aiming −X: a robot 120 uu ahead takes all 7 pellets, robot on (33, 40) behind rock (34, 40) neither hit nor pushed, pellets gone after 5 frames, rock intact); `CritPerPellet` (one robot per ray at 560 uu, crit 0.5, `FMath::RandInit(20261005)`: one hit each, 6 or 9 by its own roll, pattern CCCCnCn); `Knockback` (at 560 uu: 250 along its pellet at resistance 0, 187.5 at 0.25, none at 1, none for a robot killed by its pellet; a robot at 250 uu takes the 0 and ±5° pellets: launch (748.1, 0) = their sum, −18; +12.4 uu in 3 frames once its movement ticks); `CardsOnlyWhenEquipped` (200 seeded offers: rifle only 0 shotgun cards / 148 rifle; rifle + shotgun 89 / 107; a targeted shotgun damage card raises only the shotgun).
- Weapons loop (task-024-06, `RRWeaponsIntegrationTests.cpp`; final Blueprints, miner on open row (32, 42) aiming +X, weapons stopped; `StartGameMode` and `BP_RRPlayerController` where a pause or input is needed): `Integration.StartLoadoutRifleOnly` (`BP_Miner` holds `BP_BurstRifle` alone, no guaranteed class, `MaxWeapons` 4, catalogue rifle / `BP_Shotgun` / `BP_TurretLauncher` with descriptions, shotgun and launcher acquirable); `WeaponCardEquipsShotgun` (catalogue forced to the shotgun entry, chance 1, seed 20261005, `WBP_HUD` bound: level 2 screen holds one weapon card in slot 0, "Nouvelle arme" / "Fusil à pompe", `CardDesc` shown and `CardValue` hidden on it only; on a stat card `CardDesc` is visible with the flavor line (text after the first newline of `DescriptionText`) when the loader gives one, else collapsed (task-087-01); `HUD->ChooseUpgrade` equips `BP_Shotgun` firing `BP_ShotgunPellet`, 7 pellets, screen closed, world resumed; a shotgun-targeted stat card appeared at level 4 (Dégâts critiques +15 %, 1.5 → 1.65, rifle untouched)); `ShotgunPelletsPierceRobots` (`BP_Shotgun` added, crit 0, 4 frozen `BP_Chaser` on the centre pellet ray 55 uu apart from 460 uu: one shot of 7 `BP_ShotgunPellet`, the first 3 robots hit once (30 → 24), the 4th untouched (CSV pierce 3)); `PlaceTurretInputKillsRobot` (`BP_TurretLauncher` added, the controller's place-turret Started handler run once via `FPlayerControllerAccess`: one `BP_Turret` on (33, 42) firing `BP_TurretBullet`, one charge spent; a frozen `BP_Chaser` on (37, 42) locked and killed in 1.467 s, 5 hits); `LevelUpDiscEndsHidden` (levels 2 and 3 at once, level 4 at frame 30, each screen picked at once (frames 49–51): the disc is shown with its `DiscStep_Tick` timer pending when the screens pause, the timer resumes after them; it ends hidden with no timer left at `DiscStep` 5, the kept behaviour of [[bp-miner]]; no warning since task-025-01).
