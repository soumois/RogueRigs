# Automation tests — weapon types

Part of [[sys-automation-tests]] (harness, command and gotchas live there).

- `RogueRigs.Turret.*` (task-024-03; `BP_Miner` weapons stopped + native `ARRTurretLauncher` via `AddWeapon`, frozen native chasers; [[sys-turret]]): `LauncherStats` (not in the default loadout; values of `WeaponStats.csv` row `TurretLauncher`: charges from `MagazineSize`, recharge from `ReloadTime`, `TurretLifetime`, `Range`; nothing placed after 2 s idle); `ChargesAndRecharge` (button path `PlaceTurret`: 2 → 1 → 0, third press null; one charge at a time, 0.1 s leftover carried; broadcasts (1, max, `ReloadTime`) … (max, max, 0); just before `ReloadTime` none, just after one, after twice that two; `ReloadSpeed` 2 → `ReloadTime` / 2; `MagazineSize` 3 starts a recharge); `Placement` (miner (32, 42) aiming +X: floor cell 200 uu ahead, centered, owner launcher, instigator miner; miner (35, 40) aiming −X at rock (34, 40): cell (35, 40), never behind the rock); `StatCopy` (`Damage` and `Pierce` of the launcher copied (set by the test, then read back), constants, `ARRTurretBullet`, life span = row `TurretLifetime`, no magazine, no collision, not a miner weapon; a later launcher change reaches only the next turret); `LineOfSight` (turret (35, 40): robot (33, 40) behind rock ignored for one at (39, 40); none after that one goes; locked once the rock is destroyed); `HeadYaw` (task-350-01; native turret, Muzzle moved 40 uu ahead and 30 up: HeadPivot under Root and Muzzle under HeadPivot, targeting origin = centre, actor yaw unchanged, head world yaw within 1 degree of the target, shot origin at the canon tip, aim direction, firing hits, retarget to a robot behind-right); `FireRangeLifetime` (no grid, launcher alone: nearest robot locked, 1600 uu out of range, lock kept when a nearer robot appears, a range of bullets (from `FireRate`) with a minimum hit count in the first second, `MaxHits` = row `Pierce`, row `Damage` per hit, retarget on loss, alive just before `TurretLifetime`, gone at it).
- `RogueRigs.Shotgun.*` (task-024-02 pellet model; `BP_Miner` with its M1 loadout, weapons stopped, native `ARRChaser` frozen (actor and movement ticks off, so a pending launch can be read) placed on pellet rays from the shot origin; [[sys-weapons]]): `StatsAndLoadout` (weapons `BP_BurstRifle` then the shotgun (`BP_Shotgun_C`); row `Shotgun` of `WeaponStats.csv`, `Pierce`, display name "Fusil à pompe"; accumulator alone: shots at 0 and 0.5 s, magazine 0 and 2.5 s reload, then times derived from `FireRate`, `MagazineSize` and `ReloadTime`, `PelletCount` pellets each); `PelletFan` (`PelletCount` `ARRShotgunPellet` at the shot origin, flat, yaw offsets −15 to +15 by 5, `MaxHits` = `Pierce`, knockback = `KnockbackForce`, owner the shotgun, instigator the miner, lifetime range / speed, gone after it; `Pierce` 5 reaches the next shot's pellets); `Pierce` (robots on the center ray at 460, 515, 570, 625 uu: the first `Pierce` hit for `Damage`, the next untouched); `RockStopsPellets` (miner (36, 40) aiming −X: a robot 120 uu ahead takes all pellets, robot on (33, 40) behind rock (34, 40) neither hit nor pushed, pellets gone after 5 frames, rock intact); `CritPerPellet` (one robot per ray at 560 uu, crit 0.5, `FMath::RandInit(20261005)`: one hit each, `Damage` or `Damage` × `CritDamage` by its own roll, pattern CCCCnCn); `Knockback` (at 560 uu: `KnockbackForce` along its pellet at resistance 0, 0.75 of it at 0.25, none at 1, none for a robot killed by its pellet; a robot at 250 uu takes the 0 and ±5° pellets: launch = the sum of their pellets, −3 × `Damage`; +12.4 uu in 3 frames once its movement ticks); `CardsOnlyWhenEquipped` (200 seeded offers: rifle only 0 shotgun cards / 148 rifle; rifle + shotgun 89 / 107; a targeted shotgun damage card raises only the shotgun).
- Weapons loop (task-024-06, `RRWeaponsIntegrationTests.cpp`; final Blueprints, miner on open row (32, 42) aiming +X, weapons stopped; `StartGameMode` and `BP_RRPlayerController` where a pause or input is needed): `Integration.StartLoadoutRifleOnly` (`BP_Miner` holds `BP_BurstRifle` alone, no guaranteed class, `MaxWeapons` 4, catalogue rifle / `BP_Shotgun` / `BP_TurretLauncher` with descriptions, shotgun and launcher acquirable); `WeaponCardEquipsShotgun` (catalogue forced to the shotgun entry, chance 1, seed 20261005, `WBP_HUD` bound: level 2 screen holds one weapon card in slot 0, "Nouvelle arme" / "Fusil à pompe", `CardValue` hidden on it only, its weapon variant shows `WeaponHeader` = `DisplayName`, `WeaponName` = `TargetText`, `WeaponDesc` = `DescriptionText`, and `CardDesc` holds `LoreText`, visible only when not empty (task-169-05, layout of [[wbp-upgrade-choice]]); on a stat card `CardDesc` is visible with the flavor line (text after the first newline of `DescriptionText`) when the loader gives one, else collapsed (task-087-01); `HUD->ChooseUpgrade` equips `BP_Shotgun` firing `BP_ShotgunPellet`, `PelletCount` pellets, screen closed, world resumed; a shotgun-targeted stat card appeared at level 4 (Dégâts critiques +15 %, 1.5 → 1.65, rifle untouched)); `ShotgunPelletsPierceRobots` (`BP_Shotgun` added, crit 0, 4 frozen `BP_Chaser` on the centre pellet ray 55 uu apart from 460 uu: one shot of `PelletCount` `BP_ShotgunPellet`, the first `Pierce` robots hit once (minus the row `Damage`), the next untouched); `PlaceTurretInputKillsRobot` (`BP_TurretLauncher` added, the controller's place-turret Started handler run once via `FPlayerControllerAccess`: one `BP_Turret` on (33, 42) firing `BP_TurretBullet`, one charge spent; a frozen `BP_Chaser` on (37, 42) locked and killed within a time derived from `FireRate` and `Damage`).

- Rule (task-420): the expected numbers of these tests are the `WeaponStats.csv` rows `TurretLauncher` and `Shotgun`, read through `RRTestStatsRows.h`; the turret first-second check is a range plus a minimum hit count.
