# Automation tests — weapons

Part of [[sys-automation-tests]] (harness, command and gotchas live there).

- `RogueRigs.Weapons.BurstTiming`: `AdvanceTime` alone at 1/60 s; values of `WeaponStats.csv` row `BurstRifle`; exact shot times and firing frame (±1) derived from the row `FireRate`, `BurstCount` and `BurstDuty` (gaps and pause computed), then again at a raised `FireRate` set by the test ([[sys-auto-fire]] example).
- `Weapons.MagazineReload`: shot 30 at 7.36 s, 1.5 s reload with `Ammo` 0, `Ammo` 30 at its end, shot 31 at 8.86 s.
- `Weapons.ProjectileStopsOnRock`: shot from (42, 38) stops on rock (43, 38) at frame 2, no damage to the rock or a still chaser behind it.
- `Weapons.ProjectileHitFilter` (task-020-02): projectiles damage terrain by default; a shot whose `HitActorClass` is set to the miner class passes through a still chaser (no hit, health unchanged); one set to `ARRChaser` hits it (minus the row `Damage`).
- `Weapons.Pierce*` (task-024-01; `BP_BurstRifle` without miner, crit 0, native `ARRChaser` frozen (actor and movement ticks off), health 30): `PierceFourRobots` (`WeaponStats.csv` row `BurstRifle` `Pierce`; from (32, 42) +X, robots on (34–38, 42): one bullet hits `Pierce` robots for the row `Damage` each and is destroyed before the next (frame 20); `Pierce` + 1 → the next bullet hits all of them; `Pierce` 0 → 1 hit); `PierceStopsOnRock` (from (36, 40) −X: robots 60 uu west of (36, 40) and on (35, 40) hit, bullet stops on rock (34, 40) at frame 8, robot on (33, 40) untouched); `PierceNoDoubleHit` (first robot hit at frame 8, then teleported 250 uu ahead of the bullet: passed through without a second hit, next robot hit, 2 distinct hits); `PierceUpgrade` (`BP_Miner` `KeepRifleOnly` on (42, 41): no grey or green row, blue / purple / yellow +1 / +2 / +3 global and targeted, "Perforation", "+1"; 400 seeded offers: 40 piercing cards, none below blue, always 3 cards; blue all-weapons then yellow on the rifle: 4 → 5 → 8, base 4, next bullet `MaxHits` 8).
- Pierce 1 forced (single-target checks, task-024-01): `Weapons.ProjectileDamagesStillChaser`, `Weapons.ProjectileHitFilter`, `Enemies.ChaserDiesAndDropsXP` (each bullet must end on its target).
- `Weapons.ProjectileDamagesStillChaser` (was `ProjectileDamagesDummy`): still chaser on (44, 41), shot from (42, 41): `CritChance` 0 → −`Damage`, not critical; `CritChance` 1 → −`Damage` × `CritDamage`, critical (row `BurstRifle`).
- `Weapons.MinerStartingWeapon`: miner with `WeaponAttachSocketName` forced to none, walking on (42, 38): `BP_BurstRifle` on the capsule at `WeaponAttachOffset`, pointing along the aim, no actor collision, acquired, firing; capsule Overlap on `Projectile`; one shot spawns `ShotHeight` (50) above the floor, below `BlockHeight`, capsule radius + `ShotForwardOffset` (54 uu) ahead, and stops on rock (43, 38) at frame 1.
- `Weapons.MinerMovesWhileFiring`: miner (weapons on the capsule) pushed +X by `AddMovementInput` for 1 s from (30, 43), once with the weapon stopped, once firing: 425 uu both (kinematic 420), no lateral drift, no projectile or non-floor capsule hit; firing must keep >= 90 % of the distance.
- `Weapons.MinerHitsStillChaser` (was `MinerHitsFloorDummy`, target changed in task-031-01): final `BP_Miner` (no socket override) on (38, 43) with its starting `BP_BurstRifle`, aim set by `ApplyStickAimInput((-1, 0))` (= world −Y) toward a still chaser resting on the floor of (38, 40) (capsule 124 uu tall, actor Z = floor + 62); `Health` must drop below `MaxHealth` within 2 s (measured task-031-01: first hit at frame 11, 0.183 s, 3 shots fired, 990 / 1000).
- Two-weapon loadout adjustments (task-015-01): `Weapons.MinerStartingWeapon`, `MinerMovesWhileFiring`, `MinerHitsStillChaser` expect 2 weapons and use `FindRifle` (the stopped pass also stops the shotgun); armed `Integration.*` loops stop the shotgun (rifle kill times and hit counts were measured alone); `Upgrades.*` spawn the miner with `KeepRifleOnly` (seeded counts measured with one weapon). `Integration.LevelUpUpgradeLoop` passes unchanged with the stopped shotgun present (seed 6 still rerolls to Dégâts, Toutes les armes, +5 %).
- `RogueRigs.Upgrades.WeaponCards*` (task-024-04; `RRWeaponCardTests.cpp`; `BP_Miner` rifle only on (42, 38), weapons stopped, catalogue forced to rifle class + native `ARRShotgun` + native `ARRTurretLauncher`, chance 0.3, one per offer, seed 20261005): `WeaponCardsDefaults` (native miner: no starting or guaranteed weapon, catalogue shotgun + launcher with descriptions, `MaxWeapons` 4, chance 0.3, 1 per offer; card "Nouvelle arme", "Fusil à pompe" / "Lance-tourelles", catalogue description, empty value); `WeaponCardsOfferRule` (3000 offers: always 3 cards, at most one weapon card, never the owned rifle; 66.4 % with one (expected 65.7 ±3), slots 30 / 21 / 14.7 % ±3, shotgun 1038 vs launcher 954; `MaxWeapons` 1: none in 300 offers and a weapon card refused; every catalogue weapon owned: none); `WeaponCardsPickEquips` (global damage then rifle fire rate picked; the shotgun card equips a shotgun: 2 weapons, offer ended, recorded with the weapon, damage = row `Shotgun` `Damage` × (1 + global), fire rate = its row `FireRate`; same card refused once owned; 300 offers: shotgun-targeted stat cards (105), shotgun never offered, launcher offered (199); launcher card: `FindTurretLauncher`, 3 weapons, global damage replayed); `WeaponCardsReroll` (268 rerolled offers with a weapon card: never the same weapon or stat choice, at most one weapon card; the other weapon offered 173 times).
- New-weapon card adjustments (task-024-04): `BP_Miner` starts with the rifle only, so tests measured with the rifle + shotgun loadout spawn it with `M1TestLoadout`: `Cheats.*`, `LevelUp.*`, `Integration.*` (`SetUp`), `Integration.Heavy*`, `Shotgun.*` (equipped cases), `Weapons.MinerStartingWeapon`, `MinerMovesWhileFiring`, `MinerHitsStillChaser`; other tests keep the Blueprint loadout.

- Rule (task-420): the expectations above that name a damage, fire rate, pierce or crit are the row values of `WeaponStats.csv` read at run time, with the robot health raised so a target survives the shots.
