# Spawner wave director

- responsibility: when and what the spawner spawns while `DirectorConfig.bEnabled` (default): lone robots, waves of packs from distinct sides, announced hordes ([[enemies-index]] wave director, [[drgs-analysis]]).
- main_classes: `Source/RogueRigs/Enemies/RRWaveDirector.h/.cpp`: data structs `FRRWaveDirectorConfig` {`bEnabled`, `HordeTrigger` (`ERRHordeTrigger` RunTime | Alert), `Phases`, `PackTypes`, `Hordes`}, `FRRDirectorPhase` {`Name`, `StartTime`, `SingleInterval` (first one at phase start), `SingleClass`, `bWaveOnStart`, `bKeepWaveTimer` (task-028-01: entering the phase keeps the previous wave timer, for a phase that only changes the pack mix), `WaveIntervalStart` → `WaveIntervalEnd` over `WaveIntervalRampSeconds`, `SidesMin`–`SidesMax` packs per wave, `PackWeights` {`PackType` name, `Weight`}}, `FRRDirectorPackType` {`Name`, `Members`}, `FRRDirectorPackMember` {`RobotClass`, `CountMin`, `CountMax`, `bFront`, `bBehind` (task-030-01: never leads the pack)}, `FRRDirectorHorde` {`TriggerTime` (front time), `TriggerAlert` (lull start), `LullSeconds`, `WarningSeconds`, `SpreadSeconds`, `SizeMin`, `SizeMax`, `Members`, `FillClass`}; world-free scheduler `FRRWaveDirector` (`Advance` → `FRRDirectorAction` orders). Executed by `ARREnemySpawner` ([[sys-spawner]]).
- defaults (`FRRWaveDirectorConfig::MakeDefault`, provisional, owner 2026-10-06 after the first play test, task-030-01): no calm phase; Isolated 1 s, a chaser every 2.35 s (1, 3.35 … 12.75 s; task-045-01, owner: first level too hard, about 15 % fewer robots; was 2 s); FirstPack 15 s, one `FirstChasers` pack (chaser 6–8); Flow 21 s, waves of 1–2 packs, interval 7.1 → 4.1 s over 210 s (task-045-01; was 6 → 3.5 s), weights Chasers 96 / Heavies 4 (task-045-01; 92 / 8 since task-030-02, 85 / 15 before); FlowShields 60 s and FlowWide 90 s continue Flow (keep the wave timer, interval and ramp computed from Flow, no start wave): FlowShields weights ShieldChasers 96 / Heavies 4; FlowWide 2–3 packs, weights WideShieldChasers 96 / WideHeavies 4. Pack types (task-034-04, owner fourth test, provisional; no shooter-only pack): until 90 s packs of 5–10 and no shooter: Chasers = chaser 5–8 + kamikaze 0–1; Heavies = heavy 2–3 front + chaser 4–7; ShieldChasers = chaser 4–5 + shield chaser 1–2 + kamikaze 0–1 ([[sys-enemies-shield-chaser]]). From 90 s: WideShieldChasers = chaser 6–12 + shield chaser 2–4 + kamikaze 0–1 + shooter 0–1 behind; kamikaze 0–1 at equal odds (task-044-01: about one pack out of two, at most 1; was 1–2); WideHeavies = heavy 2–3 front + chaser 8–12 + shooter 0–1 behind. "One pack out of two" = the 0–1 shooter draw (uniform, so half the packs on average, not a strict alternation). Drawn over 210 s (3 seeds, before the cap): 30–35 shooters, 3.2–3.6 % of the robots outside hordes, none before 90 s. Before task-034-04: chaser 8–14 + kamikaze 2–3, every flow pack with shooter 1–2 behind (about 9 % of the robots), cap 6. Shooter counter (task-030-02, provisional): `ShooterMaxAlive` 4 (6 before task-034-04), `ShooterCapReplacement` chaser: the shooters a pack draws beyond the cap become chasers in the same back-half places (shooters fire from afar and pile up while the miner backs away). Hordes: fronts at 95 s and 175 s (alert mode: 0.4 and 0.75), lull 5 s, banner 4 s before, 34–51 robots over 2 s (task-045-01, set in `MakeDefault`; struct default still 40–60): heavy 2–3 front (task-045-01, was 3–5), kamikaze 1–2 (task-044-01, was 3–5), chasers fill. Heavy alive cap: only the `WeightedEnemyClasses` entry `MaxAlive` on [[bp-enemy-spawner]] (5; task-045-01 asks 3, a Blueprint change). Before task-030-01: calm 0–10 s, chaser every 3 s, first pack 8–10 at 22 s, 2–3 packs from 28 s, 6 → 3 s over 180 s, a Shooters pack (shooter 3–5) at weight 25.
- overtime (task-032-01, [[difficulty]], provisional): `FRRWaveDirectorConfig::Overtime` (`FRRDirectorOvertime` {`bEnabled` true, `StartTime` 210 s of spawning = level time 3:30, `HordeInterval` 30, `StepSeconds` 60, `WaveIntervalMultiplier` 0.5, `MinWaveInterval` 0 = no floor, `Horde` = a copy of the timed horde, so 34–51 with 2–3 heavies since task-045-01; overtime timing unchanged}). From `StartTime`: one `OvertimeStarted` order (`OnOvertimeStarted` delegate); an overtime horde whose lull starts every `HordeInterval` (lulls 210, 240, 270 … s, fronts 5 s later: 215, 245, 275 …), indexed after `Hordes` (`FindHorde`, `IsOvertimeHorde`, `GetHordeFrontTime`; `GetHordeCount` and the HUD alert-bar icons cover the listed hordes only); the wave interval × 0.5 per overtime step begun (interpretation: halved already at 3:30, quartered at 4:30, an eighth at 5:30): measured before task-045-01 (6 → 3.5 s ramp): 3.762 s at 209 s, 1.869 at 211 s, 0.875 at 271 s, 0.438 at 331 s; waves per minute 12 (150–210 s), 25 (210–270 s), 52 (270–330 s). An alert-trigger horde never reached does not block overtime hordes. Pure `IsInOvertime`, `GetCurrentWaveInterval`.
- behavior: lull, banner and front silence lone robots and waves; the wave timer restarts after the front. Robot classes resolve to the `WeightedEnemyClasses` entry with the same nearest native class (the Blueprints of [[bp-enemy-spawner]]), else the class itself; entry `MaxAlive` caps apply (excess dropped; horde fill keeps the size). Pack: one candidate scan per wave; shooter room = `ShooterMaxAlive` − shooters alive (`GetDirectorShooterRoom`), shared by the wave, excess shooters replaced without an extra random draw; a distinct side per pack (still: random side with room, else largest, random anchor; running: sector drawn first, side and anchor = a random cell of that sector, [[sys-spawner]] run heading), the cells nearest the anchor, front members on the cells nearest the view, the other members shuffled, then `bBehind` members inserted at random in the back half of them (middle or back of the pack, never the front row). Horde: one side (running: the side of a random cell of the drawn sector), cells sorted by distance to the view (the front row spans the side), front members first, spawned in order over `SpreadSeconds`; a cell that entered the margin meanwhile is skipped. Packs and hordes clamp to `MaxAlive` (150).
- entry_points: delegates `OnDirectorPhaseChanged(PhaseIndex, PhaseName)`, `OnHordeLull(HordeIndex)`, `OnHordeIncoming(HordeIndex, SecondsUntil)` (banner), `OnHordeStarted(HordeIndex, Side)`, `OnHordeFinished(HordeIndex, Spawned)`, `OnOvertimeStarted()`; pure `IsInOvertime`, `GetCurrentWaveInterval`, `IsDirectorEnabled`, `GetDirectorPhase`, `GetHordeTrigger`, `GetHordeCount`, `GetHordeTriggerTime(i)`, `GetHordeTriggerAlert(i)`, `GetHordeState(i)` (`ERRHordeState`), `GetActiveHorde`, `GetSecondsUntilHordeFront`; C++ `GetDirectorConfig`, `GetDirector`, `ResolveRobotClass`, `TrySpawnDirectorWave` (→ `FRRSpawnedPack` {type, side, robots, cells}), `TrySpawnEnemyOfClass`.
- data: `ARREnemySpawner::DirectorConfig` (also `ShooterMaxAlive`, `ShooterCapReplacement`) (EditAnywhere, category Spawner|Director); tune on [[bp-enemy-spawner]] or the C++ defaults.
- depends_on: [[sys-spawner]], [[sys-objectives]] (alert, Alert trigger only).
- implements: [[enemies-index]] wave director; horde events for [[level-flow]] M1.
- gotchas: run time counts only enabled spawning. Alert mode at 0.4 coincides with the console drop. A saturated cap shrinks hordes. Heavy capsules nudge neighbours off compact pack cells. Tests: `RogueRigs.Spawner.Director*` ([[sys-automation-tests]]).
