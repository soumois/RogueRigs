# Spawner wave director

- responsibility: when and what the spawner spawns while `DirectorConfig.bEnabled` (default): lone robots, waves of packs from distinct sides, announced hordes ([[enemies-index]] wave director, [[drgs-analysis]]).
- main_classes: `Source/RogueRigs/Enemies/RRWaveDirector.h/.cpp`: data structs `FRRWaveDirectorConfig` {`bEnabled`, `HordeTrigger` (`ERRHordeTrigger` RunTime | Alert), `Phases`, `PackTypes`, `Hordes`}, `FRRDirectorPhase` {`Name`, `StartTime`, `SingleInterval` (first one at phase start), `SingleClass`, `bWaveOnStart`, `bKeepWaveTimer` (task-028-01: entering the phase keeps the previous wave timer, for a phase that only changes the pack mix), `WaveIntervalStart` → `WaveIntervalEnd` over `WaveIntervalRampSeconds`, `SidesMin`–`SidesMax` packs per wave, `PackWeights` {`PackType` name, `Weight`}}, `FRRDirectorPackType` {`Name`, `Members`}, `FRRDirectorPackMember` {`RobotClass`, `CountMin`, `CountMax`, `bFront`, `bBehind` (task-030-01: never leads the pack)}, `FRRDirectorHorde` {`TriggerTime` (front time), `TriggerAlert` (lull start), `LullSeconds`, `WarningSeconds`, `SpreadSeconds`, `SizeMin`, `SizeMax`, `Members`, `FillClass`}; world-free scheduler `FRRWaveDirector` (`Advance` → `FRRDirectorAction` orders). Executed by `ARREnemySpawner` ([[sys-spawner]]).
- defaults (`FRRWaveDirectorConfig::MakeDefault`, provisional, owner 2026-10-06 after the first play test, task-030-01): no calm phase; Isolated 1 s, a chaser every 2 s (1, 3 … 13 s); FirstPack 15 s, one `FirstChasers` pack (chaser 6–8); Flow 21 s, waves of 1–2 packs, interval 6 → 3.5 s over 210 s, weights Chasers 85 / Heavies 15; FlowShields 60 s and FlowWide 90 s continue Flow (keep the wave timer, interval and ramp computed from Flow, no start wave): FlowShields weights ShieldChasers 85 / Heavies 15; FlowWide the same with 2–3 packs. Pack types (no shooter-only pack): Chasers = chaser 8–14 + kamikaze 2–3 + shooter 1–2 behind; ShieldChasers = chaser 6–12 + shield chaser 2–4 + kamikaze 2–3 + shooter 1–2 behind ([[sys-enemies-shield-chaser]]); Heavies = heavy 2–3 front + chaser 8–12 + shooter 1–2 behind. Every flow pack carries shooters; measured shooter share over 210 s (3 seeds): 9.2–9.8 % of the robots outside hordes, 8.5–8.9 % with them. Hordes unchanged: fronts at 95 s and 175 s (alert mode: 0.4 and 0.75), lull 5 s, banner 4 s before, 40–60 robots over 2 s: heavy 3–5 front, kamikaze 3–5, chasers fill. Before task-030-01: calm 0–10 s, chaser every 3 s, first pack 8–10 at 22 s, 2–3 packs from 28 s, 6 → 3 s over 180 s, a Shooters pack (shooter 3–5) at weight 25.
- behavior: lull, banner and front silence lone robots and waves; the wave timer restarts after the front. Robot classes resolve to the `WeightedEnemyClasses` entry with the same nearest native class (the Blueprints of [[bp-enemy-spawner]]), else the class itself; entry `MaxAlive` caps apply (excess dropped; horde fill keeps the size). Pack: one candidate scan per wave, a distinct side per pack (random side with room, else largest), the cells nearest a random anchor, front members on the cells nearest the view, the other members shuffled, then `bBehind` members inserted at random in the back half of them (middle or back of the pack, never the front row). Horde: one side, cells sorted by distance to the view (the front row spans the side), front members first, spawned in order over `SpreadSeconds`; a cell that entered the margin meanwhile is skipped. Packs and hordes clamp to `MaxAlive` (150).
- entry_points: delegates `OnDirectorPhaseChanged(PhaseIndex, PhaseName)`, `OnHordeLull(HordeIndex)`, `OnHordeIncoming(HordeIndex, SecondsUntil)` (banner), `OnHordeStarted(HordeIndex, Side)`, `OnHordeFinished(HordeIndex, Spawned)`; pure `IsDirectorEnabled`, `GetDirectorPhase`, `GetHordeTrigger`, `GetHordeCount`, `GetHordeTriggerTime(i)`, `GetHordeTriggerAlert(i)`, `GetHordeState(i)` (`ERRHordeState`), `GetActiveHorde`, `GetSecondsUntilHordeFront`; C++ `GetDirectorConfig`, `GetDirector`, `ResolveRobotClass`, `TrySpawnDirectorWave` (→ `FRRSpawnedPack` {type, side, robots, cells}), `TrySpawnEnemyOfClass`.
- data: `ARREnemySpawner::DirectorConfig` (EditAnywhere, category Spawner|Director); tune on [[bp-enemy-spawner]] or the C++ defaults.
- depends_on: [[sys-spawner]], [[sys-objectives]] (alert, Alert trigger only).
- implements: [[enemies-index]] wave director; horde events for [[level-flow]] M1.
- gotchas: run time counts only enabled spawning. Alert mode at 0.4 coincides with the console drop. A saturated cap shrinks hordes. Heavy capsules nudge neighbours off compact pack cells. Tests: `RogueRigs.Spawner.Director*` ([[sys-automation-tests]]).
