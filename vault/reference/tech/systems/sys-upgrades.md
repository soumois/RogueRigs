# Upgrades

- responsibility: build the card offer and apply the picked upgrade. The level-up flow (pause, heal, chained screens) drives it: [[sys-level-up]].
- main_classes (`Source/RogueRigs/Upgrades/`): `URRUpgradeComponent` (default subobject `Upgrades` of `ARRMiner`, `GetUpgrades()`); `FRRUpgradeCard` (`CardType`, `WeaponClass`, `Stat`, `Tier`, `Target`, `TargetWeapon`, `Value`, `Operation`, UI texts `DisplayName`, `TargetText`, `ValueText`, `DescriptionText`) and enums `ERRUpgradeCardType` (`StatUpgrade`, `NewWeapon`), `ERRUpgradeStat`, `ERRUpgradeRarity` (rank = value), `ERRUpgradeTarget` (`Player`, `AllWeapons`, `Weapon`), `ERRUpgradeOperation` in `RRUpgradeCard.h`; rows `FRRUpgradeValueRow`, `FRRUpgradeRarityRow`.

## Rules
- Offer: each card picks a stat among offerable stats by its CSV `DrawWeight` (task-032-01, [[upgrades]]: Damage, FireRate, ReloadSpeed 3, every other stat 1; the lowest tier row of a stat gives its weight; 0 = never offered; measured first-card counts over 6000 offers: about 1045 for each weight-3 stat vs 350 for a weight-1 stat) (at least one row; weapon stats only while the miner owns a weapon), then a target uniformly among that stat's targets (all weapons or one weapon of `ARRMiner::GetWeapons()`: a new weapon is a target automatically), then a tier. A stat without a row at the drawn tier (piercing below blue) is dropped from this offer and the card is drawn again, so piercing cards are rarer and only blue, purple or yellow (implementation choice). Two cards never share stat and target. Stat and target odds: provisional implementation choice.
- New-weapon cards (task-032-01, [[upgrades]]; replaces the 30 % per slot roll of task-024-04): only on an offer for a level multiple of `WeaponCardLevelInterval` (5: levels 5, 10, 15…): the first slots become weapon cards, `GetNewWeaponCardCount(Level, Acquirable)` = min(`MaxNewWeaponCardsPerOffer` 2, acquirable weapons) (`ARRMiner::GetAcquirableWeapons`: not owned, fewer than `MaxWeapons` 4 owned, [[sys-player]]), weapons picked uniformly; other slots get stat cards; other levels and non-level offers (`OfferLevel` 0, e.g. a chest) hold none and draw nothing for it. The level comes from [[sys-level-up]]: `URRLevelUpComponent` queues the level of each owed choice (`PendingLevels`) and passes it to `BuildOfferWithOwnerLuck(CardCount, BonusLuck, OfferLevel)`; a reroll keeps the offer level. Card: `CardType` `NewWeapon`, `DisplayName` "New weapon", `TargetText` = CSV weapon name (`ARRWeapon::GetClassDisplayName`), `DescriptionText` = catalogue description, empty `ValueText`, `Tier` unused: the UI styles it by `CardType`, not by rarity. Picking calls `ARRMiner::AddWeapon` (acquisition replays the global cards); the picked card records the weapon in `TargetWeapon`; the new weapon becomes a target of later stat cards. Refused (false) when owned, at the limit or without a miner. A reroll never offers a weapon of the replaced offer. Measured: levels 1–30, 20 offers each: two weapon cards on every level-5k offer, none elsewhere; chained level-ups 2→6: weapon cards 0 0 0 2 0.
- Tier draw: [[rarities]] formula, k per row (all 0.1); luck <= −10 floors growth at 0.
- Application: one infinite runtime GE per (attribute, operation), SetByCaller `Data.UpgradeValue`: `AddBase` = value, `MultiplyAdditive` = 1 + value, so percentages add ([[upgrades]] stacking). Player stats on the miner ASC; `MaxHealth` also adds the value to `Health` (instant GE). Weapon cards on that weapon ASC; all-weapons cards on every owned weapon ASC, replayed by `ARRWeapon::Acquire` after the base values. Never removed.
- Reroll: once per offer; the new cards share no (stat, target) with the replaced ones (only exact duplicates excluded when the pool is too small).
- Display (English since task-032-01, [[readability-rules]]): stat names Damage, Fire rate, Reload speed, Crit chance, Crit damage, Magazine size, Max health, Move speed, Pickup radius, XP gain, Mining speed, Luck, Piercing; target "All weapons" or the weapon `DisplayName` (WeaponStats.csv: Burst rifle, Shotgun, Turret launcher); values "+12%", "+7.5%", "+20" (crit stats in %); piercing "+1". Catalogue descriptions (native `ARRMiner`): shotgun "A short-range spray of pellets that knocks robots back.", turret launcher "Places turrets that fire at nearby robots on their own." (`BP_Miner` overrides them, [[bp-miner]]).

- entry_points: `BuildOffer(CardCount, Luck, OfferLevel = 0)`, `BuildOfferWithOwnerLuck(CardCount = 3, BonusLuck, OfferLevel = 0)`, `GetNewWeaponCardCount`, `GetStatDrawWeight`, `GetWeaponCardLevelInterval` (chest bonus), `Reroll(OutCards)`, `CanReroll`, `HasOffer`, `GetCurrentOffer`, `ApplyUpgrade(Card)` (refuses unowned weapons; new-weapon cards equip; ends the offer; `OnUpgradeApplied`), `GetPickedUpgrades`, `ReplayGlobalUpgrades(Weapon)`, `GetUpgradeValue`, `GetTierWeights`, `DrawTier`, `MakeCard`, `MakeWeaponCard(Entry)` (static), `GetMaxNewWeaponCardsPerOffer`, `SetRandomSeed`.
- data: `Content/RogueRigs/Data/Tables/UpgradeValues.csv` (row `<Stat>_<Tier>`: `Stat`, `Tier`, `GlobalValue` in attribute units, `TargetedMultiplier` 1.5, `Operation`, `DrawWeight` (task-032-01); values from [[upgrades]], raised in task-028-01 (% stats 0.10 / 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.30 / 0.40, MaxHealth 20 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 80, CritChance 0.03 / 0.05 / 0.08 / 0.11 / 0.15, CritDamage 0.1 → 0.5 by 0.1, Luck unchanged); no `MagazineSize` rows in M1; `Pierce_Blue/Purple/Yellow` +1 / +2 / +3 `AddBase` with `TargetedMultiplier` 1 (whole robots; interpretation, the 1.5 rule would give fractions)); `UpgradeRarities.csv` (row = tier: `BaseWeight`, `LuckFactor`); `WeaponStats.csv` column `DisplayName`. Tunables on the component (EditDefaultsOnly, provisional): `WeaponCardLevelInterval` 5 (0 = never), `MaxNewWeaponCardsPerOffer` 2.
- depends_on: [[sys-gas-core]], [[sys-weapons]], [[sys-player]].
- implements: [[upgrades]], [[rarities]], [[player-stats]].
- gotchas: runtime GEs are never replicated (single-player). A missing SetByCaller returns 0 and logs an error ([[gas-api-notes]]). Effect cards are not built (M2); piercing is a stat (`ERRUpgradeStat::Pierce`, weapon attribute `Pierce`), not an effect.
