# Kamikaze (`ARRKamikaze`)

- responsibility: the charger that arms near the miner and explodes.
- main_classes: `ARRKamikaze` (Blueprintable, `RRKamikaze.h/.cpp`), `ERRKamikazeState` Chase, Armed, Dead. Components: `VisualRoot` (on the capsule) carrying `BodyMesh` and `CoreMesh` (no collision; meshes, eyes and logo plate set by the Blueprint).
- behavior: Chase (movement on, arrival arm range × `ArrivalRangeRatio` = 125 uu) → Armed when the target is within the arm range (`IsTargetWithin`): movement off, committed → after the fuse (`HasStateLasted`): queues one `ExplosionRowName` explosion ([[sys-explosions]], instigator and source = itself), adds `State.Dead` and dies without XP, so its own blast is refused and never counts as a kill. Killed (task-096-01, [[enemy-kamikaze]] owner 2026-10-10), rule on the damage path: `HandleDeath` reads `URRHealthAttributeSet::GetLethalEffectClass` ([[sys-damage]]). Lethal effect `URRGE_DamageExplosion` (chain, barrel, another kamikaze): instant, queues the same explosion and dies, XP as usual. Any other lethal effect (`URRGE_Damage` weapons and drone, `URRGE_DamageFlat` turrets, cheats) is the player side: kill fuse. `State.Dead` stays on (every damage GE refuses it, turrets skip it, cannot die twice), hit capsule `NoCollision` (shots pass through), not dying yet; it enters Armed exactly as on proximity (`ArmTime` = kill time; already Armed keeps its running fuse), `OnFlareStart` / `OnFlareStarted`. At the fuse end `Detonate` queues the explosion and runs the base death with the killer as instigator: the kill (run-flow `NotifyEnemyKilled`, alert bar) counts then; death shrink then XP as any robot. `IsDeathPending` (= `IsKillFuseRunning`) keeps its spawner slot and class cap until it exploded ([[sys-enemies-base]] `HoldsSpawnSlot`). The recycler never relocates it (`RelocateTo` refuses dead robots). Never exploded twice.
- visuals: `VisualRoot` `HoverHeight` above the capsule bottom with a sine bob; `CoreMesh` scale = BeginPlay scale × growth × (1 + `CorePulseAmplitude` × sin(phase)), phase at `CorePulseRate` (Armed: `ArmedPulseRate`), growth linear to `ArmedCoreScale` over the fuse while armed.
- flare (task-027-01, [[enemy-kamikaze]]): the Armed phase is the flare; duration = fuse (CSV `WindUp`, 0.65 s; 0.5 before task-034-01). Arming calls the Blueprint event `OnFlareStart(Duration)` and the native `OnFlareStarted(Kamikaze)`; leaving Armed calls `OnFlareEnd(bDetonatedByFuse)` (true at a proximity or kill fuse explosion, false when an explosion killed it during the flare; no Blueprint change for the kill fuse). `IsFlaring`, `GetFlareDuration`, `GetFlareProgress` (0 to 1 over the fuse; 1 after the fuse explosion; 0 before arming or when killed earlier) are BlueprintPure, for the core light ramp. A death by explosion stays instant, no flare. XP burst: 4 cubes ([[sys-pickups]]). Measured before task-034-01 (0.5 s fuse): flare start = arm time, explosion 0.500 s later, progress 0.967 on the last flare frame; the tests now expect 0.65 s (39 frames at 60 fps).
- animation (task-284-05, base clip writer, [[sys-enemies-base]]): the native `BodyMesh` loops the leg sway (clip 0, rows 0..15, material RunRate 0.35, cycle 1.524 s) from a random phase at BeginPlay and each pool reuse; no rest clip (`RestClipId` -1), so arming, the fuse and death leave it running. Static mesh until task-284-09.
- entry_points: `GetState`, `HasDetonated`, `IsKillFuseRunning` (BlueprintPure), `IsDeathPending`, `GetArmRange` (CSV `AttackRange`, 250 uu; 150 before task-034-01), `GetFuseTime` (CSV `WindUp`, 0.65 s), `GetArmTime`, `GetExplosionQueuedTime`, `GetCoreScaleFactor`.
- data: arm range and fuse in its CSV row (task-021-01, R8 of [[code-review-m1-deferred]]; 250 uu and 0.65 s since the owner's fourth test, task-034-01); `EditDefaultsOnly`: `StatsRowName` `Kamikaze`, `ArrivalRangeRatio` 0.5 (set in the constructor), `ExplosionRowName` `Kamikaze`, `HoverHeight` 40, `HoverBobAmplitude` 4, `HoverBobRate` 1.5 Hz, `CorePulseRate` 1.5 Hz, `ArmedPulseRate` 8 Hz, `CorePulseAmplitude` 0.15, `ArmedCoreScale` 1.8 (visual values are code defaults, not gdd values). Capsule 37.4 × 96.8 (1.1 × the miner).
- depends_on: [[sys-enemies-base]], [[sys-explosions]].
- implements: [[enemy-kamikaze]].
- pool: `ResetForNewLife` also clears the kill fuse (`bKilledByPlayer`, killer) and restores the hit capsule collision.
- gotchas: contact with the miner is at 71.4 uu, inside arrival and the arm range, so it always arms first. Once armed it stays put: `RogueRigs` kamikaze test checks a drift under 1 uu over the fuse (task-034-01; already true before). Measured (task-034-01 run): arms at 249.5 uu, explosion queued 0.650 s after arming, core peak 2.02, armed drift 0.00 uu (at 150 uu / 0.5 s: 149.5 uu, 0.500 s, 2.00).
