# Audio (sound events)

- responsibility: plays the sound of a named event from one data table, with the mix rules of [[audio-direction]] (task-511, commits db961979, a6285d70, f10a5ab6). Adding or swapping a sound is one table line; a new event is one line plus one call.
- main_classes: `Audio/RRAudioSettings.h/.cpp`: `URRAudioSettings` (config object) and `FRRSoundEventRow`. `Audio/RRAudioSubsystem.h/.cpp`: `URRAudioSubsystem` (GameInstance subsystem) and a Slate input pre-processor. `Audio/RRAudioDebugCommands.cpp` (not shipping).
- entry_points: `URRAudioSubsystem::PlayEvent(Context, Event, Variant)` (centred) and `PlayEventAt(Context, Event, Location, Variant, EdgeRadius)` (placed; `EdgeRadius` > 0 measures the fade from the edge of that circle). Lookup: `Event.Variant` row first, else its parent name (`Shot.Shotgun` falls back to `Shot`). No row or no sound: silent, logged once at Verbose.
- call_sites: `Shot` in `ARRWeapon::FireOnce` and `ARRShotgun::FireOnce` (one per volley) as `Shot.<StatsRowName>` (`ShotSoundEvent`); `Impact` on a robot hit in `ARRProjectile::PlayImpactSpark` (`ImpactSoundEvent`); `Impact.Wall` in `ARRProjectile::OnProjectileStop` when a shot stops on anything that is not a robot (blocks of every kind, map edge; `WallImpactSoundVariant`, task-513, commit 14c24a29); `EnemyDeath` in `ARREnemy::HandleDeath` (not a kamikaze's own fuse); `PlayerHit` in `ARRMiner::HandleDamageReceived`; `XPPickup` in `ARRPickup::Collect`; `OrePickup` in `ARRPickup::Collect` for an ore chunk (task-515; its row uses the XP pickup sound and settings for now, placeholder, owner 2026-11-13); `LevelUp` in `URRLevelUpComponent::HandleLevelUp`; `Explosion` in `URRExplosionSubsystem::Explode`, every resolved explosion (barrel, kamikaze, each chain link; `ExplosionSoundEvent`, task-516, commit d645a43f). Turret, turret bullet and robot bullet set None: silent (owner confirmation open).
- buttons: a real-time ticker plays `UIHover` when the enabled Slate `SButton` under the cursor (game window) changes; the pre-processor plays `UIClick` on a left press over one. Disabled = the button or a parent disabled. Every screen, paused ones included, no per-widget code; keyboard or gamepad activation plays nothing.
- data: `Config/DefaultGame.ini` `[/Script/RogueRigs.RRAudioSettings]`, one `+Events=(...)` line per event: `Event`, `Sound`, `Volume`, `PitchJitter` (0.05 = ±5 %), `MaxConcurrent` (0 = no cap; the oldest play stops), `bSpatial` (placed: spatialized, no engine attenuation), `bUI` (plays while paused), optional `FadeInner` / `FadeOuter` (uu). Values per [[audio-direction]].
- distance_fade: any row with `FadeOuter` > 0, on a play with a location (`PlayEventAt`): horizontal distance player pawn to event at the moment of the play, minus `EdgeRadius` and never below 0 (`URRAudioSubsystem::GetFadeDistance`); `Volume` up to `FadeInner`, linear to silence at `FadeOuter`, no play at or beyond it (checked before the cap, so it never stops an audible play). No pawn: no fade. `URRAudioSubsystem::GetDistanceFade(Row, Distance)` gives the factor. Faded rows: `Impact.Wall` (`Volume` 0.5) and `EnemyDeath` (`Volume` 1.0, task-514), both `FadeInner` 200, `FadeOuter` 1075 = left/right screen edge at 1920x1080 measured with `rr.Audio.ScreenEdge` (top 1237, bottom 579, top corners 2090 uu); provisional, owner 2026-11-13. `Explosion` (`Volume` 1.0) passes the `Radius` of its `ExplosionStats.csv` row as `EdgeRadius`, with `FadeInner` 0 and `FadeOuter` 1075: full level inside the blast or at its edge, silence 1075 uu beyond the edge (Q6 of [[task-516-audio-explosion-edge-distance]]). Assets `/Game/RogueRigs/Audio/SFX/SW_*`; sources, originals, prompts and licences in `Art/Source/audio/` and `Art/Source/audio/sound_manifest.json`. Every kept sound is a placeholder, "à remplacer avant publication" (owner 2026-11-13; `status` field of each manifest entry); `SW_Explosion` is the owner's ElevenLabs file (task-516, commit 9a5bee3c). Every play uses the Effects class of `URROptionsSettings` ([[sys-options]]), so the Effects and Master sliders set it.
- add_a_sound: put the prepared WAV in `Art/Source/audio/`, add its entry to `sound_manifest.json`, import with `Tools/unreal/import_sounds.py` (PythonScript commandlet, editor closed, usage in the script header), then add or edit the table line. New event: also one `PlayEvent`/`PlayEventAt` call at its moment.
- debug: `rr.Audio.Play <Event> [DX DY [EdgeRadius]]` (centred, or at the player + offset in uu: placed rows and fade, from the edge of an `EdgeRadius` circle as an explosion); `rr.Audio.ScreenEdge` (distances player to screen edges and corners on the player's plane, through the camera); `rr.Audio.Stats [reset]` (per row plays, cap, peak, cap stops; device volumes of Effects and Music); `rr.Debug.Click [down|up]` (a press and release in one frame do not activate a UMG button: send them apart); per-play lines with `log LogRRAudio Verbose` (volume, distance and fade; `Skip` lines beyond `FadeOuter`).
- depends_on: [[sys-options]] (sound classes and volume mix), [[sys-button-hover]] (same buttons, separate mechanism).
- implements: [[audio-direction]]
- gotchas: on a paused world, finished UI plays may still count as active (UI rows have no cap). No audio under `-nullrhi`. A missing `Impact.Wall` row falls back to `Impact` (full level, no fade). `FadeOuter` follows the camera: re-measure after a camera or resolution change.
