# Explosions

- responsibility: the single path of every explosion and chain reaction: terrain, character damage, visual, event.
- main_classes (`Source/RogueRigs/Explosions/`): `URRExplosionSubsystem` (`UTickableWorldSubsystem`, `Config = Game`), `FRRExplosionStatsRow` (CSV row), `ARRExplosionFX` (abstract visual actor). Damage effect `URRGE_DamageExplosion` in `Source/RogueRigs/Damage/` ([[sys-damage]]).
- entry_points: `QueueExplosion(Center, RowName, Instigator, SourceObject)` (BlueprintCallable; false when the row is missing or the source is spent); `QueueExplosionWithStats(Center, RowName, Stats, Instigator, SourceObject)` (C++); `GetExplosionStats(RowName, OutStats)`; `IsSourceSpent(SourceObject)`; `GetPendingCount()`; `GetChainDelay()` / `SetChainDelay()`; `SetGrid()` (default: first `ARRArenaGrid`). Dynamic delegate `OnExplosion(Center, RowName)` (BlueprintAssignable), broadcast after each explosion is resolved.

## Rules (as built)
- Never synchronous: `QueueExplosion` only appends to a FIFO queue; `Tick` resolves at most one explosion per tick, when world time ≥ previous explosion time + `ChainDelay` (tolerance 1e-4 s). A queue that was idle resolves on the next subsystem tick. At 60 fps the 0.08 s delay gives a 5-frame gap (0.083 s, measured).
- One explosion per source: the source object is recorded when queued (`FObjectKey`, kept for the world's life); queuing it again, before or after it explodes, is refused. A null source is never deduplicated.
- Resolution order: grid `QueueDestroyCellsInRadius(Center, Radius, TerrainSource)` (every block whose cell center is within the 2D radius, whatever its type or health; since task-121-01 barrels at once and the other blocks nearest first over the next frames under the grid's 2 ms per-frame budget, [[sys-grid-barrels]]); then characters; then FX; then the camera shake; then `OnExplosion`, all in the explosion frame.
- Characters: every actor whose ASC (`UAbilitySystemGlobals::GetAbilitySystemComponentFromActor`) owns a `URRHealthAttributeSet` and whose actor location is within the 2D radius gets one `URRGE_DamageExplosion` (deduplicated by ASC; targets collected before any damage). Value (task-294-01): an `ARRMiner` takes the row's `MinerDamage`, any other character (robots) its `Damage` (before: `Damage` for every character); an explosion with both at 0 hurts nobody. An `ARREnemy` target first gets `RecordHitDirection(location − center)` (task-166-02: its death debris flies radially; a robot on the centre keeps its previous direction, [[sys-enemies-base]]). Context: instigator = `Instigator`, effect causer = the source when it is an actor (else the instigator), source object = the source.
- Camera shake (task-418-01, provisional, owner 2026-11-02): protected `PlayShake(FPendingExplosion)`, called in `Explode` after `SpawnFX` and before `OnExplosion.Broadcast`, calls `URRCameraShakeLibrary::PlayCameraShakeAtLocation(this, Center, Radius × ShakeMaxDistanceFactor, ShakeScale, ExplosionShakeClass)` ([[sys-camera-shake]]). No-op when the class is null or the scale or distance is <= 0. One shake per resolved explosion, so one per chain link (a link restarts the shake at the stronger scale); none on fuse start. Strength 1 at the centre, 0.5 at the max distance, nothing beyond (2D distance to the camera target). Config property `ExplosionShakeClass` (`TSubclassOf<UCameraShakeBase>`, constructor default `URRExplosionCameraShake`, `None` = no shake), getter `GetExplosionShakeClass()` (BlueprintPure).
- Wall debris (task-439-01, provisional, owner 2026-11-05): protected `PlayWallDebris`, called in `Explode` after `SpawnFX`, plays `WallDebrisSystem` ([[bp-explosion-wall-debris]]) once through `URRNiagaraPoolSubsystem` at the centre. Input: the blocks `ARRArenaGrid::QueueDestroyCellsInRadius` reports in its optional `OutBrokenBlocks` (`FRRExplosionBrokenBlock`: cell, type; nearest first, barrels last). Left out: a barrel whose fuse was only lit (it still stands), and the blocks outside the radius of a deposit broken whole. No block = no request. Count = min(`WallDebrisMaxParticles` 60, blocks × `WallDebrisParticlesPerBlock` 4), blocks taken at an even stride. Each start point = cell centre + random ±`WallDebrisCellJitter` 0.4 × cell size per axis, height `WallDebrisMinHeight` 20 to `WallDebrisMaxHeight` 90 uu above the floor. Colour = `RRBlockColors::ForType` (`Grid/RRBlockColors.h`, also the defaults of the drone chip colours). User parameters: static names `WallDebris{Center,Positions,Colors,Count}Parameter`. Debris leaves in the explosion frame, while the grid removes far blocks over the next frames. Bound on chains: `WallDebrisPoolSize` 8 effects at once (480 particles at most), prewarmed at `OnWorldBeginPlay` in rendering game worlds; a link finding no free slot shows no debris. Config properties, same section as below; `WallDebrisSystem` is a soft path, loaded once, a missing package logged once. Debug and tests: `GetLastWallDebrisRequest`, `SetWallDebrisSystem`. Tests: `RogueRigs.Explosions.WallDebrisRequest` (`-nullrhi`), `RogueRigs.Explosions.WallDebrisAsset` (`-RenderOffScreen`). Destruction and damage take the same path as before. Not seen on screen: the owner validates the look.
- No-one-shot rule: if the target is an `ARRMiner` with `Health >= MaxHealth` (full health), his damage (`MinerDamage`) is capped at `Health − MinerHealthLeftAtFullHealth` (1): a full-health miner survives any single explosion with at least 1 Health. Below full health the damage is not capped and an explosion can kill.
- I-frames: respected. `URRGE_DamageExplosion` refuses targets with `State.Invulnerable` or `State.Dead`; the miner's i-frames (applied after a non-lethal explosion hit like any hit) make a following chain link within 0.25 s miss him.
- FX: the row's `FXClass` is loaded once (package checked first, a missing or abstract class is logged once at Log level and skipped), spawned deferred at the center with `SetRadius(GetVisualRadius(Radius))` = Radius × `VisualRadiusScale` and `SetHoldSeconds(VisualHoldSeconds)` (task-097-01). Only the visual is scaled: terrain and character tests keep the row radius.
- `ARRExplosionFX`: root `Mesh` (no collision, no shadow); over `Duration` (0.25 s) its scale goes linearly from 0 to `Radius / MeshRadius` (`MeshRadius` 50 = engine sphere). The scalar parameter `FadeParameterName` (`Opacity`) of a dynamic instance of material slot 0: with `HoldSeconds` 0 (class default, e.g. BP_HealRing) it goes from 1 to 0 over the same `Duration`; with `HoldSeconds` > 0 (every explosion) it stays 1 during the growth and the hold (full size), then goes from 1 to 0 over `FadeSeconds` (0.25 s) at full size. The actor destroys itself at `GetLifetime()` (explosions: 0.25 + 0.1 + 0.25 = 0.6 s; was 0.25 s). Visual size and hold need no Blueprint change (Kamikaze 312 uu → visual 327.6 uu, sphere scale 6.55). The Blueprint child (`/Game/RogueRigs/Blueprints/FX/BP_ExplosionFX`, task-011-06) sets the sphere mesh and a translucent material exposing `Opacity`.
- Burst and shockwave (task-197-02, owner 2026-10-16; values provisional, owner 2026-10-16): two optional class defaults on `ARRExplosionFX`, both unset in `BP_ExplosionFX`: unset = exactly the previous behaviour (no component created, same lifetime). Both set in the child `BP_ExplosionFX_Barrel` (bp tasks 197-04 to 197-06), which the `Barrel` and `Kamikaze` rows play (task-434-01, owner 2026-11-05). Contract names, exact:
  - `BurstSystem` (`UNiagaraSystem`, EditDefaultsOnly, category Explosion|Burst): one-shot (non-looping) system played once at `BeginPlay` at the explosion centre by a `UNiagaraComponent` named `Burst` created then (attached to the root for location only: absolute rotation zero and absolute scale 1, so the sphere growth does not scale it; auto-destroy off, destroyed with the actor). Before activation it gets the float user parameter named by `BurstRadiusParameterName` = `User.Radius` (the Niagara user parameter is `Radius` in the User namespace) = the visual radius in uu (row radius × 1.05, Barrel 735, Kamikaze 327.6). Not pooled (one per explosion with a burst). `BurstSeconds` 1.5 s: how long the actor stays alive for the burst.
  - `ShockwaveMaterial` (`UMaterialInterface`, EditDefaultsOnly, category Explosion|Shockwave): deferred decal material of the ground ring. At `BeginPlay` a `UDecalComponent` named `Shockwave` is created with a dynamic instance of it: projected straight down (absolute rotation pitch −90, like the ground telegraph: floor and rock tops), absolute scale 1, `DecalSize` = (`ShockwaveHalfDepth` 256, Radius, Radius) so the box half size equals the visual radius (ring at full progress on the box edge, UV distance 0.5 from the centre), `FadeScreenSize` 0, `SortOrder` = `ShockwaveSortOrder` 1 (above the ground telegraphs, 0), no navigation. Scalar parameters (same names as `M_GroundTelegraph`, [[sys-ground-telegraph]]; static names `ARRExplosionFX::Shockwave{Radius,Progress,Opacity}ParameterName`): `Radius` (uu, set once = visual radius), the ring has its own timeline since task-198-01 (owner 2026-10-16: the ring lasts after the sphere; was the sphere's): `Progress` 0 → 1 linearly over `ShockwaveGrowSeconds` 0.6 s (was `Duration` 0.25 s) at `Opacity` 1, then `Opacity` 1 → 0 over `ShockwaveFadeSeconds` 0.2 s at `Progress` 1 (both EditDefaultsOnly, category Explosion|Shockwave, provisional, owner 2026-10-16); `GetShockwaveLifetime()` = 0.8 s. The decal is hidden at `GetShockwaveLifetime()` (opacity 0 already), the mesh at `GetVisualLifetime()`, independently. The ring shape and colour are the material's (task-197-05; cartoon bands since task-446-03, [[bp-explosion-shockwave]]).
  - Cartoon look (task-446, provisional, owner 2026-11-05): no C++ change. The sphere material of `BP_ExplosionFX_Barrel` reads `1 - Opacity` as its fade progress (bands cool, a hole opens), the burst is a copy of the robot death flash sized by `User.Radius` and drawn above the sphere (translucency sort priority 1), the ring material draws cel bands from `Radius`, `Progress`, `Opacity` and `RingWidth`: [[bp-explosion-fx-barrel]], [[bp-barrel-explosion]], [[bp-explosion-shockwave]].
  - Lifetime: `GetVisualLifetime()` = the sphere timeline (0.6 s for explosions); `GetLifetime()` = the longest of `GetVisualLifetime()`, `GetShockwaveLifetime()` when `ShockwaveMaterial` is set, and `BurstSeconds` when `BurstSystem` is set. Burst set: 1.5 s (barrel and kamikaze; sphere hidden at 0.6 s, ring at 0.8 s, then only the burst plays). Shockwave without burst: 0.8 s. Neither: the sphere timeline, unchanged (BP_ExplosionFX).
  - First-frame warm-up (task-201-01): `OnWorldBeginPlay` (rendering game worlds only) loads every row's FX class (kept in `PreloadedFXClasses` for the world) and warms its `ShockwaveMaterial` (`ARRExplosionFX::GetShockwaveMaterial`, read on the class default) with `ARRDecalWarmUp`: the first shockwave no longer draws the engine default decal box (cause in [[sys-ground-telegraph]]). The two rows share one class and one material, warmed once per world (`AddMaterial` skips a known material). The ring has no static material cache, so the second-run defect of task-431 (static weak pointer of the telegraph) does not apply: the material is a hard property of the class default (task-434-01, read in the code, no packaged run).
  - Not verified by a test on screen: `-nullrhi` runs never activate Niagara. The kamikaze look (task-434-01) is left to the owner in game; not seen by the agent.

- data: `Content/RogueRigs/Data/Tables/ExplosionStats.csv` (`FRRExplosionStatsRow`: `Radius`, `Damage` (robots), `MinerDamage` (miner, task-294-01), `TerrainSource`, `FXClass`, `ShakeScale`, `ShakeMaxDistanceFactor` (task-418-01; float, default 0 = no shake)), read through the shared cache `FRRBaseStatsLoader::FindRow` ([[sys-gas-core]]): `Barrel` 700 / 40 / 40 / Barrel (radius owner 2026-10-12, task-114-01, was 350; visual 735 uu; miner damage = damage, unchanged; shake scale 1, factor 2 = max distance 1400 uu), `Kamikaze` 312 / 40 / 30 / Kamikaze (radius owner 2026-10-04, task-017-01, was 250; damage 40 to robots and 30 to the miner, provisional, owner 2026-10-23, task-294-01; was 30 to every character; shake scale 0.7, factor 2 = max distance 624 uu; shake values provisional, owner 2026-11-02, task-418-01), FX `/Game/RogueRigs/Blueprints/FX/BP_ExplosionFX_Barrel.BP_ExplosionFX_Barrel_C` for both rows ([[bp-explosion-fx-barrel]], Barrel task-197-06, Kamikaze task-434-01, was `BP_ExplosionFX`; the CSV is read directly at runtime, no DataTable asset to reimport). Config properties (defaults in code, override in `DefaultGame.ini` section `[/Script/RogueRigs.RRExplosionSubsystem]`): `ChainDelay` 0.08 s (provisional), `MinerHealthLeftAtFullHealth` 1, `VisualRadiusScale` 1.05 and `VisualHoldSeconds` 0.1 s (owner 2026-10-10, getters `GetVisualRadiusScale`, `GetVisualRadius(CollisionRadius)`, `GetVisualHoldSeconds`). `ARRExplosionFX` defaults: `Duration` 0.25, `FadeSeconds` 0.25 (gdd "fades in 0.25 s"; fade time after the hold chosen by task-097-01, tunable), `HoldSeconds` 0, `ShockwaveGrowSeconds` 0.6, `ShockwaveFadeSeconds` 0.2 (task-198-01, provisional, owner 2026-10-16).
- depends_on: [[sys-grid]], [[sys-damage]].
- implements: [[explosions]], [[destruction-rules]] (explosion side); [[barrel]] (the grid queues `Barrel` when a barrel's 1.5 s fuse ends, whatever brought it to 0, [[sys-grid]]; a barrel hit by an explosion fuses too, so a barrel chain link takes the fuse, not `ChainDelay`) and [[enemy-kamikaze]] (task-011-05) queue into it. Since task-096-01 a kamikaze killed by an explosion (lethal effect `URRGE_DamageExplosion`) still queues its own at once; killed by the player it queues it after its 0.65 s kill fuse ([[sys-enemies-kamikaze]]); one explosion per source either way.
- cost (task-114-01, `RogueRigs.BarrelFuse.Radius700FineGridAndCost`, editor Development build, `-nullrhi`): one 700 uu Barrel explosion at the densest centre of `arena_m1_fine` destroys 347 blocks; the frame that resolves it costs 7.8 to 8.5 ms more than an idle frame (three runs), `DestroyCellsInRadius` alone 4.6 to 5.6 ms for 341 blocks (the rest: `OnCellDestroyed` listeners, pickup requests, target search, FX). Above the 5 ms budget; built in task-121-01: block removal spread over frames at about 2 ms per frame (not measured, owner PC busy; `RogueRigs.Explosions.BlocksSpreadOverFrames` proves the split).
- gotchas: synchronous chains in damage callbacks cause recursion and double hits; that is why the queue exists — never call the grid or apply explosion damage directly. Target search iterates every actor of the world once per explosion (fine at M1 horde size). The character radius test uses the actor location only (no capsule radius). Barrels have no ASC: their shot health lives in the grid ([[sys-grid]] `ApplyShotDamage`); the grid queues their explosion with one `URRBarrelSource` per cell as source.
