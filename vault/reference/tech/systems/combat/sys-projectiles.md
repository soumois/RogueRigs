# Projectiles

- responsibility: carry shot data from the weapon to the impact.
- main_classes (in `Source/RogueRigs/Projectiles/`): `ARRProjectile` (abstract; no ASC, approved for now): root `UCapsuleComponent` `CollisionCapsule` (task-412-01, commit e1e65788; was a `USphereComponent` named `CollisionSphere`; `GetCollisionCapsule()`, overlap handler `OnCollisionBeginOverlap`; `InitCapsuleSize(14, 14)` by default: radius 14 uu for miner shots, owner fourth test task-034-01, was 12 in task-033-01 and 10 before; `ARREnemyProjectile` sets (10, 10), so turret bullets and robot shots behave as before; mesh not scaled; radius editable in the Blueprint, no projectile Blueprint overrides it). A per-shot half height replaces the sphere: `SetCapsuleHalfHeight` (clamped to at least the radius), `GetDefaultCollisionRadius(Class)` + `UProjectileMovementComponent` (no gravity, no bounce, `bForceSubStepping = false`, rotation follows velocity). `RRCollisionChannels::Projectile` (`RRCollisionChannels.h`) names the channel.

## Rules
- The projectile carries the spec made by the weapon's ASC at fire time and applies it only on impact ([[sys-damage]]).
- Overlap with an actor that has an ASC: `ApplyGameplayEffectSpecToSelf` once per actor; destroyed after `MaxHits` distinct actors (pierce, below). Pawns without an ASC are passed through. The owner weapon, the instigator (miner) and the owner's owner are ignored, both in the overlap handler and with `IgnoreActorWhenMoving`.
- Blocking hit: `OnProjectileStop` destroys it. If the hit actor is the `ARRArenaGrid` and the cell (`GetCellFromHit`: hit instance, else impact point) is a barrel, it first calls `ApplyShotDamage(Cell, GetCarriedDamage())` once ([[sys-grid]]). `GetCarriedDamage`: the fire-time snapshot of the weapon's `URRWeaponAttributeSet` `Damage` captured in the spec (same capture definition as `URRDamageExecution`: source, snapshot), read from `CapturedRelevantAttributes` without any ASC on the barrel; no critical roll; 0 when the spec has no such capture (enemy flat-damage shots do not hurt barrels). Every projectile stops on rock ([[destruction-rules]]); a projectile spawned inside a block stops at once.
- Collision: object channel `Projectile` = `ECC_GameTraceChannel1`, declared in `Config/DefaultEngine.ini` with default response Block (so rock HISM `BlockAll` responds Block to it). The capsule ignores every channel except Pawn (overlap), WorldStatic and WorldDynamic (block); projectiles ignore each other. A capsule/pawn pair resolves to the weaker response (Overlap), so a projectile never blocks a pawn; the miner capsule also sets Overlap on `Projectile` explicitly ([[sys-player]]). Robots take shots on their body-size `HitCapsule` only; their root capsule ignores `Projectile` (task-034-01, [[sys-enemies-base]]). The capsule is the only collision: `PostInitializeComponents` calls `DisableVisualCollision`, which sets every other primitive (the Blueprint `ShotMesh`) to NoCollision without overlap events (task-180-01: `BP_Projectile`'s mesh was QueryAndPhysics, WorldDynamic, Block Pawn; carried into robot root capsules, it made their next move resolve the penetration, a push up to 66 uu at a peak 1714 uu/s, and it stopped other bullets).
- Spawn point (task-412-01, e1e65788): the weapon's shot placement `GetShotPlacement()` ([[sys-weapons]]). With a miner holding a hand weapon, the capsule is centred on that weapon's `Muzzle` with half height = Muzzle Z - (floor Z + `ShotFloorClearance` 20): bottom 20 uu above the floor (below the block height), top about 2 x Muzzle height - 20; side effect: the shot stops on any WorldStatic or WorldDynamic blocker in that band. The projectile is spawned on the miner axis at that height, then `SweepTo(Muzzle)` runs one sweep: robots crossed are hit through the normal overlap (damage, pierce, knockback), a blocking hit calls `OnProjectileStop` (barrel damage, destroy), so a shot is never born inside a rock the miner hugs. Fallbacks and miner-less cases: [[sys-weapons]]. Turret bullets and robot shots keep their point origin and a 10 or 14 radius capsule, no sweep.
- First drawn frame (task-413-01, commit 148676a4): a projectile spawned during the world tick has its movement tick queued the same frame, so it moved `speed x dt` (3000 x dt: 27 uu at 110 fps, 50 at 60 fps) before its first draw. `HoldFirstMove()` / `IsHoldingFirstMove()` set `ProjectileMovement->bSimulationEnabled` false for the spawn frame; the projectile has an actor tick (`bCanEverTick` true, disabled by default, `TG_PostUpdateWork`) that runs once at the end of the held frame, re-enables the simulation and disables itself; life span is extended by the held frame dt, so range is unchanged. No effect outside the world tick (tests), on spent projectiles or once flying. Called by `ARRWeapon::SpawnShot` only when `bSweepFromMiner` and `SweepTo` returned true (rifle bullets and shotgun pellets); turret bullets and robot shots (`ARRShooter` calls `SpawnProjectile` directly) are not held. Hits land one frame (10-17 ms) later; speed, capsule and sweep unchanged. Measure: console variable `rr.Debug.ShotFirstFrame` (0 off, default; compiled out of shipping; `Debug/RRShotFirstFrameDebug.h/.cpp`) logs `LogRogueRigs` lines `rr.Debug.ShotFirstFrame shot=N fire ...` and `frame=+0/+1/+2` (`dist2D_fireMuzzle`, `dist2D_muzzleNow`, `distZ_muzzleNow`, `muzzleMoved2D`, `dt`, `renderedSinceSpawn`) read at `OnWorldPostActorTick`. Measured (-game about 100 fps, running miner, L_ArenaM1): 26-34 uu from the Muzzle per bullet on the first drawn frame before (hitches up to 172 uu), 0.0-0.8 uu after (shots 1-40); Muzzle moved 0.0-0.1 uu and ShotMesh Z offset 0 ruled out. Unverified: shotgun in game, 60 fps, visual look, motion blur.
- Speed and lifetime come from the weapon: speed `ProjectileSpeed`, lifetime `Range / ProjectileSpeed`.
- Piercing (task-024-01): `MaxHits` (>= 1, default 1) set at spawn from the weapon's `GetPierceCount()` at fire time ([[sys-weapons]]); `HitActors` (weak set) keeps every actor already damaged, so a pierced robot is never hit twice by the same bullet; on the `MaxHits`-th distinct hit the projectile is destroyed. Rock still stops it at once; a bullet that pierced robots still damages a barrel it then hits. Robot shots keep `MaxHits` 1.
- Hit direction (task-166-02): before applying the spec to an `ARREnemy`, `RecordHitDirection(velocity)` (forward when the velocity is zero), the direction its death debris flies ([[sys-enemies-base]]).
- Knockback (task-024-02): `KnockbackSpeed` (uu/s, default 0) set at spawn; after the spec is applied to an `ARREnemy`, `ApplyKnockback(flight direction × KnockbackSpeed)` ([[sys-enemies]]; a robot the hit killed is not pushed). Used by `ARRShotgunPellet` (`RRShotgunPellet.h/.cpp`, Blueprintable, concrete so the native shotgun fires without a Blueprint; no mesh) with the shotgun's `KnockbackForce` ([[sys-weapons]]). `ARRTurretBullet` (`RRTurretBullet.h/.cpp`, Blueprintable, concrete, no mesh): the turret launcher's default bullet, fired by turrets ([[sys-turret]]).
- Pool projectiles before any enemy pool (provisional; M2, if stress test S2 asks for it).
- Bounce, homing, tornado, lightning, explode on impact: deferred with the module framework ([[wishlist]]).

- entry_points: static `ARRProjectile::SpawnProjectile(World, Class, Transform, Owner, Instigator, Spec, Speed, Lifetime, MaxHits = 1, KnockbackSpeed = 0, CapsuleHalfHeight = 0)` (0 keeps the default half height = radius) (deferred spawn, `InitProjectile`, `FinishSpawning`; `GetMaxHits()`, `GetHitCount()`, `GetKnockbackSpeed()`; lifetime goes through `InitialLifeSpan`, speed through `InitialSpeed`), called by `ARRWeapon::FireOnce` and `ARRShotgun::FireOnce` ([[sys-weapons]]). Hit filter (task-020-02): virtual `CanHit(Actor)`, checked on overlap after the ignored actors; default = instance of `HitActorClass` (`EditDefaultsOnly`, None = any actor with an ASC); non-hittable actors are passed through. `bDamagesTerrain` (default true): barrel damage on a blocking hit. Robot shots: `ARREnemyProjectile` (`RREnemyProjectile.h/.cpp`, Blueprintable, task-021-01) sets `HitActorClass` = `ARRMiner` and `bDamagesTerrain` off: robots pass through, rock and barrels only stop it; fired by the shooter with its flat damage spec ([[sys-enemies-shooter]]).
- data: none of its own; values come from the weapon. Blueprint subclass sets the mesh (collision forced off at spawn) and may change the capsule radius.
- depends_on: [[sys-weapons]], [[sys-damage]].
- implements: [[burst-rifle]], [[shotgun]], [[turret]], [[enemy-shooter]].
- gotchas: see [[perf-risks]]. Fast bullets rely on the movement sweep for overlaps; no sub-stepping.
