# Damage numbers

- responsibility: every floating damage number of a world, from hit to Niagara particles (task-025-01). No actor per hit.
- main_classes: `URRDamageNumberSubsystem` (`Damage/RRDamageNumberSubsystem.h/.cpp`, `UTickableWorldSubsystem`, Game and PIE worlds only), structs `FRRDamageNumber` (Location, Value, bCritical, bHeal, Size, Color, SpawnTime, Velocity; one per hit or heal) and `FRRDamageDigit` (Location, Digit 0–10 = atlas cell, 10 = "+", Size, Color, bCritical, SpawnTime, Velocity; one per drawn sprite, task-025-04); `URRGameUserSettings` (`Settings/RRGameUserSettings.h/.cpp`, `UGameUserSettings` subclass, `bShowDamageNumbers` saved in the user's GameUserSettings.ini; `Config/DefaultEngine.ini` `[/Script/Engine.Engine]` `GameUserSettingsClassName=/Script/RogueRigs.RRGameUserSettings`).
- hits: no merging since task-059-01 ([[damage-numbers]]). `AddHit(Target, Value, bCritical, Location, InSpawnRadius = -1)` records one pending number per hit, pushed on the next tick; it starts at a uniform random point (`FMath::RandPointInCircle`) of a horizontal disc around `Location`, radius `InSpawnRadius`, or the config `SpawnRadius` when negative, so repeated hits on one robot start from different spots. The radius is per robot class: `ARREnemy::DamageNumberSpawnRadius` (EditAnywhere, 30) passed by `HandleDamageReceived`, set to 45 in the `ARRHeavy` constructor ([[sys-enemies-base]]). A number stays at its hit location even if the robot dies or is destroyed; `Target` may be null and is not used. Value ≤ 0 ignored. Crowd stacking (task-027-01): a new number whose screen footprint (digits x `DigitAdvance` x size wide along `DigitDirection`, size tall along `StackDirection`) overlaps another pending number, or a number still on screen (pushed less than `DisplayLifetime` ago, risen by `RiseVelocity` since its push, task-027-03), is lifted along `StackDirection` just above it, `StackGap` apart, at most `MaxStackSteps` times. Safe inside a GE execution (no spawn, no broadcast).
- heals (task-324-02, [[damage-numbers]] heal numbers line, values provisional, owner 2026-10-27): `AddHeal(Location, Amount, InSpawnRadius = -1)` records a heal number through the same pending list (shared protected `AddPendingNumber`): same spawn disc, size by value (`GetHitSize`, never critical), rise draw, pop and fade; colour `HealColor`; `AppendDigits` puts glyph `PlusGlyph` (10, "+") before the digits and centres the whole; the stacking footprint (`GetFootprint`) counts the "+" as one more cell. Amount ≤ 0 or toggle off = ignored (the toggle hides heal numbers too). Caller: `ARRPickup::ApplyHeal` ([[sys-pickups]]), with the HP actually gained (Health after the heal effect minus before, so +10 at 90/100 with a 15 HP pickup), at the miner capsule top + `ARRPickup::HealNumberHeight` (40), default spawn disc (`SpawnRadius` 30); nothing when the gain is 0. Level-up heals show no number.
- push: each tick, pending numbers (oldest first, at most `MaxNumbersPerTick`; the rest wait one tick) become `FRRDamageNumber`s (size `GetHitSize(Value, bCritical)` = `NumberSize` × lerp(`SizeMin`, `SizeMax`, clamp(Value / `DamageForMaxSize`, 0, 1)), × `CriticalScale` on a crit, task-239-01; the stacking footprint uses the same size; `NormalColor` / `CriticalColor` / `HealColor` for a heal; a rise velocity `DrawRiseVelocity()` drawn once per number, task-240-01: `RiseVelocity` rotated around the view axis `DigitDirection` × `StackDirection` by a uniform angle in [−`RiseAngleMax`, `RiseAngleMax`] degrees, so its screen direction turns left or right of screen up, × a uniform factor in [`RiseSpeedMin`, `RiseSpeedMax`]; every digit of the number copies it, so the number stays readable; the stacking keeps the mean `RiseVelocity` as approximation), are laid out into digits by `AppendDigits` (value rounded, at least 1, at most `MaxDigits` nines; most significant digit first, left to right along `DigitDirection`, `DigitAdvance` × size apart, centred on the number position), the batch id is incremented, the digits are written to the Niagara component and the numbers broadcast by the native `OnNumbersPushed`. Ticks without due numbers write nothing. Pauses with the world.
- Niagara: one persistent `UNiagaraComponent` created at world BeginPlay at the origin (owner world settings, absolute transform, not attached, no auto-destroy), built by hand like `SpawnSystemAtLocation` so it also exists in a process that cannot render (activated only when `FApp::CanEverRender`); a push reactivates it when its system completed; unset or missing package = nothing rendered, numbers are still recorded and broadcast. Per-digit user parameters and emitter recipe: [[sys-damage-numbers-niagara]]. At BeginPlay the asset's exposed parameters are checked: missing ones are logged once (`LogRogueRigs` Warning) and never written.
- entry_points: `ARREnemy::HandleDamageReceived` (capsule top + `DamageNumberHeight` 40, [[sys-enemies-base]]); `AddHeal` from `ARRPickup::ApplyHeal` (heal numbers); constant `PlusGlyph` = 10; `AreNumbersShown`, `GetPendingCount`, `GetSpawnRadius`, `GetNiagaraComponent`, `GetTotalPushedCount`, `GetParticleStats` (tests and `rr.Debug.Shots`), `AppendDigits`, `GetLastDigits`, `GetDigitBatchId`, `GetHitSize`, `DrawRiseVelocity`; `LogRogueRigs` Verbose line per push (batch id, digit count); `URRGameUserSettings::GetRRGameUserSettings`, `SetShowDamageNumbers` (effect at once; `SaveSettings` keeps it), `GetShowDamageNumbers`.
- data: config properties, `DefaultGame.ini` section `[/Script/RogueRigs.RRDamageNumberSubsystem]`: `NumberSystem` (default `/Game/RogueRigs/FX/NS_DamageNumbers.NS_DamageNumbers`), `SpawnRadius` 30, `NumberSize` 60, `SizeMin` 0.8, `SizeMax` 1.4, `DamageForMaxSize` 20 (twice the burst rifle's base Damage 10 in `WeaponStats.csv`; the three provisional, owner 2026-10-19, task-239-01; header defaults only; ≤ 0 = every hit at `SizeMax`), `CriticalScale` 1.6 (on top of the damage factor), `NormalColor` white, `CriticalColor` (1, 0.7, 0.35) (lighter orange, provisional, owner 2026-10-19, task-238-01; was gold (1, 0.55, 0.08) since task-027-01; header default only, not set in the ini), `HealColor` (0.1, 1, 0.2) (the heal ring green, [[color-code]] heal number row, provisional, owner 2026-10-27, task-324-02; header default only), `StackDirection` (0.766, 0, 0.643) (screen up of the camera at pitch -50, yaw 0), `StackGap` 4, `MaxStackSteps` 4 (0 = off), `DisplayLifetime` 0.7 s and `RiseVelocity` (0, 0, 85.7) (NS_DamageNumbers values; at BeginPlay `ReadDisplayFromSystem` replaces them only with a direct `InitializeParticle.Lifetime` and a non-zero `Particles.Velocity` spawn-script constant: the asset has neither (its random-range Lifetime Max is a stale 1.75 s, its rise is not a velocity constant), so the config values hold; `GetDisplayLifetime`, `GetRiseVelocity`, `IsDisplayReadFromAsset`, `GetDisplayedCount`), `RiseSpeedMin` 0.7, `RiseSpeedMax` 1.3, `RiseAngleMax` 35 degrees (0 = straight up; the three provisional, owner 2026-10-19, task-240-01; header defaults only), `MaxNumbersPerTick` 64 (≤ 320 digits per batch), `DigitAdvance` 0.62, `DigitDirection` (0, 1, 0) (screen right of the top-down camera at yaw 0), `MaxDigits` 5.
- depends_on: [[sys-damage]] (`OnDamageReceived`); `Niagara` private module dependency ([[build-toolchain]]).
- implements: [[damage-numbers]].
- gotchas: toggle off drops pending numbers on the next tick and ignores new hits. Tests `RogueRigs.DamageNumbers.*` ([[sys-automation-tests]]). Per-number cost: one stacking scan of pending and displayed numbers per hit; a burst of hits on one robot now shows one number per hit. Rise, fade, pop and glyph rendering live in the Niagara asset; layout and digit values live in C++. `DigitDirection` is fixed: a rotating camera would need it from the view. Heal number check (task-324-02, `-game`, miner at 60/100, three 15 HP pickups via `rr.Debug.SpawnNear <BP_PickupHeal> 120 0 3 15`): green "+15", "+15", "+10" stacked above the miner; captures in `Art/Source/damage-digits/task-324-heal-captures/` (`heal_before_*` = before the change, ring only, no number; `heal_after_*` = green numbers; `heal_robots_contact_sheet.png` = with 6 chasers around the miner, no robot number caught on it).
