# Ground telegraph

- responsibility: draw the [[ground-telegraph]] signal: a decal projected straight down (follows the floor and the top of rocks) whose material shows a zone circle and a progress circle growing to its edge. Pure visual: no gameplay rule, no collision.
- main_classes: `Source/RogueRigs/Telegraph/`: `URRGroundTelegraphComponent` (`RRGroundTelegraphComponent.h`, derives from `UDecalComponent`, Blueprint-spawnable), enum `ERRGroundTelegraphMode` (Danger, ZoneBlocked, ZoneActive).
- entry_points (owner usage): add the component to the owner (Blueprint or C++, attached under the root); call `SetRadius(uu)`, `SetMode`, then `Show()`; drive `SetProgress(0..1)` each tick of the hack, fuse or slam wind-up; `Hide()` when done. `SetOpacity(0..1)`. `SetTelegraphMaterial(Material)` replaces the material (stored values are reapplied). `Show()` returns false and stays hidden while the owner is gone. Statics: `HideAllOn(Actor)`, `DisableDecalsOn(Actor)`.
- material contract (built by task-112-02, deferred decal domain): dynamic instance parameters, names exact:
  - `Radius` (scalar, uu, ≥ 0): zone radius; the decal box half size equals it, so the zone circle is at the UV edge (UV distance 0.5 from the centre); use it to keep dot spacing and line width constant in uu.
  - `Progress` (scalar, 0..1): radius of the progress circle as a fraction of the zone radius; 1 = touches the zone circle.
  - `ZoneColor` (vector, linear RGB): zone circle colour from the mode.
  - `ProgressColor` (vector, linear RGB): progress circle colour, white by default.
  - `Opacity` (scalar, 0..1): global opacity of both circles and of the fill.
  - `FillOpacity` (scalar, 0..1, material default 0.25; task-118-01): opacity of the progress disc fill before `Opacity`. Not pushed by C++: the default applies, no C++ change needed (follow-up only if a per-mode value is wanted).
  - Asset (task-112-02): `/Game/RogueRigs/FX/M_GroundTelegraph`, domain Deferred Decal, blend mode Translucent, shading model Unlit, decal response None (no DBuffer dependency: it only adds colour and opacity). Graph: TexCoord0 and Radius/Progress into two Custom HLSL nodes returning masks; emissive = lerp(ZoneColor, ProgressColor, progressMask); opacity = max(zoneMask, progressMask) x Opacity. Defaults: Radius 700, Progress 0, Opacity 1, FillOpacity 0.25, ZoneColor orange, ProgressColor white.
  - Maths: q = (UV - 0.5) x 2 x Radius (world uu from the centre; the box edge is Radius). Rings are 12 uu wide, inner-aligned: a ring of radius r covers distances r-12 to r, so line width is constant in world units and the zone ring is not cut by the box. Edges: smoothstep(-aa/2, aa/2, e) with aa = fwidth(distance) (one pixel), e = signed distance to the ring edge. Zone ring: 48 dashes, 55 % on / 45 % off, atan2 angle, dash AA scaled to arc length (no seam artefact); dotted for every mode (Danger too). Progress ring: solid, radius = Radius x clamp(Progress, 0, 1), fully transparent when Progress <= 0.001. Fill (task-118-01): a third Custom node `fill` returns a disc of radius Radius x clamp(Progress, 0, 1) (one-pixel AA) x `FillOpacity`, 0 when Progress <= 0.001; opacity = max(max(zoneMask, progressMask), fill) x Opacity; the fill takes `ZoneColor` (the mode colour: white-blue hacks, gold blocked, orange danger) because the emissive lerp to `ProgressColor` follows the ring mask only. The fill radius equals the ring radius, so it reaches the zone circle only at Progress 1. The zone interior beyond it stays transparent. No glow.
  - Look ([[ground-telegraph]]): sharp lines, no diffuse disc; zone circle dotted, progress circle solid.
- decal warm-up (task-201-01, `ARRDecalWarmUp`, `Telegraph/RRDecalWarmUp.h`): cause of the one-frame pale green box when a telegraph or shockwave first appeared: uncooked runs (editor, `-game` from UnrealEditor) start with an incomplete shader map (logged: shader map present, complete 0); the first decal draw asks for its pixel shader, `FMaterial::TryGetShaders` (`Engine/Private/Materials/MaterialShared.cpp`) submits an on-demand compile and fails, and `TryGetDeferredDecalMaterial` (`Renderer/Private/DecalRenderingShared.cpp`) draws the fallback, the engine default deferred decal, over the whole box for that frame. Hidden decals have no scene proxy, so pre-created hidden components alone do not help; PSO precache is not it (it delays the proxy, it never draws the fallback). Fix: `ARRDecalWarmUp::WarmUp(World, Material)` (game worlds that render, deferred decal materials, once per material) draws one 4 uu decal per material 100 uu in front of the player camera (box touches no surface: nothing visible, fallback included) for 2 s of camera time, then destroys those decals and stops ticking; it keeps the materials referenced for the world. Called by every telegraph `BeginPlay` and by [[sys-explosions]] at world begin play; the grid creates one hidden barrel telegraph at `BeginPlay` ([[sys-grid-barrels]]). Defaults `WarmUpSeconds` 2, `CameraDistance` 100, `BoxHalfSize` 4 (EditDefaultsOnly). Verified in `-game` frames `barrelfx4_01` (fuse start) and `barrelfx4_11` (explosion): no green box. Not used under `-nullrhi`.
- packaged green square (task-431-01, commit 1939e428): a package showed the same-looking box over the whole fuse zone for the whole fuse, from the second run of one process (death and restart, or pause-menu exit and New Run) until relaunch. Not a shader or warm-up defect. Cause: `ResolveDefaultMaterial` kept the material in a static weak pointer resolved once; outside the editor nothing keeps the asset between two maps (`GARBAGE_COLLECTION_KEEPFLAGS` has no `RF_Standalone`), so the first map change collects it and later resolves returned null. `SetTelegraphMaterial(nullptr)` left `DecalMaterial` null, `ARRDecalWarmUp::WarmUp` skipped it (warmed set: shockwave only), and `FDeferredDecalProxy` draws the engine default decal for a null material. Hack zones and the heavy slam shared the default path. Fix: resolve again when the weak pointer `IsStale()`; a missing package is still checked once. Evidence, three-run scenario in one process, staged Development, `rr.Debug.FuseBarrel` ([[sys-cheats]]) with `rr.Debug.Delayed`, `rr.Debug.Shots`, `rr.Run.Restart`, `rr.Debug.PauseMenu exit`, `rr.Menu.NewRun` through `-ExecCmds`: before, log `decal material None` and 44 of 205 frames over 3 % pale green in runs 2 and 3 only; after, `decal material MID_M_GroundTelegraph_*` in each run and 0 of 194 frames, ring and burst seen. `-game` frames clean. Shipping built and staged for the owner's check; it has no debug commands, so it was only launched.
- data (component defaults, `EditDefaultsOnly`): `DefaultMaterialPath` (config `[/Script/RogueRigs.RRGroundTelegraphComponent]`, default `/Game/RogueRigs/FX/M_GroundTelegraph`; used when `DecalMaterial` is not set on the component); `DangerColor` (1.0, 0.45, 0.05), `ZoneBlockedColor` (1.0, 0.8, 0.2), `ZoneActiveColor` (0.7, 0.9, 1.0), `ProgressColor` white; `ProjectionHalfDepth` 256 uu (box above and below the component). Starts hidden, `FadeScreenSize` 0, absolute rotation pitch −90.
- users: `ARRArenaGrid` barrel fuse (task-114-01: pooled components on the grid, one per fusing barrel, at the cell centre: Danger, radius = Barrel explosion radius 700, progress = elapsed / 1.5 s fuse, hidden and returned to the pool at the explosion or rebuild; [[sys-grid]]); `ARRConsole` and `ARRBonusChest` hack zones (task-113-01, `GroundTelegraph` default subobject: radius = zone radius, ZoneBlocked / ZoneActive from the marked rocks, progress = hack progress, hidden at completion; [[sys-objectives]]); `ARRHeavy` slam (task-115-01, `GroundTelegraph` default subobject at the feet: Danger, radius = slam radius, progress = telegraph progress, shown only during Telegraph, progress reset on pool reuse; [[sys-enemies-heavy]]).
- hiding: `ARREnemy::HandleDeath` (every death, pending kamikaze fuse included) and `ARREnemy::OnPoolReleased` call `HideAllOn`; `ARRMiner::HandleDeath` too. While shown, the component ticks and hides when `IsOwnerGone` (owner invalid, being destroyed, `IsPooledIdle`, robot `IsDead` / `IsDeathPending`, miner `IsDead`): covers barrels, consoles and any other owner. Reuse from the pool leaves it hidden until the owner shows it. Destroyed with its owner (a component).
- no decals on actors (task-312-01): cause of the washed-out console, miner or drone in the hack disc: a deferred decal colours every primitive in its box (half depth 256 uu) that receives decals, sides included; the console and chest meshes and the drone received it. `DisableDecalsOn` turns `bReceivesDecals` off on every primitive (stencil-masked out of every decal, explosion decals too): `ARREnemy::PostInitializeComponents` (every robot), `ARRDrone::PostInitializeComponents`, `ARRMiner::BeginPlay` and each carried weapon at attach; and every telegraph `BeginPlay` on its owner when `bKeepDecalsOffOwner` (default true: console, chest, heavy). `ARRArenaGrid::CreateBarrelTelegraph` sets it false (`SetKeepDecalsOffOwner`) before registering: the floor and rocks are grid primitives. No material change.
- depends_on: [[sys-actor-pool]] (`IsPooledIdle`), [[sys-enemies]], [[sys-player]].
- implements: [[ground-telegraph]], [[readability-rules]] (ground circles).
- gotchas: without the material asset, values are kept and one warning is logged per process (the missing package is checked once, not retried); a shown telegraph then has a null `DecalMaterial` and draws the engine default decal (task-431-01). A decal is a scene component, not a primitive: no collision, overlap or navigation by construction. Weapons acquired later than the miner attach path are not covered. The Danger zone circle is dotted like the others (material choice, task-112-02). The material was compiled without error in the editor but its look was not checked on screen (owner judges).
