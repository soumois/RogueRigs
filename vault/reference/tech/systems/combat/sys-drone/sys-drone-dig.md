# Drone — dig

  - `RequestArcDig(Grid, ArcDirection)`: called every tick by the miner while it touches rock. Refused while hacking, without miner or grid. Selects the arc with `SelectArcCells` from the miner location; zero direction, or empty arc with no fallback cell = `ReleaseDig`, false. C++ overload `RequestArcDig(Grid, ArcDirection, TouchingCells)` (used by the miner, task-065-01): when the arc is empty, `SelectContactFallbackCells` digs the touching block whose direction from the miner is closest to the arc direction (ties within 0.01 cosine kept, at most `MaxArcBlocks`), so contact always digs at least one block. Cause it fixes: the miner steers the arc with any move input that has a positive dot with the wall direction (up to 90° off), while the cone is only ±60°; moving diagonally into a corner or along a wall pointed the cone between or past the touching blocks. Cells leaving the arc: `OnDigBlockStopped(Cell, false)`, damage kept. Cells entering: `OnDigBlockStarted(Cell)`. Enters Dig. The shake follows the veins, not the arc (`RefreshDigShake`, `ShakeCells`): every block of the dig targets shakes, the others stop.
  - `SelectArcCells(Grid, MinerLocation, ArcDirection, OutCells)` (C++ public, const): block cells whose center is within `ArcReachCells` × cell size of the front point (miner location + direction × capsule radius) and within `ArcAngleDegrees` / 2 of the direction seen from the miner location; sorted nearest to the front point, then smaller angle, then Y, X; at most `MaxArcBlocks`, so the front row is cut before the second. 2D, unrotated grid. Against a flat wall at 67 uu with a 34 uu capsule: the 3 wall blocks within 60°, plus the block behind the nearest, whose center sits exactly 1.5 cells from a touching front point; it is in only with no gap (character movement keeps about 0.1 uu, so 3 blocks in play; 4 when the miner stands at a cell center, 0.5 uu into the wall, as in the tests). Corners and diagonal pushes can give 4.
  - Dig tick: Orbit if miner or grid gone; cells no longer blocks (explosions) leave the arc (`OnDigBlockStopped(Cell, true)`); move toward the arc centre (mean of dug cell centers; Z = miner capsule bottom + `HoverHeight`); one beam (`LaserMesh`, task-232-01, owner 2026-10-19) from the drone to `GetDigImpactPoint()`, whatever the number of dug cells, hidden when the arc is empty; then the targets are built: each dug cell expanded to its deposit with `GetDepositCells` ([[sys-grid]]; by deposit id since task-070-01, so a touching deposit is not pulled in), duplicates removed, and each target takes `ApplyTerrainDamage(Cell, DeltaSeconds × GetMiningSpeed(), Drone)` once per tick, however many of its vein blocks are in the arc; a dug cell no longer a block leaves the arc (`OnDigBlockStopped(Cell, true)`); then the shake is refreshed, so destroyed vein blocks stop shaking. Dig holds with an empty arc until the miner's next request or release.
  - `GetDigImpactPoint()` (BlueprintPure, task-232-01): mean of `GetDigTargetPoint` over `DigCells`, projected on the plane of the face of the dug cell whose target point is nearest the miner (that face's axis coordinate replaces the mean's), so second-row cells do not pull the end into the rock. Zero outside Dig or with an empty arc. Recomputed on every call, so it follows the arc each tick; the beam end and the impact effect of [[task-233-dig-impact-particle]] read it. Flat wall (test `ArcContactDig`, 6 cells on two rows): the end sits on the front face at the mean Y, half block height. A corner arc mixes two face orientations; the projection uses the nearest face only.
  - Dig impact effect (task-233-02, [[bp-dig-impact]]): one owned `UNiagaraComponent` `DigImpactComponent` (constructor subobject, absolute transform, not auto-activated, never spawned per frame) plays `DigImpactSystem` (`EditDefaultsOnly`, default `/Game/RogueRigs/FX/Dig/NS_DigImpact` through a `ConstructorHelpers::FObjectFinder` constructor reference, so BP_Drone needs no edit; None = no effect). `UpdateDigImpact`, called in the Dig tick right after the laser (before the damage, so the dug cells still hold their type): `ComputeDigImpact` (shared with `GetDigImpactPoint`) gives the point and the grid side of the face it lies on; the component moves there, `User.WallNormal` = (side X, side Y, 0) (outward normal of that face, set every tick), `User.ChipColor` = `GetDigChipColor()`, `User.Intensity` 1, activated if inactive. Chip colour = the cell type held by most dug cells, ties to the type met first in `DigCells` (nearest the miner's front); `GetChipColorForType`: Rock `RockChipColor` (0.35, 0.28, 0.5) (the system's default lavender), Ore and SpecialOre `OreChipColor` (0.6, 0.15, 1.0) Duskite violet, HealOre `HealOreChipColor` (0.9, 0.06, 0.05) red, Barrel `BarrelChipColor` (1, 0.35, 0.05) orange, anything else rock ([[color-code]]; the three non-rock values provisional, editable on the Blueprint). Empty arc in Dig, or Orbit: `FadeDigImpact` lowers `User.Intensity` by `DeltaSeconds / DigImpactFadeTime` (0.15 s, provisional) and deactivates at 0 (live particles finish their lifetime). Hack and `EndPlay`: `StopDigImpact` (intensity 0, deactivate at once). Never active under `-nullrhi`.
  - Orbit tick: angle += 2π / `OrbitPeriod` per second; orbit point = miner + `OrbitRadius` × (cos, sin), Z = capsule bottom + `HoverHeight`; `VInterpTo` at `FollowInterpSpeed`. Hack tick: hover `HackHoverDistance` from the target toward the miner, laser to target + `HackLinkOffset`.
  - Leaving Dig (release, empty arc, `SetState(Orbit)`, hack, `SetOwnerMiner`, `EndPlay`): every dug cell stops shaking with `OnDigBlockStopped`, the beam hidden, arc cleared, orbit resumes from the drone's current angle. Damage stays on the blocks ([[sys-grid]]).
