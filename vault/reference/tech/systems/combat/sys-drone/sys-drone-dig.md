# Drone — dig

  - `RequestArcDig(Grid, ArcDirection)`: called every tick by the miner while it touches rock. Refused while hacking, without miner or grid. Selects the arc with `SelectArcCells` from the miner location; zero direction, or empty arc with no fallback cell = `ReleaseDig`, false. C++ overload `RequestArcDig(Grid, ArcDirection, TouchingCells)` (used by the miner, task-065-01): when the arc is empty, `SelectContactFallbackCells` digs the touching block whose direction from the miner is closest to the arc direction (ties within 0.01 cosine kept, at most `MaxArcBlocks`), so contact always digs at least one block. Cause it fixes: the miner steers the arc with any move input that has a positive dot with the wall direction (up to 90° off), while the cone is only ±60°; moving diagonally into a corner or along a wall pointed the cone between or past the touching blocks. Cells leaving the arc: `OnDigBlockStopped(Cell, false)`, damage kept. Cells entering: `OnDigBlockStarted(Cell)`. Enters Dig. The shake follows the veins, not the arc (`RefreshDigShake`, `ShakeCells`): every block of the dig targets shakes, the others stop.
  - `SelectArcCells(Grid, MinerLocation, ArcDirection, OutCells)` (C++ public, const): block cells whose center is within `ArcReachCells` × cell size of the front point (miner location + direction × capsule radius) and within `ArcAngleDegrees` / 2 of the direction seen from the miner location; sorted nearest to the front point, then smaller angle, then Y, X; at most `MaxArcBlocks`, so the front row is cut before the second. 2D, unrotated grid. Against a flat wall at 67 uu with a 34 uu capsule: the 3 wall blocks within 60°, plus the block behind the nearest, whose center sits exactly 1.5 cells from a touching front point; it is in only with no gap (character movement keeps about 0.1 uu, so 3 blocks in play; 4 when the miner stands at a cell center, 0.5 uu into the wall, as in the tests). Corners and diagonal pushes can give 4.
  - Dig tick: Orbit if miner or grid gone; cells no longer blocks (explosions) leave the arc (`OnDigBlockStopped(Cell, true)`); move toward the dig hover point (task-248-01, owner 2026-10-20, replaces the arc centre, which sat inside the rock): `GetDigImpactPoint` + the impact face's outward normal × `DigHoverFaceDistance` (90 uu, the free side), Z = miner capsule bottom + `DigHoverHeight` (130 uu), made clear by `ResolveClearHoverPoint` (if the miner-to-point segment, `DroneWallClearance` past it, crosses a block, the point moves back toward the miner to `DroneWallClearance` before the first block; `ARRArenaGrid::GetClearDistance`); the drone's XY when the arc is empty; one beam (`LaserMesh`, task-232-01, owner 2026-10-19) from the drone (since task-253-01 the laser arm's emitter tip, `GetLaserEmitterLocation`, [[sys-drone-parts]]) to `GetDigImpactPoint()`, whatever the number of dug cells, hidden when the arc is empty; then the targets are built: each dug cell expanded to its deposit with `GetDepositCells` ([[sys-grid]]; by deposit id since task-070-01, so a touching deposit is not pulled in), duplicates removed, and each target takes `ApplyTerrainDamage(Cell, DeltaSeconds × GetMiningSpeed(), Drone)` once per tick, however many of its vein blocks are in the arc; a dug cell no longer a block leaves the arc (`OnDigBlockStopped(Cell, true)`); then the shake is refreshed, so destroyed vein blocks stop shaking. Dig holds with an empty arc until the miner's next request or release.
  - `GetDigImpactPoint()` (BlueprintPure, task-232-01): mean of `GetDigTargetPoint` over `DigCells`, projected on the plane of the face of the dug cell whose target point is nearest the miner (that face's axis coordinate replaces the mean's), so second-row cells do not pull the end into the rock. Zero outside Dig or with an empty arc. Recomputed on every call, so it follows the arc each tick; the beam end and the impact effect of [[task-233-dig-impact-particle]] read it. Flat wall (test `ArcContactDig`, 6 cells on two rows): the end sits on the front face at the mean Y, half block height. A corner arc mixes two face orientations; the projection uses the nearest face only.
  - Dig impact effect (task-233-02, [[bp-dig-impact]]): one owned `UNiagaraComponent` `DigImpactComponent` (constructor subobject, absolute transform, not auto-activated, never spawned per frame) plays `DigImpactSystem` (`EditDefaultsOnly`, default `/Game/RogueRigs/FX/Dig/NS_DigImpact` through a `ConstructorHelpers::FObjectFinder` constructor reference, so BP_Drone needs no edit; None = no effect). `UpdateDigImpact`, called in the Dig tick right after the laser (before the damage, so the dug cells still hold their type): `ComputeDigImpact` (shared with `GetDigImpactPoint`) gives the point and the grid side of the face it lies on; the component moves there, `User.WallNormal` = (side X, side Y, 0) (outward normal of that face, set every tick), `User.ChipColor` = `GetDigChipColor()`, `User.Intensity` 1, activated if inactive. Chip colour = the cell type held by most dug cells, ties to the type met first in `DigCells` (nearest the miner's front); `GetChipColorForType`: Rock `RockChipColor` (0.35, 0.28, 0.5) (the system's default lavender), Ore and SpecialOre `OreChipColor` (0.6, 0.15, 1.0) Duskite violet, HealOre `HealOreChipColor` (0.9, 0.06, 0.05) red, Barrel `BarrelChipColor` (1, 0.35, 0.05) orange, anything else rock ([[color-code]]; the three non-rock values provisional, editable on the Blueprint). Empty arc in Dig, or Orbit: `FadeDigImpact` lowers `User.Intensity` by `DeltaSeconds / DigImpactFadeTime` (0.15 s, provisional) and deactivates at 0 (live particles finish their lifetime). Hack and `EndPlay`: `StopDigImpact` (intensity 0, deactivate at once). Never active under `-nullrhi`.
  - Dig debris (task-438-01, [[bp-dig-debris]], values provisional): in the Dig tick, a target whose `ApplyTerrainDamage` returns true calls `PlayDigDebris(Cell, Type)` (type read before the hit), except a barrel, which only lit its fuse. One `DigDebrisSystem` (`EditDefaultsOnly`, default `/Game/RogueRigs/FX/Dig/NS_DigDebris`, constructor reference) through `URRNiagaraPoolSubsystem`: cap `DigDebrisPoolSize` 16, prewarmed by `SetOwnerMiner`, skipped when no slot is free, so at most 192 sprites. At the cell centre + `DigDebrisSpawnHeight` (70 uu) above the floor; `User.ChipColor` = `GetChipColorForType`, `User.ThrowDirection` = (`GetDigFaceSide`, 0). Explosions, extractor, shots and direct `DestroyCell` calls never reach it. Measured by `Dig.DigDebrisBurst` and `Dig.DigDebrisAsset`.
  - Orbit tick = patrol (task-251-01, owner 2026-10-20, values provisional, [[sys-drone]]; replaces the task-247-01 escort heading, move blend, figure-eight and spring, and the task-248-01 slot candidates): the bob phases advance, then `UpdatePatrolPoint` re-picks the point when the drone is within `PatrolReachDistance` (25 uu, 2D) of it, when `PatrolTimer` runs out, or when `IsPatrolOffsetClear` fails (fallback: only the `PatrolFallbackRetryInterval` timer, 0.3 s). The point is `PatrolOffset`, a 2D offset from the miner, so the zone moves with the miner; nothing reads the miner's facing, aim or velocity. `PickPatrolPoint`: up to `PatrolPickAttempts` (12) candidates from the drone's own `PatrolRandom` stream (seeded in `SetOwnerMiner` from the unique id, never the global FMath stream), angle uniform, radius uniform in [`PatrolMinRadius` 60, `PatrolMaxRadius` 160]; a candidate is valid (task-248-01 rule) when `HasLineOfSight` holds from the miner to `DroneWallClearance` past it; the first valid one whose straight path from the drone stays at least `PatrolMinRadius` from the miner (`FMath::ClosestPointOnSegment2D`) and lies at least `PatrolMinHopDistance` (60 uu) from the drone wins, else the valid one whose path passes farthest from the miner (hop failures last); none valid = fallback, the miner's XY at `GetEscortHeight()` + `EscortFallbackExtraHeight` (above the 190 uu band on purpose). The lifetime is drawn in [`PatrolMinInterval` 3.5, `PatrolMaxInterval` 5] s. `ComputeOrbitPoint` = miner XY + `PatrolOffset`, Z = capsule bottom + `GetEscortHeight()` (task-249-01). Follow (XY): target = the point, or the miner when the drone has no clear line to the point but has one to the miner; desired speed `GetPatrolSpeed(d)` = lerp(`PatrolNearSpeed` 40, `PatrolFarSpeed` 700, smoothstep(0, `PatrolSpeedRampDistance` 300, d)), capped at d × `PatrolArrivalRate` (4 /s); velocity += (desired − velocity) × (1 − exp(−`PatrolVelocityResponse` 5 × dt)); a step that would cross the point lands on it (velocity zero). Steady lag behind a point moving at the miner's 600 uu/s: about 225 uu (smoothstep = 0.85), so a running miner always has the drone behind, even with its point 160 uu ahead. Z: `FInterpTo` toward the point Z at `PatrolHeightInterpSpeed` (6). Beyond `EscortSnapDistance` (2D) from the point it snaps and the velocity resets. `SetOwnerMiner` (and tests through `RestartPatrol`) pick a point from the miner location and snap onto it; `ResumeOrbit` (after Dig or Hack) zeroes the velocity and picks afresh. Last guard in Orbit, Dig and Hack: `ConstrainToClearCells` cuts a move whose end cell is a block (X only, then Y only, else none; the velocity component is zeroed). Dig and Hack still use `VInterpTo` at `FollowInterpSpeed`. Measured by `Dig.DronePatrol` ([[sys-tests-dig-ore]]). Hack tick: hover `HackHoverDistance` from the target toward the miner at `HoverHeight`, made clear by `ResolveClearHoverPoint` on the miner's grid (task-248-01), laser from the emitter tip to target + `HackLinkOffset`.
  - Alive (task-249-01, owner 2026-10-20, values provisional, visual only, no collision change, [[sys-drone]]): `Tick` calls `UpdateLiveliness` before the state tick and `UpdateAttitude` after it (both only with a miner and DeltaSeconds > 0). `UpdateLiveliness`: every `InterestRefreshInterval` (0.25 s) `RefreshInterest` looks for the nearest living `ARREnemy` within `InterestEnemyRadius` (900 uu, 2D from the miner; `TActorIterator`, `IsDead` skips pooled robots) and sets the alert flag when it is within `EscortAlertRadius` (700 uu); without an enemy, the nearest grid cell with `GetDepositId` != INDEX_NONE (standing deposit block, ore or heal vein; cells scanned in a square of `InterestSiteRadius` / cell size around the miner, about 15 × 15 at 67 uu) or the run objective (console while not complete, else the landed shuttle, from `URRRunFlowSubsystem`) within `InterestSiteRadius` (500 uu), whichever is nearer. The alert blend `EscortAlert` follows the flag (`FInterpTo`, `EscortAlertBlendSpeed` 1.5). `GetEscortHeight()` = lerp(`EscortCalmHeight` 158, `EscortAlertHeight` 174, alert) + `EscortBobAmplitude` (9) × sin (period 2.4 s) + `EscortBobSecondAmplitude` (4) × sin (period `EscortBobSecondPeriod` 0.9 s) − dip, clamped to [`EscortMinHeight` 130, `EscortMaxHeight` 190]; the drone height follows it at `PatrolHeightInterpSpeed` (no clamp on the drone itself). Dig keeps `DigHoverHeight` (130 uu), Hack `HoverHeight` (150 uu). Idle gestures: only in Orbit with the miner at or below `MinerRestSpeed` (50 uu/s); a countdown drawn in [4, 8] s from the drone's own `FRandomStream` (seeded in `SetOwnerMiner` from the unique id) starts one of ScanSweep (yaw + `ScanSweepDegrees` 35 × sin 2πu over 1.6 s), Nod (looks at the miner, pitch − `NodPitchDegrees` 12 × sin πu over 1.2 s) or Dip (height − `DipDepth` 12 × sin πu over 0.9 s), u = progress; the next countdown starts with the gesture; any miner movement or state change cancels the gesture and redraws the countdown. `UpdateAttitude`: look target `GetLookTarget()` = `GetDigImpactPoint()` in Dig, the hack target in Hack, the miner during a nod, else the interest actor or deposit cell, else the miner; yaw += `FInterpTo`(0, delta to that direction (+ scan offset), `LookYawInterpSpeed` 6) (the delay); since task-253-01 the step is capped at `BaseYawTurnRate` (120 deg/s), and in Dig and Hack it is the plain delta at that constant rate, no interp ([[sys-drone-parts]]). Miner acceleration = velocity difference per tick, clamped to 5000 uu/s², smoothed (`VInterpTo`, `AccelerationSmoothingSpeed` 10); in the drone's yaw frame pitch = −forward × `TiltDegreesPerAcceleration` (nose down when the miner speeds forward), roll = right × the same (banked toward the side acceleration, into turns), the nod added, each clamped to ±`MaxTiltDegrees` (15) and damped (`FInterpTo`, `TiltInterpSpeed` 6); `SetActorRotation(Pitch, Yaw, Roll)`. `SetOwnerMiner` resets everything and sets yaw = the miner's facing. Measured by `Dig.DroneAlive` ([[sys-tests-dig-ore]]).
  - Leaving Dig (release, empty arc, `SetState(Orbit)`, hack, `SetOwnerMiner`, `EndPlay`): every dug cell stops shaking with `OnDigBlockStopped`, the beam hidden, arc cleared, the patrol resumes from the drone's location at rest with a fresh point (`ResumeOrbit`, velocity zero; also after Hack). Damage stays on the blocks ([[sys-grid]]).
