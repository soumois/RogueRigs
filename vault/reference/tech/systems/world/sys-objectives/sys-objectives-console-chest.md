# Objectives — console chest

  - `ARRConsole` (Blueprintable, not abstract): marks every block of `MarkedCellTypes` (Rock, Ore, HealOre, SpecialOre; barrels excluded; ore table deposits included since task-120-01) whose cell center lies within `ZoneRadiusCells` of the zone centre (the footprint centre, where the actor stands: anchor + 0.5 cell on X and Y for the 2 × 2 footprint; Euclidean) through `ARRArenaGrid::SetCellMarked` ([[sys-grid]]); there is no separate mark radius (`MarkRadiusCells` removed by task-120-01, objective-console 2026-10-13: the marking radius equals the zone radius, so the zone holds no unmarked rock). `FindMarkableCells(Grid, Anchor)` (also used on the class default object by the drop picker) returns nothing when `MarkedCellTypes` is empty. States `ERRConsoleState`: Locked (marked rocks left, polled each tick), Idle, Hacking, Complete (final). Miner alive within `ZoneRadiusCells` 7 (469 uu on the 67 uu grid; 9 before task-120-01; 2D, from the actor): `ARRDrone::StartHack`; while linked progress += dt / `HackDuration` 12 (16 before task-120-01, 20 before task-042-01); otherwise `StopHack` and drain `DrainPerSecond` 0.05/s. Mesh: NoCollision by default.
  - `ARRBonusChest : ARRConsole` (task-056-01, Blueprintable, not abstract, [[objective-bonus-chest]]): same hack code, defaults `HackDuration` 8, `ZoneRadiusCells` 6, `DrainPerSecond` 0.05 (inherited), empty `MarkedCellTypes` (no marked rocks: starts Idle), footprint 2 × 2. Dropped once per level, after `CheckExtraction`, when the alert reaches `BonusChestAlertThreshold` and extraction is not ready (a cheat jump to full skips it only when extraction becomes ready in that tick); never pauses the bar; spot picked by the console picker (`FindObjectiveCell`) 30–45 cells away, no marked-rock preference, retried each tick. On `CompleteHack` (or `ForceComplete`) the reward, once: `AddXP(XPNeedFraction` 0.5 × current level need) (XPGain stat applies, implementation choice), `OreChunkCount` 5 pickups of `OrePickupClass` (none = grid `GetDropPickupClass(Ore)`) popped around it, evenly spread yaw, and `URRLevelUpComponent::QueueBonusChoice(BonusLuck` 3) (offer level 0, owner luck + 3 for that offer and its reroll; same delay and pause as a level-up choice). All EditDefaultsOnly.
  - Zone visual (task-113-01, rendering only, rules unchanged): `ARRConsole::GroundTelegraph` (`URRGroundTelegraphComponent`, created in the constructor under `ConsoleRoot`, getter `GetGroundTelegraph`; inherited by `ARRBonusChest` and both Blueprints, [[sys-ground-telegraph]]). `InitializeConsole` sets `SetRadius(GetZoneRadius())` (`ZoneRadiusCells` × grid cell size: console 7 × 67 = 469 uu, chest 6 × 67 = 402 uu), the mode and progress, then `Show()`. Mode: `ZoneBlocked` while marked rocks remain, `ZoneActive` when none (chest: at once); refreshed on every marked-rock count change. Progress = `HackProgress` on every `SetProgress` (rise and drain: the circle shrinks out of the zone). `Hide()` in `CompleteHack` and `EndPlay`. Old diffuse circle, Blueprint-only (REMOVED by task-113-02, 2026-10-13: both HackRing components and both SetRelativeScale3D calls are gone, the GlowAmount calls stay; MI_GroundRingTeal and MI_GroundRingGold are left in place, unreferenced). It was: in `BP_ObjectiveConsole` the `HackRing` component (engine Plane, `MI_GroundRingTeal`) and the `SetRelativeScale3D(HackRing)` call in its `OnHackProgress` graph; in `BP_BonusChest` the `HackRing` component (Plane, `MI_GroundRingGold`) and the same `SetRelativeScale3D` call in its `OnHackProgress` (the `GlowAmount` mesh glow calls stay). Checked in the assets on 2026-10-12.
  - Landing clearance (task-026-00, `RRLandingFootprint`): `ARRConsole::FootprintCells` 2 × 2 and `ARRShuttle::FootprintCells` 4 × 3 (cells of 67 uu, EditDefaultsOnly; meshes 100 × 100 and 240 × 160 uu), rotation zero, X along the grid X axis. The footprint spans `Anchor - (Size - 1) / 2` to `+ Size - 1` per axis; the actor stands at its centre (a cell corner for an even size); `GetCell` is the anchor. A spot is valid only when every footprint cell is Floor reachable in the flow field. Fallbacks, each logged as a warning: console, no fitting spot in the ring → any ring cell (mesh may overlap blocks); shuttle, no fitting spot in the 37–50 band → the fitting spot nearest to the band, then the reachable floor cell nearest to it (footprint ignored).
