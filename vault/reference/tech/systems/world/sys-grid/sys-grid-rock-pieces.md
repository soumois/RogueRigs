# Grid — dual-grid rock pieces (task-230-07)

- responsibility: draw rock, ore, heal ore and special ore cells (`IsRockPieceType`; barrels keep their own mesh) with one slab piece per cell corner, design in [[rock-render-piece-set]]; gameplay, health and collision cubes unchanged.
- main_classes: `ARRArenaGrid` (`Grid/RRArenaGrid.h/.cpp`): `ERRRockPieceShape` (Full, Edge, Outer, Inner, Diagonal), `ERRRockPieceSet` (Intact, Broken: swap and meshes in [[sys-grid-rock-damage]]), `FRRRockPieceMeshes` (one mesh list per shape, entry = variant), `FRRGridPieceVisual` (set, shape, variant, chunk, HISM, `InstanceCorners`).
- build: corner (x, y) at (x · CellSize, y · CellSize), (W + 1) × (H + 1) corners; mask bit = (+X side) | (+Y side) << 1 over the 4 cells around. `BuildPieceTable` turns the canonical rock corners of `SM_RockSlab.report.json` (Y mirrored when `bRockPieceImportFlipsY`) by 0-3 yaw quarter turns and keeps the first shape and turn per mask. Variant = hash(corner, `RockPieceSeed`) mod mesh count; full pieces get a hash-chosen turn, the others the table turn. Transform: corner on the floor top, scale CellSize / `RockPieceMeshSize` horizontally, BlockHeight / `RockPieceMeshHeight` vertically. One ISM (plain, remove at swap; HISM until task-230-14) per set, shape, variant and chunk (corner / `ChunkCells`, last line in the last chunk), created on first instance from the `RockInstances` template, override materials cleared, `RockPieceMaterial` if set, NoCollision, `bDisallowNanite` = !`bRockPiecesUseNanite`. Instances added in one batch per ISM; maps `CornerPieceVisual`, `CornerPieceInstance`, plus `InstanceCorners` per ISM (remove at swap, patched like the cell maps). Logged: `N rock pieces in M piece ISMs`.
- floor: a slab under every non-void cell (`HasFloorSlab`), `bBlocksFillFloorSlab` false; a destruction adds no floor (guarded add kept).
- destruction: `DestroySingleCell` on a piece type calls `UpdateCellCornerPieces`: each of the 4 corners removes its old instance and adds the new shape (none when empty), `MarkRenderStateDirty` on add (task-202-01 rule); same ISM: `UpdateInstanceTransform`. Not batched per frame (open lead in [[rock-render-rebuild]]).
- properties (provisional): `RockPieceMeshes` (empty: meshes come with [[task-230-11-rock-slab-assign]]), `RockPieceMaterial` none, `RockPieceMeshSize` 67, `RockPieceMeshHeight` 100 (report values), `bRockPieceImportFlipsY` true (checked in game by [[task-230-12-rock-slab-check]]: outlines join, no mirroring seen), `RockPieceSeed` 1, `bRockPiecesUseNanite` true, `bBrokenRockPiecesUseNanite` false (approved by owner 2026-10-19; task-230-17: the broken set ISMs get `bDisallowNanite`, fallback mesh; under Nanite the M_RockSlab WPO sag did not draw in game, with `r.Nanite 0` it did; intact pieces stay on Nanite, so their WPO shake is likely not drawn either, unverified).
- gotchas: unset meshes are tolerated (pieces placed and tracked, nothing drawn, Log line): until task-230-11 the rock is invisible in game, only collision and floor. Rock and special ore have no per-cell render instance; shake, outline and damage live in the piece custom data, ore and heal ore draw crystals ([[sys-grid-rock-damage]]). `RockMesh`, `OreMesh`, `HealOreMesh`, `SpecialOreMesh` now only shape the collision ISMs. Tests: [[sys-tests-dig-ore]].
- collision (checked in game by [[task-230-13-rock-collision-fix]], 2026-10-18, L_ArenaM1 -game, no code change): the collision chunk ISMs exist (`RockCollision_<x>_<y>`, 156 rock chunks) with `/Engine/BasicShapes/Cube`, profile BlockAll, ObjectType WorldStatic, QueryAndPhysics; the miner capsule (radius 34) is Pawn, QueryAndPhysics. A scripted run into a wall (`rr.Pacing.Run 20 1 0 2` with `rr.Pacing.RunLookahead 0`) stops the miner centre at x 7136 against the rock face at x 7102 (face + radius) and turns; on the +X wall the miner enters a cell only after the drone digs it. The owner's walk-through came from `BugItGo`: the engine cheat (`UCheatManager::BugItWorker`) calls `Ghost()` (actor collision off, flying), measured `bActorEnableCollision = False` and a straight flight through rock and void. After a `BugItGo` in a check run, send `Walk` to restore collision (measured: collision True, wall blocks again).
- ISM, not HISM ([[task-230-14-barrel-chain-crash]], 2026-10-18): with piece HISMs every dig fired the ensure "precision loss while converting matrix to GPU format" in `FDistanceFieldSceneData::UpdateDistanceFieldObjectBuffers`, and the 30-barrel chain crashed in `LumenMeshCards.cpp:1332`. The transforms the grid adds were all valid (logged on a dig: corner on the floor top, scale 1, yaw multiple of 90). Cause in the engine (UE 5.8 source): `UHierarchicalInstancedStaticMeshComponent::RemoveInstance` keeps `InstanceCountToRender` and shrinks the reorder table, so until the async tree rebuild one render slot is mapped by no instance; the legacy reordered instance proxy fills its arrays with `SetNumUninitialized` and only hides that slot (`VisibleInstances`), while the distance field scene (`ProcessPrimitiveUpdate` reads every `GetInstanceToWorld`) and the Lumen mesh cards still read its garbage transform. Pieces are removed and added on every dig, in new or just emptied HISMs. Plain ISMs track instances by id with no slot gap: same dig run, 0 ensures. The cell HISMs (barrel, ore and heal crystals, floor) still remove instances the HISM way (they did before task-230 too); none of their removals showed the ensure in the runs of this task.
