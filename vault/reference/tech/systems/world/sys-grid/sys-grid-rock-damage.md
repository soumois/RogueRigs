# Grid — rock damage display data (task-230-08)

- responsibility: per-piece data the rock material reads ([[rock-render-damage]]), broken piece swap, ore and heal crystal clusters. Gameplay, health and collision unchanged.
- main_classes: `ARRArenaGrid` (`Grid/RRArenaGrid.h/.cpp`): `GetCellDisplayDamage`, `ComputeCornerPieceData`, `WriteCornerPieceData`, `RefreshPieceDataAround`, `BreakCellCornerPieces`, `CornerBroken`, `GetCrystalTransform`.
- data: 13 custom data floats per piece (damage, shake, marked, solid mask per quadrant), layout and damage rule in [[sys-grid-rock-piece-data]].
- broken swap: `ApplyTerrainDamage` on a piece cell sets `CornerBroken` on its 4 corners once d > 0 and rebuilds those pieces in the broken set; sticky until the next build (a destroyed cell's corners stay broken). Meshes `RockPieceBrokenMeshes` (per shape, entry = variant; `SM_RockSlabBroken_<Shape>_01/_02`, assigned in [[task-230-11-rock-slab-assign]]), `RockPieceBrokenMaterial` (null: `RockPieceMaterial`). Variant hashed per set, same turns as intact.
- writes: at build the solid mask only; then a damage tick or destruction rewrites corners x − 1..x + 2, y − 1..y + 2; shake start or stop and mark change rewrite the 4 corners. Only changed instances are written, each touched piece ISM dirtied once per frame. No instance moves for rock shake.
- shake (task-231-01, owner 2026-10-19): 8 uu horizontal, 3 uu vertical, 8 Hz (`ShakeAmplitude`, `ShakeVerticalAmplitude`, `ShakeFrequency`; were 25 and 8). Rock pieces shake in M_RockSlab from MI_RockSlab `ShakeParams` (8, 3, 8, 50), kept equal to the C++ values by hand ([[bp-rock-slab-material]]); ore and heal crystals move their instance by the C++ values. Checked in game (`-game`, D3D12 SM6, Nanite on): the shake draws on intact Nanite pieces (cells shaken by `rr.Debug.ShakeCell` with no damage) and on broken pieces; captures `Saved/DigShake/before|after/` (not committed). The task-230-17 note "WPO not drawn under Nanite" does not hold for the shake; the Nanite sag was not rechecked.
- crystals: ore and heal ore cells have a per-cell rendering instance again: one cluster in the ore or heal ore chunk HISM, lockstep with collision (removed with the cell, marked and ore colour floats as before). `OreCrystalMesh` default `SM_OreChunk_01`, `OreCrystalMaterial` / `HealOreCrystalMaterial` (null: mesh materials; heal material: [[task-230-10-heal-crystal-material]]), `OreCrystalScale` 1.5 (provisional), bounds bottom on the block top, hashed yaw. Crystals still shake by moving their instance (existing tick). Special ore and weak rock: no crystal.
- deposit crystals (task-234-03, [[ore-blocks]]: a deposit is one entity): when `OreDepositMeshes` (Ore) or `HealDepositMeshes` (HealOre) holds a non-null mesh (`UsesDepositCrystals`; null entries skipped), that type draws one formation per deposit instead of the per-cell clusters (its chunk visuals keep the collision ISM only). Mesh = non-null entry hash(deposit id, `DepositCrystalSeed` 1) % count, yaw a second mix of that hash in quarter turns, at the centre of the deposit cells' bounding box on the block top (mesh pivot = footprint centre, base z 0), uniform `DepositCrystalScale` 1 (134 uu footprint on 2 x 2 cells of 67). Materials: the ISMs keep the mesh slot materials set at import (Duskite one slot `crystal`; heal `rock` and `crystal`); `OreDepositMaterial` / `HealDepositMaterial` (default null) override, when set, only the slot named `DepositCrystalSlotName` (default `crystal`); a mesh without that slot keeps all its materials. One plain ISM per type and variant over the whole grid (`FRRGridDepositVisual`, remove at swap, no collision; task-230-14 HISM lesson), maps `DepositCrystalVisual` / `DepositCrystalInstance`. Removed at the first broken block of its deposit, any source (`DestroySingleCell`). Shakes by moving its instance (same amplitudes, phase of its first cell) while any standing block of its deposit shakes, back at rest when none does. Marked float = marked if one standing block is; ore colour floats as the per-cell crystals. Both lists empty by default: per-cell clusters unchanged until the meshes are assigned ([[task-234-03-deposit-crystal-visual]]). A grid whose cells were replaced (stress tool, no deposits) draws no ore crystal in deposit mode.
- crystal shadows (task-237-01, owner 2026-10-19): `bDepositCrystalsCastShadow` (Grid|Ore Deposits, default false). False: every component `CreateChunkComponent` builds from the Ore or HealOre render template (deposit formation ISMs and per-cell cluster fallback chunks) gets `CastShadow`, `bCastDynamicShadow`, `bCastStaticShadow` and `bCastContactShadow` off, so no shadow at all. True: they copy the template shadow flags (`CastShadow`, dynamic, contact). Read at component creation (`BuildInstances`); rock pieces, barrels and collision ISMs untouched. Chunk components now also copy `bCastDynamicShadow` and `bCastContactShadow` from their template.
- gotchas: meshes unset are tolerated; crystals do not follow the broken-piece sag (open). Broken pieces render off Nanite (`bBrokenRockPiecesUseNanite` false, task-230-17): the sag is WPO and Nanite did not draw it. Check in game: `rr.Debug.DamageCell <X> <Y> <Fraction>` (logs the 4 corner pieces, mesh and 13 floats) and `rr.Debug.Camera <Pitch> <Arm> [Yaw]` ([[sys-cheats]]). Tests: `RogueRigs.Dig.RockDamageData` ([[sys-tests-dig-ore]]).
