# Grid — deposits

- deposits (task-070-01, [[ore-blocks]]): `FRROreTypeRow` (`Grid/RROreTypeRow.h`), one row per ore type in `Content/RogueRigs/Data/Tables/OreTypes.csv` (row name = ore id; columns `CellType`, `RockShare`, `GroupSize`, `ChunkValue`, `HealValue`, `Color`), read through `FRRBaseStatsLoader`; `Duskite` (renamed from Gold, task-100-01): Ore, 0.08, 2, 1, 0, colour alpha 0 (no colour written, the crystals keep the violet of their material; name provisional); `Heal` (task-088-02, [[heal-ore]]): HealOre, 0 (never placed: heal veins come from the layout), 2, 0, 15 HP per pickup, colour alpha 0 (no colour written, the heal material keeps its own). `BuildDeposits` runs after `LoadLayout`: layout Ore and HealOre cells become one deposit per 4-connected group, taking the first row (by id) of their block type (`arena_m1_fine`: 12 heal veins of 2 × 2, 48 cells, row `Heal`), then, when `bPlaceOreDeposits` (true), each row with a share places round(share × layout rock / size²) squares of GroupSize × GroupSize rock blocks, turned into the row CellType: anchors scored by a fixed integer hash of (x, y, `OreSeed` (1), CRC of the ore id), taken greedily while their square is still all rock, so only rock is converted (never heal ore, barrels, floor) and deposits may touch; no start-zone protection. Logged per type (`359 Duskite deposits of 2 x 2` on `arena_m1_fine`, 17 964 rock blocks after task-088-01; 44 on `arena_m1`). `FRRGridDeposit` (OreId, CellType, Cells, ChunkValue, HealValue, Color, `bHasColor`, `bPlacedFromTable`), `Deposits` (id = index), `CellDepositId` (cleared when a block breaks). Colour: block types of deposits with a row whose colour alpha is above 0 get custom data floats `OreColorCustomDataIndex` (1) to +2 = R, G, B on every instance, for the material (task-070-02). Consoles mark table deposit blocks like any rock inside their zone (`ARRConsole::FindMarkableCells`, [[sys-objectives]]; skipped before task-120-01).
- whole on explosion (task-187-01, [[ore-blocks]] owner 2026-10-15): `DestroyCell` with an explosion source (`IsExplosionSource`: Barrel, Kamikaze; every explosion row, the barrel queue and `DestroyCellsInRadius` / `QueueDestroyCellsInRadius` use them) on a deposit block reads the deposit's standing blocks by id (`GetDepositCells`, before the cell leaves the deposit), destroys the block, then the others in the same call through the protected `DestroySingleCell` (no re-expansion: no recursion; a block already broken, e.g. still in the explosion queue, is skipped), the remaining deposit pickups popping at once (loot over the dig below), unmark and `OnCellDestroyed` (console marked rocks counted per block; flow field and minimap update from those events as for any destruction; the flow field collects the opened cells and repairs them in its next budgeted pass). Duskite and heal veins alike; touching deposits stay separate (by id). Drone and Shot destroy one block (the drone mines whole deposits itself). `DestroyCellsInRadius` counts every block broken this way (`DestroyedBlockCount`). Tests: `RogueRigs.Dig.FineGridDefaults` and `RogueRigs.BarrelFuse.Radius700FineGridAndCost` expectations include touched deposits; `RogueRigs.Ore.ExplosionDrops` unchanged (4 blocks, 4 chunks). Verbose log `deposit broken whole from (x, y), N block(s)`. Measured (-game, `rr.Stress.Run 10 2 4 1 chain`, Barrel, `arena_m1_fine`): deposit 161 with 2 of its 4 cells inside the 700 uu radius broke whole, 4 blocks, 4 chunks requested; 15 other deposits 4 blocks / 4 chunks each.
- loot over the dig (task-235-01, [[ore-blocks]], [[heal-ore]], owner 2026-10-19): a deposit holds N = blocks x `DestroyDropCount` pickups (`GetDepositPickupCount`, 4 for a 2 x 2, Duskite and heal alike) and keeps two runtime counters on `FRRGridDeposit`: `PoppedPickups` and `BrokenBlocks`. `ApplyTerrainDamage` on a deposit block computes the block dig progress p = 1 - health / max health (the drone gives every block of the deposit the same damage, so any block is the deposit progress) and pops up to floor(p x N + 0.001) through the protected `PopDepositPickups`, the destroying hit included (p = 1 pops the last one, so a dug deposit owes nothing at its break): 1.5 s dig at mining speed 1 = one pickup every 0.375 s, mining speed scales it (damage per tick = DeltaSeconds x MiningSpeed). Health is never restored, so a stopped dig keeps its progress and count and resumes. `DestroySingleCell` on a deposit block increments `BrokenBlocks`, then pops up to N for an explosion source (the remaining pickups at once) or up to BrokenBlocks x `DestroyDropCount` otherwise (a block destroyed outright, e.g. a stress or debug `DestroyCell` with source Drone, owes its own share). Pickup k pops from the centre of its own block `Cells[k / DestroyDropCount]` (not the deposit centre), as the same `SpawnPickups` request as before (same pop motion, value `ChunkValue`, heal `HealValue`; heal refusal at full health unchanged, [[sys-pickups-lifecycle]]). Log line per dig pop (`LogRogueRigs` Log): `deposit <id> (<ore>) pickup k/N popped at <world s> s, progress <p> (dig) from (x, y)`; explosion and break pops are Verbose. Blocks outside any deposit keep the former rule (one pickup per multiple of `DropInterval` crossed while standing, `DestroyDropCount` at destruction). Requests go to `URRPickupSubsystem::RequestSpawn` (pool, spread over `SpawnMaxFrames`); rule summary in [[sys-pickups]]. Tests: `RogueRigs.Dig.HealOreDrops` (one heal vein block dug frame by frame: 4 pops at frames 23, 45, 68, 90 within +- 1 of 22.5 k, the last on the destroying hit, the 3 other blocks then blown up pop nothing), `RogueRigs.Ore.MineDeposit` and `RogueRigs.Ore.HealVein` (drone dig: 4 pops 21-24 frames apart, the last on the break frame), `RogueRigs.Ore.ExplosionDrops` unchanged (4 and 12 at once). `Ore.HealVein` kept its pre-existing failure (3 of 4 heal pickups collected, 55 HP instead of 70: a pickup rests outside the pickup radius; same messages before this task), its new timing checks pass. Task-250-01: the pop from the pickup's own block (up to 2 cells from a miner digging the vein) plus the 80-300 uu pop lets a pickup settle beyond the 300 uu pickup radius of a still miner; it stays at rest and is collected once the miner comes near (heal-ore rule, no game change); the test now walks the miner to it ([[sys-tests-dig-ore]]). Measured (-game, `L_ArenaM1`, `BugItGo` next to the vein then `Walk`, mining speed 1): heal vein 7 pops at 5.309, 5.689, 6.058, 6.441 s (gaps 0.380, 0.369, 0.383 s); Duskite deposit 13 at 12.308, 12.684, 13.060, 13.433 s (gaps 0.376, 0.376, 0.373 s); each the last on the break. Unverified: no visual check of the pops, mining speed above 1 not run.

- look (task-234-03): a deposit of a type with deposit meshes is drawn as one crystal formation (variant and quarter turn hashed from its id), removed at its first broken block; rule and properties in [[sys-grid-rock-damage]].

- pulse data (task-257-01): the crystal material M_XPNutGlow reads per-instance custom data index 4 = pulse phase (turns, 0..1, neutral 0) and 5 = pulse rate (scale of PulseFrequency, neutral 1) on the deposit crystal ISMs; indices 0 (console mark) and 1-3 (ore colour) are unchanged, so those components need NumCustomDataFloats of at least 6 ([[bp-pickup-xp]]).
- pulse desync (task-257-02, owner 2026-10-20, provisional): `ARRArenaGrid` properties (`EditDefaultsOnly`, category `Grid|Ore Deposits`) `PulseSeed` 1, `PulseRateMin` 0.8, `PulseRateMax` 1.2, `PulsePhaseCustomDataIndex` 4, `PulseRateCustomDataIndex` 5. The Ore and HealOre templates are sized to at least max(4, 5) + 1 = 6 floats (`GetCrystalPulseFloatCount`), so the per-cell cluster chunks and the deposit formation ISMs (`BuildDepositCrystals`, now max of template, marked + 1 and 6) have 6 floats; indices 0 (mark) and 1-3 (colour) unchanged. Each formation instance gets phase = `FRRPulseDesync::Phase(PulseSeed, DepositDomain, deposit id)` (uniform in [0, 1) turns) and rate = `FRRPulseDesync::Rate(..., 0.8, 1.2)` (uniform), written once at build (`WriteCrystalPulseData`; the data follows the instance through remove-at-swap); per-cell clusters (a type with an empty deposit mesh list) use the cell index in `CellDomain` (`WriteCellCrystalPulseData`). `FRRPulseDesync` (`Pickups/RRPulseDesync.h`) is a pure integer hash (lowbias32), never the global FMath stream: same layout and seed, same pulses on every run. The pickups use the same helper ([[sys-pickups-lifecycle]]). Check: `RogueRigs.PulseDesync.SeededValues` ([[sys-tests-pools-pickups]]). Unverified: the look on screen (no capture). Since task-258-01 the formation ISMs of a mesh with a rock slot (heal deposits) get no pulse data (next bullet).
- heal deposit self-vibration (task-258-01, owner 2026-10-20: the upper part of a heal deposit shook untouched, also in the editor viewport). Cause: SM_HealDeposit_01..03 have two slots on the same formation ISM, `rock` (MI_RockSlab) and `crystal` (MI_HealDepositCrystal); every slot reads the same per-instance custom data. M_RockSlab ([[bp-rock-slab-material]]) reads it with the rock piece layout: PerInstanceCustomData3Vector index 4 plus scalar 7 = shake of quadrants 0-3, HLSL `RockWPO`: `on = s >= 1.0`, phase (s - 1) 2 pi, offset (8 sin, 8 sin, 3 sin) uu at 8 Hz, blended by world position (quadrant 1 = +X, -Y side). Task-257-02 gave the heal formation ISMs 6 floats and wrote the pulse rate (0.8 to 1.2) at index 5 = quadrant 1 shake, so every heal deposit with rate >= 1 shook its +X side forever (the crystal slot has no WPO and stayed still; the C++ dig shake moves the whole instance, not part of it). Read back headless (graph of M_RockSlab and M_DepositCrystalGlow, mesh slots) and counted in `RogueRigs.PulseDesync.SeededValues` (numbers in [[sys-tests-pools-pickups]]). Fix (C++): `DepositMeshHasRockSlot` (a slot other than `DepositCrystalSlotName`) and `CanWriteDepositPulseData` (crystal-only mesh, or both pulse indices at or above `PieceCustomDataCount` 13); `BuildDepositCrystals` gives a rock-slot ISM max(mark + 1, colour + 3) = 4 floats and no pulse write (`FRRGridDepositVisual::bPulseData` false), so the rock reads its defaults 0 at 4-7 (still, as before task-257) and the crystal its neutral pulse (phase 0, rate 1, plus the world position term of the material). Duskite formations (crystal slot only) keep 6 floats and the hashed pulse. Cost: the heal crystals lost their hashed phase and rate (they still differ by the position term). To restore it (bp subtask, not done): make M_DepositCrystalGlow read the pulse at indices the rock never reads (PerInstanceCustomData_0 DataIndex 4 -> 13, PerInstanceCustomData_1 DataIndex 5 -> 14), then set `PulsePhaseCustomDataIndex` / `PulseRateCustomDataIndex` to 13 / 14 for the deposits (needs separate deposit indices in C++, as the per-cell clusters and pickups using M_XPNutGlow keep 4 / 5); `CanWriteDepositPulseData` then turns the heal pulse back on by itself. Side note: M_RockSlab also reads index 0 (the mark) as quadrant 0 damage, so a marked heal deposit would show cracks on that quadrant (heal veins come from the layout; not checked whether a console can mark them).
