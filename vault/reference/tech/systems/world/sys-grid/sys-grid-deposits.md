# Grid — deposits

- deposits (task-070-01, [[ore-blocks]]): `FRROreTypeRow` (`Grid/RROreTypeRow.h`), one row per ore type in `Content/RogueRigs/Data/Tables/OreTypes.csv` (row name = ore id; columns `CellType`, `RockShare`, `GroupSize`, `ChunkValue`, `HealValue`, `Color`), read through `FRRBaseStatsLoader`; `Duskite` (renamed from Gold, task-100-01): Ore, 0.08, 2, 1, 0, colour alpha 0 (no colour written, the crystals keep the violet of their material; name provisional); `Heal` (task-088-02, [[heal-ore]]): HealOre, 0 (never placed: heal veins come from the layout), 2, 0, 15 HP per pickup, colour alpha 0 (no colour written, the heal material keeps its own). `BuildDeposits` runs after `LoadLayout`: layout Ore and HealOre cells become one deposit per 4-connected group, taking the first row (by id) of their block type (`arena_m1_fine`: 12 heal veins of 2 × 2, 48 cells, row `Heal`), then, when `bPlaceOreDeposits` (true), each row with a share places round(share × layout rock / size²) squares of GroupSize × GroupSize rock blocks, turned into the row CellType: anchors scored by a fixed integer hash of (x, y, `OreSeed` (1), CRC of the ore id), taken greedily while their square is still all rock, so only rock is converted (never heal ore, barrels, floor) and deposits may touch; no start-zone protection. Logged per type (`359 Duskite deposits of 2 x 2` on `arena_m1_fine`, 17 964 rock blocks after task-088-01; 44 on `arena_m1`). `FRRGridDeposit` (OreId, CellType, Cells, ChunkValue, HealValue, Color, `bHasColor`, `bPlacedFromTable`), `Deposits` (id = index), `CellDepositId` (cleared when a block breaks). Colour: block types of deposits with a row whose colour alpha is above 0 get custom data floats `OreColorCustomDataIndex` (1) to +2 = R, G, B on every instance, for the material (task-070-02). Consoles mark table deposit blocks like any rock inside their zone (`ARRConsole::FindMarkableCells`, [[sys-objectives]]; skipped before task-120-01).
- whole on explosion (task-187-01, [[ore-blocks]] owner 2026-10-15): `DestroyCell` with an explosion source (`IsExplosionSource`: Barrel, Kamikaze; every explosion row, the barrel queue and `DestroyCellsInRadius` / `QueueDestroyCellsInRadius` use them) on a deposit block reads the deposit's standing blocks by id (`GetDepositCells`, before the cell leaves the deposit), destroys the block, then the others in the same call through the protected `DestroySingleCell` (no re-expansion: no recursion; a block already broken, e.g. still in the explosion queue, is skipped), the remaining deposit pickups popping at once (loot over the dig below), unmark and `OnCellDestroyed` (console marked rocks counted per block; flow field and minimap update from those events as for any destruction; the flow field collects the opened cells and repairs them in its next budgeted pass). Duskite and heal veins alike; touching deposits stay separate (by id). Drone and Shot destroy one block (the drone mines whole deposits itself). `DestroyCellsInRadius` counts every block broken this way (`DestroyedBlockCount`). Tests: `RogueRigs.Dig.FineGridDefaults` and `RogueRigs.BarrelFuse.Radius700FineGridAndCost` expectations include touched deposits; `RogueRigs.Ore.ExplosionDrops` unchanged (4 blocks, 4 chunks). Verbose log `deposit broken whole from (x, y), N block(s)`. Measured (-game, `rr.Stress.Run 10 2 4 1 chain`, Barrel, `arena_m1_fine`): deposit 161 with 2 of its 4 cells inside the 700 uu radius broke whole, 4 blocks, 4 chunks requested; 15 other deposits 4 blocks / 4 chunks each.
- loot over the dig (task-235-01, [[ore-blocks]], [[heal-ore]], owner 2026-10-19): a deposit holds N = blocks x `DestroyDropCount` pickups (`GetDepositPickupCount`, 4 for a 2 x 2, Duskite and heal alike) and keeps two runtime counters on `FRRGridDeposit`: `PoppedPickups` and `BrokenBlocks`. `ApplyTerrainDamage` on a deposit block computes the block dig progress p = 1 - health / max health (the drone gives every block of the deposit the same damage, so any block is the deposit progress) and pops up to floor(p x N + 0.001) through the protected `PopDepositPickups`, the destroying hit included (p = 1 pops the last one, so a dug deposit owes nothing at its break): 2 s dig at mining speed 1 (task-259-01; was 1.5 s, one every 0.375 s) = one pickup every 0.5 s, mining speed scales it (damage per tick = DeltaSeconds x MiningSpeed). Health is never restored, so a stopped dig keeps its progress and count and resumes. `DestroySingleCell` on a deposit block increments `BrokenBlocks`, then pops up to N for an explosion source (the remaining pickups at once) or up to BrokenBlocks x `DestroyDropCount` otherwise (a block destroyed outright, e.g. a stress or debug `DestroyCell` with source Drone, owes its own share). Pickup k pops from the centre of its own block `Cells[k / DestroyDropCount]` (not the deposit centre), as the same `SpawnPickups` request as before (same pop motion, value `ChunkValue`, heal `HealValue`; heal refusal at full health unchanged, [[sys-pickups-lifecycle]]). Log line per dig pop (`LogRogueRigs` Log): `deposit <id> (<ore>) pickup k/N popped at <world s> s, progress <p> (dig) from (x, y)`; explosion and break pops are Verbose. Blocks outside any deposit keep the former rule (one pickup per multiple of `DropInterval` crossed while standing, `DestroyDropCount` at destruction). Requests go to `URRPickupSubsystem::RequestSpawn` (pool, spread over `SpawnMaxFrames`); rule summary in [[sys-pickups]]. Tests: `RogueRigs.Dig.HealOreDrops` (one heal vein block dug frame by frame: 4 pops at frames 30, 60, 90, 120 within +- 1 of 30 k (task-259-01; were 23, 45, 68, 90 at 1.5 s), the last on the destroying hit, the 3 other blocks then blown up pop nothing), `RogueRigs.Ore.MineDeposit` and `RogueRigs.Ore.HealVein` (drone dig: 4 pops 21-24 frames apart, the last on the break frame), `RogueRigs.Ore.ExplosionDrops` unchanged (4 and 12 at once). `Ore.HealVein` kept its pre-existing failure (3 of 4 heal pickups collected, 55 HP instead of 70: a pickup rests outside the pickup radius; same messages before this task), its new timing checks pass. Task-250-01: the pop from the pickup's own block (up to 2 cells from a miner digging the vein) plus the 80-300 uu pop lets a pickup settle beyond the 300 uu pickup radius of a still miner; it stays at rest and is collected once the miner comes near (heal-ore rule, no game change); the test now walks the miner to it ([[sys-tests-dig-ore]]). Measured (-game, `L_ArenaM1`, `BugItGo` next to the vein then `Walk`, mining speed 1): heal vein 7 pops at 5.309, 5.689, 6.058, 6.441 s (gaps 0.380, 0.369, 0.383 s); Duskite deposit 13 at 12.308, 12.684, 13.060, 13.433 s (gaps 0.376, 0.376, 0.373 s); each the last on the break. - pulse frozen (task-267-01, owner 2026-10-20 "les cristaux ne pulsent plus"). Cause: M_XPNutGlow (task-257-01) and its copy M_DepositCrystalGlow compute the sine argument Time x (PulseFrequency x (PerInstance 5 x PulseRate)) + position term + phase, with `PulseRate` a scalar parameter with Use Custom Primitive Data, index 2 (default 1). The engine never feeds that default to the GPU: `UMaterialExpressionScalarParameter::Compile` (Engine/Private/Materials/MaterialExpressions.cpp 8427-8434) emits `Compiler->CustomPrimitiveData(PrimitiveDataIndex, MCT_Float)` and ignores `DefaultValue` (same in the new translator, MaterialExpressionsToMIR.cpp 537-542); `FPrimitiveUniformShaderParametersBuilder::CustomPrimitiveData` (Engine/Public/PrimitiveUniformShaderParametersBuilder.h 252-272) copies the component floats or `Memzero`s the 36 floats when the array is empty, and `SetPrimitiveData` (PrimitiveComponent.cpp 2664-2667) pads a shorter array with `SetNumZeroed`; GPU Scene packs all of them (PrimitiveUniformShaderParameters.cpp 199-203). So a slot nobody writes reads 0, the rate is 0, Time drops out and the emission holds a constant 1 + PulseAmount x sin(position + phase). Measured headless (`PulseDesync.SeededValues`, `GetCustomPrimitiveDataIndexForScalarParameter("PulseRate")` = 2, material default 1.0000): Duskite and heal deposit ISMs 0 floats, GPU value at slot 2 = 0.0000; BP_PickupXP mesh 1 float (glow fade at 0), slot 2 = 0.0000 (XP nut and XP cube cores frozen too, [[bp-pickup-xp]]); ore pickup 3 floats, slot 2 = 0.8972 (pulses). The per-instance side is fine: `GetPerInstanceCustomData` returns the node default when the index is at or beyond the instance float count (MaterialTemplate.ush 1501-1513). Not the cause: GlowAmount 2 and PulseAmount 0.5 of task-262 still swing the emission between 0.5x and 1.5x (3 to 1). Fix: C++ here (13 / 14), material side in bp subtask 267-02 (M_DepositCrystalGlow reads 13 / 14 and drops its Custom Primitive Data parameters, [[bp-deposit-crystal-material]]) and the M_XPNutGlow If fix in [[bp-pickup-xp]]; both done in task-267-02.
- heal deposit self-vibration (task-258-01, owner 2026-10-20: the upper part of a heal deposit shook untouched, also in the editor viewport). Cause: SM_HealDeposit_01..03 have two slots on the same formation ISM, `rock` (MI_RockSlab) and `crystal` (MI_HealDepositCrystal); every slot reads the same per-instance custom data. M_RockSlab ([[bp-rock-slab-material]]) reads it with the rock piece layout: PerInstanceCustomData3Vector index 4 plus scalar 7 = shake of quadrants 0-3, HLSL `RockWPO`: `on = s >= 1.0`, phase (s - 1) 2 pi, offset (8 sin, 8 sin, 3 sin) uu at 8 Hz, blended by world position (quadrant 1 = +X, -Y side). Task-257-02 gave the heal formation ISMs 6 floats and wrote the pulse rate (0.8 to 1.2) at index 5 = quadrant 1 shake, so every heal deposit with rate >= 1 shook its +X side forever (the crystal slot has no WPO and stayed still; the C++ dig shake moves the whole instance, not part of it). Read back headless (graph of M_RockSlab and M_DepositCrystalGlow, mesh slots) and counted in `RogueRigs.PulseDesync.SeededValues` (numbers in [[sys-tests-pools-pickups]]). Fix (C++): `DepositMeshHasRockSlot` (a slot other than `DepositCrystalSlotName`) and `CanWriteDepositPulseData` (crystal-only mesh, or both deposit pulse indices at or above `PieceCustomDataCount` 13); with indices below 13 `BuildDepositCrystals` gives a rock-slot ISM max(mark + 1, colour + 3) = 4 floats and no pulse write (`FRRGridDepositVisual::bPulseData` false). Task-258-01 kept 4 / 5, so the heal crystals lost their hashed phase and rate; task-267-01 moved the deposit pulse to 13 / 14 (bullets above), which gave it back to the heal ISMs (15 floats, 0 at 4-12). Side note: M_RockSlab also reads index 0 (the mark) as quadrant 0 damage, so a marked heal deposit would show cracks on that quadrant (heal veins come from the layout; not checked whether a console can mark them).
- whole under the extractor (task-372-01, de889932, 0e85c9a0): source `Extractor` also breaks a touched deposit whole (`BreaksDepositWhole` = explosion sources or Extractor; ore and heal vein alike). Its pickups (the deposit's owed total) are requested with `bAtRest`: each appears at rest on the floor of its own block (`ARRPickup::SetSpawnAtRest`, no pop arc), pickable, and nothing is lost. An ore or heal block outside any deposit leaves its `DestroyDropCount` pickups at rest the same way. Pop cause logged `extractor`. Entry point: [[sys-grid-api]].
