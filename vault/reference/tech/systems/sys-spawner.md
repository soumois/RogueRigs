# Enemy spawner

- responsibility: keep robots coming from outside the screen (M1 rule in [[enemies-index]]); count the robots it spawned that are alive.
- main_classes: `ARREnemySpawner` (`Source/RogueRigs/Enemies/RREnemySpawner.h/.cpp`, Blueprintable placed actor, ticks).
  - pacing: run clock `GetRunTime` = seconds of enabled spawning. Default: the wave director ([[sys-spawner-director]], `DirectorConfig.bEnabled`). Older timers when it is off: trickle (run time < `TrickleDuration`): one `TrySpawnEnemy` every `SpawnInterval`. Pack phase (`IsInPackPhase`): one `TrySpawnPack` as the phase starts, then every `PackInterval`; between packs, every `SpawnInterval` (timer reset by each pack), a lone `TrySpawnEnemy` with chance `LoneSpawnChance` (defaults: two rolls per gap, about one lone robot every 5 gaps).
  - pack: one candidate scan; size drawn in [`PackSizeMin`, `PackSizeMax`], clamped to the free slots (`MaxAlive` − alive); candidates split by side of the view rectangle (`GetSideOfRectangle`: axis of the larger gap, X on a tie); side drawn uniformly among sides with at least that many cells, else the side with the most; distinct random cells of that side.
  - class: `PickEnemyClass`, weighted draw among `WeightedEnemyClasses` entries with a class and a weight > 0; `EnemyClass` when none is valid. Per-class cap (task-022-01): an entry whose `MaxAlive` > 0 is left out of the draw while that many robots of its class (IsA) from this spawner are alive (`GetAliveCountOfClass`), its weight going to the others; every valid entry capped = no spawn (no `EnemyClass` fallback). Every draw uses the spawner `FRandomStream` (new seed in BeginPlay; `SeedRandomStream` for tests).
  - cap: `MaxAlive` 150 (owner 2026-10-05; was 100); nothing spawns at `GetAliveCount() >= MaxAlive`; timers still run. Alive = valid, not being destroyed, not `IsDead` (pruned on query: a kill frees the slot at once).
  - candidate cell (task-031-02): walkable and reachable in the flow field ([[sys-flow-field]] `IsWalkableCell`, `GetCellDistance`), no pawn's actor location in it, center at a box distance from the view rectangle between `SpawnBandMinDistance` and `SpawnBandMaxDistance` uu; band empty: the nearest band of the same width beyond it (near edge within `SpawnBandFallbackMaxDistance`). Detour filter: cells whose walk to the miner (flow steps × cell size) exceeds `MaxPathDetourRatio` × straight distance are dropped while others remain (a robot behind rock took 7.35 s to show; now 2.25 s). Spawn at the cell center, capsule bottom `SpawnFloorClearance` above the floor top (grid actor Z), AdjustIfPossibleButAlwaysSpawn.
- run heading (task-031-02): miner (flow-field goal) location sampled every tick; heading = movement over `HeadingWindowSeconds`; below `HeadingMinSpeed` = still. While running, each lone robot, director pack and horde side draws a sector around the miner (`ERRSpawnSector` Front within `FrontHalfAngle` of the heading, Back within `BackHalfAngle` of its reverse, Side between; weights `FrontSectorWeight` / `SideSectorWeight` / `BackSectorWeight`, empty sectors left out), then spawns there: lone = random cell of the sector; pack and horde = side of a random sector cell (unused sides with room first), the pack anchored on that cell. Still: uniform as before. The older `TrySpawnPack` ignores the heading. Measured ([[sys-automation-tests]]): running, 600 lone robots 56.0 / 34.7 / 9.3 %, 150 packs 56.7 / 35.3 / 8.0 %, horde back side 4 %; still, sector shares match the cell shares.
- recycling (task-032-01, [[enemies-index]], provisional): every tick (spawning on or off) while `bRecycleEnabled`, a robot of this spawner farther than `RecycleDistance` 1800 uu (2D) from the miner and more than `RecycleOffScreenMargin` 0 uu beyond the view rectangle (box distance, i.e. off screen) for `RecycleDelay` 3 s in a row (owner fourth test, task-034-01; were 2500, 500, 4 s) is removed from the alive list and destroyed: no `HandleDeath`, so no XP, no kill count, no kamikaze explosion; its slot (MaxAlive, per-class caps, shooter cap) is free the same frame. Coming back near or within the margin resets its timer. With margin 0 a robot that spawned in the band (100–500 uu off screen) and farther than 1800 uu can be recycled before it walks in; tests that count spawns through the alive count turn recycling off (task-034-01). `OnEnemyRecycled(Enemy)` fires before the destroy; `GetRecycledCount`, `GetRecycleSeconds(Enemy)`. Measured with the former 2500 / 500 / 4 s defaults ([[sys-automation-tests]] `Spawner.RecycleKeepsSpawnsAhead`, miner running 600 uu/s on floor paths, default director): 180–210 s, 160 front-sector spawns with recycling vs 0 without (cap 150 full of robots left behind); 707 recycled in 210 s.
- view rectangle (world XY bounding box of the four frustum corner rays projected on the floor plane; rays above the horizon clamped to a 0.05 downward slope), first source available: `SetViewRectangleOverride` (tests; `ClearViewRectangleOverride`); the first player controller's camera manager (`GetCameraCacheView`, viewport aspect ratio); the first `UCameraComponent` of the flow-field goal actor (headless worlds). Miner camera on cell (40, 40) of the 200 uu grid: X [7217, 10223], Y [5312, 10888] (same world point as cell (121, 121) at 67 uu).
- editable (`EditAnywhere`): `EnemyClass` (none), `WeightedEnemyClasses` (`FRRWeightedEnemyClass` {`EnemyClass`, `Weight` 1, `MaxAlive` 0 = no cap}, empty in C++; target mix with the heavy: chaser 65, kamikaze 12, shooter 15, heavy 8 with `MaxAlive` 5, to be set on [[bp-enemy-spawner]] by task-022-02; before it chaser 70, kamikaze 15, shooter 15 (task-021-02)), `SpawnInterval` 2 s, `TrickleDuration` 30 s, `PackInterval` 6 s, `PackSizeMin` 8, `PackSizeMax` 15, `LoneSpawnChance` 0.1 per roll (chosen in task-014-01; the gdd says "rare"; these five only without the director), `MaxAlive` 150, `DirectorConfig` ([[sys-spawner-director]]), `SpawnBandMinDistance` 100, `SpawnBandMaxDistance` 500 (owner 2026-10-06; replace `ViewMargin` 3 / `MaxSpawnDistanceCells` 18, 234–1240 uu; no Blueprint or map override existed), `SpawnBandFallbackMaxDistance` 3000, `MaxPathDetourRatio` 1.5 (approved provisional by the owner), `HeadingWindowSeconds` 1, `HeadingMinSpeed` 150 uu/s (miner walks 600), `FrontSectorWeight` 55, `SideSectorWeight` 35, `BackSectorWeight` 10, `FrontHalfAngle` 45, `BackHalfAngle` 45, `bRecycleEnabled` true, `RecycleDistance` 1800, `RecycleDelay` 3 s, `RecycleOffScreenMargin` 0, `bEnabled` true, `SpawnFloorClearance` 2 uu. Provisional ([[enemies-index]]).
- entry_points: `TrySpawnEnemy`, `TrySpawnPack` (ignore timers and enabled flags), `PickEnemyClass(const FRandomStream&)`, `PickEnemyClass()` (spawner stream), `GetAliveCountOfClass`, `SeedRandomStream`, `GetRunTime`, `IsInPackPhase`, `GetSpawnableClasses` (used by [[sys-stress]]), `GetAliveCount`, `GetAliveEnemies`, `IsSpawningEnabled`, `SetSpawningEnabled`, `GetViewRectangle`, `GetSpawnCandidates`, static `ProjectViewOnFloor`, `ComputeCameraViewRectangle(World, FloorZ, FallbackCameraOwner)` (the camera part of the view rectangle without override; also the shooter's on-screen test, [[sys-enemies-shooter]]), `GetDistanceToRectangle`, `GetSideOfRectangle` (`ERRSpawnSide`), static `GetSectorOfPoint`, `GetRunHeading`, `GetRunSpeed`, `GetSpawnSector`, `SetHeadingOverride` / `ClearHeadingOverride` (tests). Director API and delegates: [[sys-spawner-director]]. Console: `rr.Spawner.Enabled 0/1` (cheat).
- data: none (tunables on the actor). Threat-driven spawn rate: open ([[difficulty]]).
- depends_on: [[sys-flow-field]], [[sys-grid]], [[sys-enemies]], [[sys-player]] (camera).
- implements: [[enemies-index]] M1 spawn rule.
- gotchas: one full-grid scan (240 × 240, 1.13 ms measured in [[stress-grid-cells]]) per single spawn or per pack. A side with fewer cells than the pack (arena edge) gives a smaller pack; other sides do not fill it. The bounding box of the trapezoid is wider than the near screen edge. Pawn occupancy uses actor locations only. No candidate logs Verbose and skips. Tests: [[sys-automation-tests]].
