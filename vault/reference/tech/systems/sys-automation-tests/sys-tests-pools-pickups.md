# Automation tests — pools pickups

Part of [[sys-automation-tests]] (harness, command and gotchas live there).

- `RogueRigs.Pickups.Heal*` (task-089-01, `RRPickupHealTests.cpp`, [[heal-ore]]; `BP_PickupHeal` worth 15 spawned deferred at the feet of `BP_Miner` on (40, 40), possessed, weapons stopped, MaxHealth 100): `HealFullHealthWaits` (100 HP: after 240 frames an XP cube is collected, the heal pickup is never magnetized, not collected, alive, in Rest at its rest point, health 100; then health set to 70: collected, health 85); `HealPartialClamp` (95 HP, two heal pickups latching together: one collected, health 100, the other refused in flight, alive, in Rest at its rest height).
- `RogueRigs.ActorPool.*` (task-090-01, `RRActorPoolTests.cpp`, bare test world, `ARRTestPooledActor`, [[sys-actor-pool]]): `ReuseReleased` (released actor parked and `IsPooledIdle`, reused at once at the new transform, double and foreign release refused, an actor in use never handed out twice); `ResetHooks` (OnInit before BeginPlay, OnPoolAcquired before OnReady, once per delivery; OnPoolReleased once per release; BeginPlay once); `SpreadRespectsMaxFrames` (30 missing, MaxFrames 3: at most 10 per tick, all within 3 ticks, CreatedThisFrame matches; 4 MaxFrames-1 requests over 20 relaxed ones served alone next tick); `CancelRequest` (cancelled and dead-requester requests never delivered); `Counters` (prewarm 4 over 2 ticks, reuse, destroyed member leaves, ResetStats, per-class stats); `Teardown` (ClearPool destroys idle, forgets acquired, no callback after; world end with pending requests).
- `RogueRigs.PoolPrewarm.*` (task-111-01, `RRPoolPrewarmTests.cpp`, [[sys-actor-pool]], [[sys-spawner]]; `FTestWorld` sets `rr.Pool.Prewarm` 0 and restores it, so spawners of other tests never prewarm and their pool counters stay exact): `PoolSpread` (`Prewarm` 30/20, 5/60, 3/60, 60/60, 130/60 alone: at most ceil(Count / MaxFrames) per tick, spread over exactly MaxFrames ticks; two 30/20 batches together: at most 2 each per tick, 20 ticks); `SpawnerOrders` (fine grid, native spawner, default director, heavy entry cap 3, `ARRTestPickupXP` set on the chaser class defaults: chaser 30, heavy 3, kamikaze, shield chaser, shooter 5, XP 30; all queued together: each class at most ceil(Count / MaxFrames) per tick, over 60 ticks, all idle, none acquired, spawner alive 0, pending 0; the first `TrySpawnEnemyOfClass` chaser is delivered at once by reuse, nothing created, 29 idle left); `AbsentClass` (director off: heavy weight 0 = 0, kamikaze unlisted = 0, shooter cap 2 = 2, chaser 30; director on without heavy packs and horde heavies: heavy 0 even with an entry weight 8); `BlueprintSpawner` (`BP_EnemySpawner` class defaults: BP_Chaser 30, BP_Kamikaze 5, BP_Shooter 5, BP_Heavy 3, BP_ShieldChaser 5, BP_Chaser XP class 30); `StartDelay` (`rr.Pool.Prewarm` 1: nothing at BeginPlay nor on the first tick, queued and creating on the second).
- `RogueRigs.RobotPool.*` (task-091-01, `RRRobotPoolTests.cpp`, fine grid, invulnerable unarmed miner, native robots, recycling off, default `SpawnMaxFrames` 20, [[sys-spawner]]): `RecycledRobotIsFresh` (chaser, kamikaze, shield chaser: a robot dirtied (knockback, far movement, shield broken, i-frames effect left on it after death) is killed, parked (dead, hidden, scale 1, not alive for the spawner) and handed back at once by the next spawn: same actor, life 2, health, max health, shield, move speed, walk speed, attack power, no active effect, alive, shown, collision, scale 1, no velocity, no pending launch, far movement off, moving; chaser in Chase with 0 attacks and 1 robot in the separation, then walks and dies again; kamikaze in Chase, not detonated, kill fuse cleared, hit capsule back to `QueryOnly`, explosion source forgotten and queued again 0.65 s after its next death (kill fuse, task-096-01); shield back to full, break count 0); `PendingRobotsCountAgainstMaxAlive` (cap 10, empty pool: a pack and 20 lone tries fill exactly 10 slots with nothing alive yet; a wave at the cap requests nothing; over 25 ticks alive + pending never above 10, cap reached); `WaveCreatedWithin20Frames` (17 queued robots: at most 1 created per tick, all delivered within 20 ticks, longest wait at most 20; killed and released, the same pack comes back at once with nothing created).
