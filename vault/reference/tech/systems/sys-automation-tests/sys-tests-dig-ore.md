# Automation tests — dig ore

Part of [[sys-automation-tests]] (harness, command and gotchas live there).

- `RogueRigs.Dig.RockDestroy`, `HealOreDrops`, `DestroyCellsInRadius`, `PickupCollect`, `FineGridDefaults`: terrain damage and destruction times, ore drops, radius destruction, game grid defaults, pickup collection (miner at 50 / 100 HP since task-089-01; not before pop + `AttractDelay`, read by reflection; collected after) ([[sys-grid]], [[sys-drone]], [[sys-pickups]]). On the 67 uu grid (task-029-04): `RockDestroy` (rock (129, 115) 0.5 s, frames 29–31), `HealOreDrops` (heal ore (148, 136) 1.5 s, no dig drop, 1 pickup), `FineGridDefaults` (unmodified `BP_ArenaGrid`: 67 uu, 240 × 240, spawn (121, 121), 48 heal ore cells (12 veins of 2 × 2 since task-088-01), `DestroyDropCount` 1; `Barrel` 700 uu (task-114-01; was 350) and `Kamikaze` 312 uu radii, 72 blocks within 700 uu of (129, 115) all destroyed). The instance-map check also requires each block collision ISM to hold the same instances, in the same order, on their cells; since task-029-01 it runs per chunk visual (each instance in its chunk, no component navigation relevant).
- Arc mining (task-029-05, game grid, `BP_Miner` on (128, 115) against the rock wall x = 129, weapons stopped, no input; [[sys-drone]], [[sys-player]]): `Dig.ArcContactDig` (touching is enough: after one tick Dig on exactly (129, 115) first, (129, 114), (129, 116), (130, 115), arc +X, 4 shaking, 4 start events, each block −1/60 s on the first frame; laser on; laser fan at frames 2 and 25: `LaserMesh` is beam 0, visible beams = dug cells, beam i from the drone to `GetDigTargetPoint` of cell i (0.5 uu); drone 0.7 uu from the arc centre at frame 25; all 4 destroyed at frame 30; next tick no contact, Orbit, no shake, laser off, every beam hidden, 4 stop events all destroyed; (129, 113) stands); `Dig.ArcLimits` (`SelectArcCells`: every pick within 1.5 cells of the front and 60°, at most 4, for 4 directions; +X the 4 cells above; −X none; +Y only (129, 116); from (126, 115) none; `MaxArcBlocks` 2 → 2, nearest first; move input (1, 1) steers the arc diagonally, (−1, 0) and length 0.05 leave it toward the wall); `Dig.ArcSpeeds` (from (148, 134) the 8-block arc holds the 4 blocks of the 2 × 2 heal vein (147–148, 135–136), destroyed at frame 90 = 1.5 s, and 4 rocks at 0.5 s; `MiningSpeed` 2: rock (129, 115) at frame 15); `Dig.ArcPersistAndRelease` (15 frames: rock health 0.25, laser fan of 4 beams; miner teleported 3 cells west: Orbit, arc cleared, no shake, laser off, every beam hidden, 4 stop events not destroyed, health unchanged for 60 frames; back: rock destroyed 15 frames later). `URRTestEventRecorder` records `ARRDrone::OnDigBlockStarted` / `OnDigBlockStopped` (cells, `bDestroyed`).
- `RogueRigs.Ore.*` (task-070-01, `RROreDepositTests.cpp`, game grid with `bPlaceOreDeposits` and `OreSeed` set before BeginPlay, `BP_PickupOre` if the grid has no ore class; [[sys-grid]], [[sys-pickups]]): `TableRow` (Duskite: Ore, 0.08, 2 × 2, value 1, alpha 0; unknown id not found, error expected); `Placement` (count = round(0.08 × plain rock / 4) = 359 on 17 964 rock, within 10 of 358, logged; every deposit a 2 × 2 Duskite square on former rock, no cell twice, heal ore and barrel cells unchanged against a build without deposits, same seed same deposits, each block mines exactly its 4, console never marks a deposit block, no deposit has a colour (task-100-01)); `MineDeposit` (miner north of a deposit, no input: the 4 blocks shake together at frame 30 and break on the same frame 89–91, 4 chunks of value 1 per broken deposit); `ExplosionDrops` (deposit nearest the spawn blown up with a 0.75-cell radius: 4 blocks, 4 chunk creations queued in the pool, delivered within 3 frames; then 3 deposits in one frame: 12 queued, created within 3 frames, at most 4 per frame (task-092-01, was the 4-per-frame queue)); `HealVein` (task-088-02, deposits off, `BP_PickupHeal`: row Heal = HealOre, share 0, 15 HP, alpha 0; 12 heal deposits, each a 2 × 2 square, 48 cells, row Heal, no colour; one block of (147–148, 135–136) reaches its 4; miner on (148, 134) at 10 / 100 HP, possessed, weapons stopped: the arc touches the vein, the 4 blocks shake together and break on frame 89–91, 4 heal pickups worth 15, all 4 collected although they pop inside the pickup radius, health 70). `Dig.DestroyCellsInRadius` ticks 4 frames before counting drops (queue); `Console.DropPlacementAndMarks` excludes deposit blocks from the expected marks.
- `RogueRigs.Dig.ChunkedInstances`: rock spread over several chunk HISMs; destroying rock (43, 38) changes exactly two chunk HISMs, both of its chunk (rock −1, floor +1) ([[sys-grid]]).
- `RogueRigs.Dig.ShakeKeepsCollision`: rock (43, 38) shaking, miner on (42, 38) with weapons stopped. The rock HISM has no collision and the collision ISM is not drawn. 30 frames: a miner-sized capsule (34 × 88) swept east on WorldStatic hits the rock's collision instance every frame at x 8566.000 (face 8600 − radius), never moving, while the rendering instance moves up to 33.2 uu and the collision instance 0. Then the miner (its drone destroyed first, so contact does not dig) walks east into the shaking rock for 60 frames: 39 frames in contact, max x 8565.90 (contact 8566), worst backward step 0; rock still standing; maps consistent after the stop ([[sys-grid]]).
- Fine grid loop (task-029-07, `RRFineGridIntegrationTests.cpp`; grid layout, cell size and transform read from the `BP_ArenaGrid` placed in `L_ArenaM1` (external actor packages), then spawned in the test world): `Integration.FineContactDigOpensPassage` (`BP_Chaser` in the pocket (118, 100) shut by rock rows y 108–109; `BP_Miner`, weapons stopped, walks from (120, 113) north up the notch (119–121, 110) by move input only: arc dig from frame 37 (largest arc 3), pocket reachable on the frame of the opening destruction (frame 115, 1.92 s; at most 3 frames lag required), 6 cells dug, all in front of the miner; miner backs out 1.5 s to (120, 120): chaser arrives within 150 uu 3.68 s after the opening, through 3 dug cells, centre never on a block cell); `Integration.FineConsoleShuttlePlacement` (placed grid is `arena_m1_fine` at 67 uu; player start (8100, 8100) on open floor within 2 cells of the layout spawn; `BP_RRGameMode` config: `BP_ObjectiveConsole`, `BP_ExtractionShuttle`, 36–60, 75, board 3; seeds 1, 20261003, 777, 4242, 90210, miner on the player start: console Blueprint at its 2 × 2 footprint centre on a reachable floor cell 2597–3506 uu away (2400–4000 ± one cell), rocks marked, zone 603 uu; shuttle Blueprint at its 4 × 3 footprint centre on a reachable floor cell 5650–6774 uu away (>= 5000 − one cell), board radius 201 uu with 23–26 reachable floor cells; asserts zero block cells under each Blueprint's bounds (console 100 × 100 uu, shuttle 240 × 160 uu; landing clearance of task-026-00, [[sys-objectives]])). `RunFlow` console placement and `Extraction` landing also assert every footprint cell is reachable floor and the actor stands at the footprint centre.
