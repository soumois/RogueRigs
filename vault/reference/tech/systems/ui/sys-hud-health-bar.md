# HUD health bar

- responsibility: drive the health bar and text of the health status panel from the miner health events (task-203-01); the shown fill glides to the real health both ways; an off-white damage chunk behind it keeps the lost health visible for a moment, then shrinks to the shown fill (task-203-02); on each hit the fill flashes towards white and fades back in about 0.1 s (task-203-03); on each hit the bar root shakes a few pixels for 0.15 s (task-203-04); on each hit the bar root grows in height then springs back to its size (task-203-05); the fill colour follows the real health left, green when full, yellow at half, red at 20 % and below (task-203-06). Vignette and GAME OVER stay in the Blueprint ([[wbp-health-status]]); the hit flash is distinct from the screen damage vignette, which is unchanged.
- main_classes: `URRHealthStatusWidget : URRHUDPanelWidget` (`Source/RogueRigs/UI/RRHealthStatusWidget.h`, Abstract), parent of WBP_HealthStatus (reparented by task-203-07). `BindWidgetOptional` `HealthBar` (UProgressBar), `DamageChunkBar` (UProgressBar, drawn behind `HealthBar`; added to WBP_HealthStatus by task-203-07) `HealthBarRoot` (UWidget holding the chunk and the bar; added by task-203-07; its render transform is owned by the class) and `HealthText` (UTextBlock): the class works on a Blueprint lacking them. `FRRHealthColorStop` (USTRUCT in the same header: `Fraction` 0..1, `Color` linear), the element of `FillColorStops`. Pure `GetShownFraction`, `GetTargetFraction`, `GetChunkFraction`, `GetFlashAlpha`, `GetCurrentFillColor`, `GetShakeOffset`, `GetPunchScaleY`, `GetBarRootTransform` (FWidgetTransform); `IsBarUpdateRunning` (C++, tests).
- entry_points: native bind `NativeBindToMiner` (new virtual of `URRHUDPanelWidget`, run by `SetMiner` just before the `BindToMiner` hook): removes its handlers from the previous miner, adds them (`AddUniqueDynamic`) to `OnHealthChanged` and `OnDamaged`, shows `GetCurrentHealth` / `GetMaxHealth` snapped (null miner: "0 / 0", fraction 0). A separate native step, not `BindToMiner_Implementation`, because a Blueprint override of the hook replaces the native implementation, so the vignette and game-over graphs keep running without a parent call. `NativeOnInitialized` (once the bound widgets exist) sets the render transform pivot of `HealthBarRoot` to `BarRootPivot` from C++, so the pivot does not depend on a Blueprint setting. `NativeConstruct` rebinds (the Blueprint Destruct unbinds every listener of the miner) and draws.
- rules: text = "round(Current) / round(Max)" at once on each `OnHealthChanged`. Target = Current / Max clamped 0..1 (0 when Max <= 0). Shown += (Target - Shown) * min(1, DrainRate * dt), snapped when the gap is under `DrainSnapThreshold`. Fill colour = `GetCurrentFillColor()` = lerp(`GetBaseFillColor()`, `FlashColor`, Flash). Colour by health: `GetBaseFillColor()` = `EvaluateFillColorStops(Target)`, the gradient of the REAL fraction (not the shown one, so the colour jumps with the hit while the fill glides): stops in any order; between the nearest stop at or below and the nearest at or above, linear lerp in linear colour space; beyond the end stops the nearest end colour (red at 20 % and below, green at 100 %); two stops at the same fraction give the lower one; no stop gives white. `ShowHealth` redraws on every `OnHealthChanged` (and at bind), so the colour changes at once even when no effect runs; it is not a per-frame effect and adds nothing to `AreBarEffectsSettled`. Hit flash: on each `OnDamaged` Flash = `FlashStrength` and the bar is drawn at once (full flash on the hit frame); each frame Flash -= `FlashDecay` * dt, floor 0 (linear, 1/9 = 0.11 s). Bind or no-world snap sets Flash = 0. Damage chunk: on each `OnDamaged` (broadcast after `OnHealthChanged`, so the chunk still holds the fraction shown before the hit) `ChunkDelayRemaining` = `ChunkDelay` (a new hit restarts it); the chunk holds while it counts down, then Chunk += (Shown - Chunk) * min(1, ChunkRate * dt), snapped under `DrainSnapThreshold`. After every step Chunk = max(Chunk, Shown): never below the shown fill, a heal past it pulls it up. Bind or no-world snap also sets Chunk = Shown, delay 0. Chunk bar: percent = Chunk, fill colour = `ChunkColor`. Hit shake: on each `OnDamaged` `ShakeTimeRemaining` = `ShakeDuration` (a new hit restarts it) and an offset is rolled at once; each frame remaining -= dt (floor 0), then X and Y are each `FMath::FRandRange(-A, A)` with A = `ShakeAmplitude` * remaining / `ShakeDuration` (linear decay, shape provisional, owner "ok" 2026-10-16); offset zero when remaining is 0 or `ShakeDuration` is 0. Bind or no-world snap clears it. Hit punch: on each `OnDamaged` scale Y = `PunchScale`, velocity 0 (a new hit restarts it), drawn at once; each frame a damped spring acc = -`PunchStiffness` * (Y - 1) - `PunchDamping` * v, semi-implicit Euler in `ceil(dt / PunchMaxSubstep)` equal substeps (at most `PunchMaxSubsteps`; a longer frame integrates only `PunchMaxSubsteps` * `PunchMaxSubstep` s), so a large dt stays stable; snapped to exactly Y = 1, v = 0 when |Y - 1| < `PunchSnapThreshold` and |v| < `PunchVelocitySnapThreshold`. Simulated (Node mirror of the code, dt 1/60 to 2 s): lowest Y 0.981 (small overshoot), within 0.008 of 1 at 0.25 s, exact snap about 0.48 s after the hit; no divergence at dt 0.5 or 2 s. Bind or no-world snap sets Y = 1, v = 0 (`SnapBarEffects`, the single end-state reset). Bar root: `ApplyBarVisuals` writes `GetBarRootTransform()` on `HealthBarRoot` every draw: the single place composing the transform (translation = shake offset, scale = (1, punch scale Y): X stays 1); identity once settled, so a render transform set on `HealthBarRoot` in the Blueprint is overwritten.
- per-frame update: no tick. A health event arms `UpdateBarEffects` with `SetTimerForNextTick` (dt = world delta seconds); it re-arms each frame until `AreBarEffectsSettled`, then stops (settled = Shown == Target, Chunk == Shown, no chunk delay left, Flash == 0, no shake time left, punch Y == 1 and velocity 0). Extension points for later effects (task-203-06): state in the class, start in `HandleDamaged` / `HandleHealthChanged`, step in `AdvanceBarEffects`, settle test in `AreBarEffectsSettled`, drawing in `ApplyBarVisuals`, bar root transform in `GetBarRootTransform` (shake translation and punch scale composed there), colour in `GetBaseFillColor` (the gradient of task-203-06; the flash lerps from it).
- data: `EditDefaultsOnly` on the Blueprint subclass, provisional (owner 2026-10-16, reference video): `DrainRate` 10 per s, `DrainSnapThreshold` 0.001, `FillColorStops` (replaces the former `FillColor` (0.9, 0.05, 0.05, 1), removed in task-203-06) = { (1, #66d26f), (0.5, #e8c547), (0.2, #e5544b) }, hex sRGB converted like `ChunkColor` (`FLinearColor(FColor(...))`), colours and stops provisional, owner "ok" 2026-10-16 (the HUD bar is not in color-code), `ChunkDelay` 0.4 s, `ChunkRate` 7 per s, `ChunkColor` #fff0e0 sRGB (`FLinearColor(FColor(0xFF, 0xF0, 0xE0))`, converted to linear; red #f25a5a and pink #ff99cc tried in the video, rejected), `FlashColor` white (1, 1, 1, 1), `FlashStrength` 1, `FlashDecay` 9 per s, `ShakeAmplitude` 4 (slate units, px at DPI scale 1), `ShakeDuration` 0.15 s, `PunchScale` 1.3 (reference video), `PunchStiffness` 400 per s², `PunchDamping` 25 per s (spring values owner "ok" 2026-10-16), `PunchMaxSubstep` 1/120 s, `PunchMaxSubsteps` 60, `PunchSnapThreshold` 0.001, `PunchVelocitySnapThreshold` 0.01 per s, `BarRootPivot` (0.5, 0.5).
- depends_on: [[sys-hud-events]] (panel base, miner events), [[sys-player]] (miner).
- implements: [[task-203-health-bar-juice]] (health bar juice, no gdd node).
- placement (task-452): the bar, its text and a riveted plate sit in HealthPanel, bottom centre just above the weapon slots ([[wbp-health-status]] layout). No C++ change: the class binds the same four widgets; shake and punch move HealthBarRoot only, inside a plate that stays still, and a punch (scale Y 1.3 on a 24-high bar) briefly overlaps the plate border by about 4 px.
- gotchas: world timers do not run while the game is paused, so a change during the card screen glides after it closes. WBP_HealthStatus is reparented (task-203-07) and its ShowHealth no longer writes the bar percent and text. Not seen on screen. The chunk only shows if `HealthBar`'s background is transparent where it is empty (Blueprint style, task-203-07); not checked on screen. The flash, the shake, the punch and the colour gradient are not checked on screen either (the gradient lerps in linear space, so the mid tones may look different from an sRGB lerp); the pivot only takes effect once `HealthBarRoot` exists (task-203-07). No automated test covers the widget yet.
