# M_RockSlab and MI_RockSlab

- asset_path: /Game/RogueRigs/Environment/Rock/M_RockSlab (material), /Game/RogueRigs/Environment/Rock/MI_RockSlab (instance with defaults). Textures: /Game/RogueRigs/Environment/Rock/T_RockTop (sRGB, colour) and T_RockCrackMask (sRGB off, TC_Masks, grayscale; brighter line = crack that appears first). Source PNGs in Art/Textures/.
- parent_class: Material (not a Blueprint). Opaque, default lit, flags `bUsedWithInstancedStaticMeshes` and `bUsedWithNanite`. Compiles with no error (task-230-09, recompile OK incl. ray tracing and Nanite permutations).
- purpose: one material for every rock piece, intact and broken ([[task-230-09-rock-slab-material]]). Reads the 13 per-instance custom data floats of [[sys-grid-rock-piece-data]] (indices 0 to 3 damage, 4 to 7 shake, 8 to 11 marked, 12 solid mask).
- marked (console rocks, task-230-09 fix): a third Custom node (RockMarked) reads custom data 8 to 11 for the pixel quadrant (same quadrant rule as the cracks); marked (above 0.5) gives EmissiveColor = white x (0.6 + 0.4 sin(2 pi 0.8 t)) x 3 over the whole quadrant surface. It reproduces the old `M_GreyBoxMarked` (parent of MI_GB_Rock before task-230-07): emissive = (sin(Time x 0.8) x 0.4 + 0.6) x PerInstanceCustomData 0 x 3, white, no outline geometry, no parameter. Not verified on screen.
- components: three Custom HLSL nodes (WPO, base colour, marked emissive), no texture-sample nodes (samples inside the HLSL, `Texture2DSample`). Gameplay logic: none, display only.
- inputs read: custom data (3Vector idx 0 and 4, scalar idx 3, 7, 12); Absolute World Position (WPO node: default; colour node: excluding all offsets, so the texture does not slide on a shaking piece); Object Position (instance origin); UV2 transformed local to world by a Transform node (block centre offset, rotation of the instance); UV3 (seed x, radius/100 y; `y > 0.0005` = broken variant, intact meshes have no UV3 so 0); vertex colour. UV4 (rim flag, block index) is NOT read: the rim comes from vertex colour R, so only UV0 to UV3 are used (Nanite limit of 4 UVs respected).
- faces (task-230-18): the FBX import of every SM_RockSlab* mesh has VertexColorImportOption Ignore (ObjectTools read of FbxStaticMeshImportData_0), so the mesh has no colour stream and VertexColor reads white (1, 1, 1) on every vertex: with the former rule every face took the side branch (flat SideColor, no top texture, no cracks, no rim: the owner symptoms of 230-16/17 that no parameter changed). Now side = abs(geometric normal z) below 0.5 (VertexNormalWS, input VN; all pieces are flat shaded, sag keeps tops above 0.8); rim needs real vertex colours (G and B below 0.5 and R as below), so with white colours there is no rim. Task-230-20 reimported the 15 meshes with VertexColorImportOption Replace ([[tool-unreal-reimport]]): render data now carries the FBX colours (R rim/top, G sides, B bottoms), so the rim rule is live; look in game not checked there. Task-230-22 (owner 2026-10-19, broken tops drew flat SideColor in the 230-19 captures, same lavender as the walls): the side test of broken variants no longer uses VN. They are drawn off Nanite, i.e. from the Nanite fallback mesh, whose interpolated normal is not reliable (cause inferred from the captures: hue of the flat squares equals SideColor, not CrackColor, so the side branch ran on tops; mesh normals in the FBX are flat, so the fallback is the suspect; not proven, no normal readback tool). Broken now use gz = abs(z) of normalize(cross(ddx(WP), ddy(WP))) (WP = unoffset world position, so no sag or shake in it), side when gz < 0.5; intact pieces keep VN. Side effect: a 1 pixel line at top/side block edges may take either branch. Old rule, kept for the rim: side = vertex colour G or B above 0.5 (SideColor, darker toward the foot by `TopParams.a`, darker with damage by `CrackParams.a`). Rim: intact R above 0.75, broken R between 0.25 and 0.75 (top colour times `TopParams.b`). Everything else is top.
- top colour: T_RockTop sampled by absolute world XY / `TileParams.r` (2000 uu), times a macro sample at `TileParams.g` (7000 uu, rotated `TileParams.a` degrees) of its mean luminance divided by `TopParams.g`, mixed by `TopParams.r`; then desaturated by `TopDesaturate` (0.25, owner 2026-10-19, was 0.55), `TopSaturation` (1.0, extra multiplier), `TopBrightness` (0.85, task-230-23, 2026-10-19, was 1.12), times `TopTint` (1, 1, 1). History: 0.55 and 1.12 answered the owner review of 230-04 (2026-10-17); the tops then read near white in the 230-19 captures. Task-230-23 estimate (linear, T_RockTop mean sRGB 0.615, 0.593, 0.691 = linear 0.335, 0.307, 0.434, lum 0.329; Contrast 2.5 about neutral at the mean, stretches highlights to about 1.8x): mean top albedo 0.28, 0.26, 0.34 at 0.85 (0.37, 0.35, 0.46 at 1.12), about 1.8x the SideColor (0.16, 0.15, 0.22), lavender hue kept (B above R by 20 %). Rim times `TopParams.b` (2.0 since task-230-24). Look in game checked in task-230-26.
- cracks: quadrant of the pixel q = (world x >= instance x) + 2 (world y >= instance y), d = damage[q]; cd = saturate(d / `CrackParams.r`) (full at 0.6); crack where mask (world XY / `TileParams.b`, 1000 uu) is above 1 - cd, edge softness `CrackParams.g`, strength `CrackParams.b`, colour `CrackColor`; none at d = 0, any d above 0.0005 shows the brightest lines.
- shake (all pieces): per quadrant value s >= 1 is active, phase = (s - 1) 2 pi, a = 2 pi f t, offset = (H sin(a + p), H sin(1.37 a + 2 p), V sin(1.71 a + 3 p)) with H, V, f = `ShakeParams` r, g, b (8, 3, 8 Hz, as `ARRArenaGrid`; task-231-01, owner 2026-10-19, were 25, 8). Draws on intact Nanite pieces and on broken pieces in game (task-231-01 captures, `rr.Debug.ShakeCell`). The 4 quadrant offsets are blended bilinearly over the piece (cell centres at its 4 corners, cell size `SagParams.b` = 67), so neighbours match on shared borders. A broken block uses the blend at its centre (rigid).
- sag (broken variants only, formula of `material_rigid_tilt` in SM_RockSlabBroken.report.json): block centre C = instance xy + rotated UV2 x 100, R = UV3.y x 100. g = D(C) x f(C): D = bilinear of the 4 quadrant damages at C; f = (1 - min(dE / 100, 1))^2 where dE = distance from C to the nearest EMPTY quadrant (bit of the solid mask at 0), measured to the quadrant's cell square (0 if C lies in it); when the piece has no empty quadrant dE = `ShakeParams.a` (50, provisional: interior damage still sags a little, f = 0.25). Hinge direction = unit vector from C toward the nearest intact solid quadrant (solid and d < 0.002); without one, away from the nearest empty quadrant; without either, a seed-random direction. Sag is 0 next to an intact quadrant because D tends to 0 there. Per block random: s = frac(UV3.x + hash(C xy)) (world-position hash combined with the stored seed, so instances differ), s2 = frac(sin(127.1 s + 311.7) 43758.5453); k = min(1, 2g); slope = max(min(`SagParams.r` g (0.7 + 0.6 s2) / (2R), 0.75) + (s - 0.5) 0.45 k, 0.02 g); hoff = 9 s2 k; per vertex u = max(dot(C + dir R - v xy, dir), 0); drop = min(hoff + u slope, `SagParams.a` - `SagParams.g`) = 100 - 60 = 40 so a block top never falls below 60 uu; z -= drop (same for the whole prism). Approximation: C++ gives only the 4 quadrant damages and the solid mask, not the distance to any empty cell or the nearest intact cell of the report.
- exposed parameters (MI_RockSlab): scalars TopDesaturate 0.25 (owner 2026-10-19), TopBrightness 0.85 (task-230-23), TopSaturation 1, TopContrast 2.5 (new, task-230-16, material default 3), Roughness 0.85; vectors (RGBA used as 4 numbers) ShakeParams (8, 3, 8, 50) (r, g set in task-231-01 by `Tools/unreal/set_material_instance_params.py`, [[tool-unreal-reimport]]; were 25, 8), SagParams (45, 60, 67, 100), TileParams (2000, 7000, 1000, 33), TopParams (0.5, 0.4, 2.0, 0.55) (b = rim brightness; 2.0 in task-230-24 (2026-10-19, was 1.3): rim albedo about (0.56, 0.52, 0.68) = 2.0x the top mean (0.28, 0.26, 0.34), no clipping at the mean, brightest top texels reach about 1.0+ so they saturate toward white (the band still reads as a step), no HLSL change; was 1.3 = 1.3x, too weak against the pale top in 230-19; history: 1.0 in task-230-16 on a wrong cause, the pale rectangle came from white vertex colours, task-230-18; restored to 1.3 in task-230-21 after the colour reimport of task-230-20; read back and saved, look in game not checked), CrackParams (0.6, 0.08, 0.9, 0.3); colours TopTint (1, 1, 1), CrackColor (0.04, 0.03, 0.07), SideColor (0.16, 0.15, 0.22); textures TopTexture, CrackMaskTexture. All colour values are provisional.
- wall_look (task-471-01, owner image 2 Art/Source/cartoon-look/reference/owner-2026-11-05-ingame-light-walls.webp, values provisional): built by Tools/unreal/rock_slab_wall_look.py ([[tool-unreal-reimport]]). A fourth Custom node RockRim (inputs WP, OP, VC, UV3, VN, RimCol, RimP) gives emissive RimColor x (RimParams.x on rim triangles that are not sides + RimParams.y x t^2 on side faces), t = saturate(1 - (RimParams.w - hz) / RimParams.z); broken pieces get half the side band. An Add node sums RockMarked and RockRim into Emissive Color. Height hz = WP.z - OP.z + RimParams.w / 2 in RockRim and RockColor: ObjectPositionWS reads the instance bounds centre, half the block height above the floor top, not the pivot (found in game: with the pivot assumption no band ever showed). RockColor sides: SideColor x lerp(TopParams.a, 1, hz / RimParams.w) x strata x crack darkening; strata = horizontal layers of StrataParams.x uu with a wobble of world XY, each layer brightness 1 + StrataParams.w x (hash - 0.5), dark lines StrataParams.y deep, StrataParams.z of a layer wide. MI_RockSlab values: TopTint (1.15, 0.78, 1.45) (lilac, was 1, 1, 1), SideColor (0.12, 0.04, 0.15) (was 0.16, 0.15, 0.22), RimColor (1, 0.2, 0.02), RimParams (1.2, 5, 12, 153) (w = BlockHeight of [[sys-grid]]; change both together), StrataParams (44, 0.45, 0.08, 0.35); parent defaults RimColor (1, 0.32, 0.04), RimParams (1, 6, 14, 153), StrataParams (34, 0.6, 0.1, 0.35). Seen in game at 1920 x 1080 (Saved/Captures/Wall471Final): lilac tops lighter than the floor, thin orange line on every lip also far from the miner, dark stratified sides; seams between sunk broken plates also show thin orange lines. Heal deposits share MI_RockSlab on their rock slot, so they take the same look. The strata and the side band height were replaced by wall_section (task-478).
- wall_section (task-478-01, owner image 3 Art/Source/cartoon-look/reference/owner-2026-11-06-wall-section-target.png, values provisional, commit 4279e647): built by Tools/unreal/rock_slab_wall_section.py ([[tool-unreal-reimport]]), run after rock_slab_wall_look.py. Sides: drawn by RockRim as emissive SideColor x streak x lerp(TopParams.a, 1, hz / RimParams.w); RockColor keeps only a lit share SideParams.x of that colour, so light, shadow and facets no longer cut the wall. Streak = soft vertical darkening of world X and Y sines, period SideParams.z uu, depth SideParams.w. New Custom RockNormal: world normal (tangent_space_normal off), side faces bent toward +Z by SideParams.y, top and rim keep VertexNormalWS. New Custom RockSpec: specular 0 on sides, 0.5 elsewhere. Top-edge glow in RockRim: distance d inside the rock to the piece outline from the solid mask (custom data 12) and rel = WP.xy minus the nearest cell corner (grid of EdgeParams.w = 67 uu, origin 0): straight line through the centre for two adjacent solid quadrants, quarter circle of radius 33.5 around the lone solid or the empty corners otherwise (outline wobble of rock_slab_pieces.py, up to about 6 uu, ignored); glow = EdgeParams.z x (1 - smoothstep(EdgeParams.x, EdgeParams.x + EdgeParams.y, d))^2, half on broken pieces, max with the rim-triangle glow, continuous across pieces. - uv3_trap (task-480 diagnostic, Saved/Captures/Wall480Diag): UV3.y above 0.0005 is true on intact pieces, which have 2 UV channels: emissive showing that flag drew every intact piece as broken. RockWPO was not changed and not checked: its sag branch may run on intact pieces, with no visible effect at zero damage.
