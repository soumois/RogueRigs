# WBP_ShopCard

- asset_path: /Game/RogueRigs/UI/WBP_ShopCard
- parent_class: UUserWidget (/Script/UMG.UserWidget). Not a HUD panel: placed five times by [[wbp-shop-screen]] (task-316-03, [[sys-run-shop]]).
- purpose: one shop slot: a stat card with the look of a [[wbp-upgrade-choice]] stat card (baked frame, tier tint, stat icon, value, name, target, lore), a price row (T_Icon_Ore + price) and a Buy button. Display only, no deal, suspense, flip or fly animation. Five share one row, each filling its Fill 1 slot of the screen's CardsRow (was a fixed 330 px).
- components: VerticalBox Root > CardHost (SizeBox without size override since task-356-02, slot Fill 1, HAlign / VAlign Fill) > CardScale (ScaleBox, ScaleToFit) > CardFace (SizeBox 500 x 720 = the UpgradeChoice card stack, so every inner value is copied from there; the card scales to fit its slot, 0.653 at 1920 x 1080) > CardStack (Overlay): CardPanel (Image, RoundedBox #15171c, padding 14 24 14 25), FrameGroup (Overlay of FrameBase / FrameAccents / FrameGlow, the baked textures T_CardFrame_Stat_Baked, _Accents_Baked, _Glow_Baked, ImageSize 500 x 720, Glow RenderOpacity 0.6; FrameGlow Collapsed since task-355-01, so the soft tier glow is gone on every card and FrameAccents keeps the tier tint), CardBox (VerticalBox, padding 38 58 38 60): ImageArea (SizeBox 408 x 320) > ImageBg (Border, rounded 4) > StatIconBox (SizeBox 156 x 156) > StatIcon; EffectScale (ScaleBox DownOnly, top 18) > EffectRow = CardValue (Roboto Bold 30, tier colour) + CardName (Bold 30 white); CardTarget (Bold 20, grey); CardDesc (Italic 16 lore, Collapsed when empty, Fill slot, bottom aligned); SoldPanel (Border, brush T_Shop_SoldPanel since task-348-06, Collapsed) > SoldBox (VerticalBox) = SoldText (Bold 48) + SoldIcon (Image T_Shop_IconSold 160 x 160) + SoldSub (Bold 24 grey, text "NO REFUNDS.", variable); TierTab (Overlay, variable, in FrameGroup; task-348-06) = TierTabImg (T_Shop_TierTab 256 x 66) + TierTabText (Roboto Bold 20 since task-355-01, was 26; slot centred, no padding); NameDivider (Image, 2 px, grey (0.45, 0.47, 0.52, 0.8), under the name row); DimShade (Image, black alpha 0.55, Collapsed, same padding as CardPanel). Below the card: PriceRow (HorizontalBox, centred, top 10, auto height) = OreIcon (Image 40 x 40, T_Icon_Ore) + PriceText (Bold 36 white); BuyHost (Overlay, vbox slot padding top 8, centred, auto height; task-343-01) > BuyPlateBox (SizeBox, not a variable, HeightOverride 68, overlay slot Fill/Fill; task-353-01) > BuyButton (still a variable, for OnClicked; task-348-06 style: all 4 states T_Shop_ButtonBuy, Box, ImageSize 256 x 68, margin L/R 0.152, the four state brushes identical; tints normal 1.0, hover 1.35, pressed 0.6, disabled (0.35, 0.37, 0.40)) > BuyTextBox (SizeBox, not a variable, MinDesiredHeight 34; task-353-01) > BuyText (Bold 28 when BUY, 16 when NEED; MinDesiredWidth 230, padding 30/17), with BuyFlash (Image white, Fill/Fill, HitTestInvisible, render opacity 0) over it; SparkHost (CanvasPanel, HitTestInvisible) holding Spark0..Spark7 (Image 10 x 10, RoundedBox radius 5, canvas anchor 0.5/0.5, alignment 0.5/0.5, opacity 0). BurnOverlay (Image, last child of CardStack, so it covers the card; task-344-01). Only the weapon-card variant of UpgradeChoice is absent: the shop sells stat cards only. All named widgets above are variables.
- anchor_rule (task-356-02, rule [[umg-layout]]): the card is sized only by its slot. CardHost has no width / height override and is Fill 1 in Root, alignment Fill / Fill; PriceRow and BuyHost are auto height. CardScale stays ScaleToFit, both axes, ratio from CardFace 500 x 720, so the card keeps its ratio and is centred in the slot. In [[wbp-shop-screen]] the slot is one of five Fill 1 cells (padding L/R 8) of the stretched ShopPanel (anchors (0.013, 0.13)-(0.987, 0.786)). Sold panel (task-355-01, commit 9c59e766): SoldPanel was already Fill / Fill in the CardStack overlay (its brush ImageSize 450 x 600 does not size a Border), so it follows the slot; the padding of the CardStack slot changed from L14 / T24 / R14 / B25 to L24 / T34 / R24 / B35 to sit inside the frame opening (an estimate, not measured against the mockup). Other card internals untouched.
- exposed_defaults: SlotIndex (int, instance editable, default 0): the shop slot this card shows (0 to ShopCardCount - 1). BuyLabel "BUY", SoldLabel "SOLD", ShortLabel "NEED {n} MORE" (task-348-06; the earlier "Short on ore" override on ShopCard0..4 became "Short on Duskite" in [[wbp-shop-screen]], task-396-01; were "Buy", "Sold. No refunds.", "Short on ore") (Text variables, English defaults, replaced at Construct by LoadLabels).
- events (parent contract, important): the card is self-driving, the parent needs no cross-Blueprint call. Construct: LoadLabels, RefreshFromShop, assigns BuyButton OnClicked (event OnClicked_Event_3) and binds the shop's OnShopChanged to custom event ShopChangedEv (the delegate pin was wired by hand with connect_pins, the DSL left it open); Destruct unbinds only this card's own ShopChangedEv from OnShopChanged (Unbind Event node, delegate pin wired by hand to the same event as the bind), so the C++ widget and the other cards keep their bindings. ShopChangedEv calls RefreshUnlessHolding (task-342-02; Construct still calls RefreshFromShop directly). Hold for the ore flight: variable bHolding (bool, false); custom event OnSlotBought_Event(BoughtSlot) bound in Construct to the shop's OnSlotBought (assigned after the OnShopChanged bind) and unbound in Destruct, calls HoldIfBought. While bHolding the card ignores OnShopChanged, so it stays visible until the screen's flight ends and calls EndPurchaseFx. The click event calls the C++ BuySlot(SlotIndex) on `URRIntermissionShopSubsystem` then the dispatcher OnBuyClicked(SlotIndex int). So the parent only has to put five instances in its tree and set SlotIndex 0..4 on each (variable value on the placed widget), and may ignore the dispatcher (it exists for effects such as a purchase sound; binding it from WBP_Intermission is untested).
- functions (public, callable from the parent; MCP lists them as `Class|WBPShopCard|<name>` node types inside other Blueprints, so a widget-to-widget call is possible, only not needed): SetCard(Card FRRUpgradeCard, Price int, bAffordable bool): texts from ValueText / DisplayName / TargetText / LoreText, StatIcon = `GetStatIcon(Stat)` at 156 x 156, tier colour on FrameAccents, FrameGlow and CardValue (grey 0.55, green 0.2/0.8/0.25, blue 0.2/0.45/1, purple 0.65/0.25/0.9, yellow 1/0.85/0.1, same as UpgradeChoice) through TintCard(Color); card visible, SoldPanel Collapsed, PriceText = Price, price row and button shown; bAffordable true: button enabled with BuyLabel, price white, no dim; false: button disabled (greyed by Slate) with ShortLabel, price red (1, 0.35, 0.3), DimShade shown over the card. SetBought(): card and frame Collapsed, SoldPanel shown with SoldLabel, price row and button Hidden (they keep their space so the row does not shift), button disabled; this is the empty slot look. RefreshFromShop(): gets the shop subsystem (`Get Intermission Shop`), `GetSlot(SlotIndex)`; slot found and not sold: SetCard(slot Card, Price, `CanBuySlot(SlotIndex)`); else SetBought. HoldIfBought(BoughtSlot): if BoughtSlot == SlotIndex, bHolding = true then StartJuice (only the bought card reacts; OnSlotBought fires once per successful BuySlot, never on refusal, rr.Shop.Buy included). Buy button juice (task-343-01, values provisional, Q10 of [[task-343-shop-buy-juice]], no sound): variables bJuiceActive (bool), JuiceStart (float), TierColor (LinearColor, stored by TintCard, so SetCard sets it). StartJuice: JuiceStart = GetRealTimeSeconds, bJuiceActive = true, JuiceStep once. JuiceStep: E = real time - JuiceStart; drives the button scale, BuyFlash opacity and SparkStep(I, E, Spark) per spark; at E >= 0.35 clears bJuiceActive. Event Tick runs JuiceStep only while bJuiceActive (real time, not game time). Scale 1 to 0.88 linear over 0 to 0.06 s, then 0.88 + 0.12 * easeOutBack(u) over 0.06 to 0.30 s with c1 = 8.25, c3 = 9.25 (peak 1.12 near 0.157 s, exactly 1 at 0.30 s; c1 chosen by the bp agent to meet Q10). Flash white opacity 0.8 * (1 - E/0.2) (0.8 peak is the bp agent's choice, not decided). 8 sparks at angle I * 45 deg, radius 64 * (1 - (1-p)^2) with p = E/0.35, scale 1 to 0.5, opacity 1 to 0, colour TierColor. RefreshUnlessHolding(): RefreshFromShop when not bHolding. EndPurchaseFx() (public, contract with the parent; changed in task-344-01): bHolding = false then StartBurn; the sold look is no longer shown at once, EndBurn shows it at the end of the burn. [[wbp-shop-screen]] calls it on the bought card when its flight ends. Burn (task-344-01, same effect as the level-up pick, [[m-card-burn]]): variables bBurnActive (bool), BurnStart (float), BurnMID (dynamic material of BurnOverlay on MI_CardBurn). StartBurn: BurnStart = real time, bBurnActive = true, BurnMID set up with Tex = T_CardFrame_Stat_Baked and Tint = TierColor, BurnStep once. BurnStep: Burn parameter 0 to 1 over 0.5 s; the card face and frame fade as it advances; at 0.5 s calls EndBurn. EndBurn: clears bBurnActive, hides BurnOverlay, calls SetBought (sold look, the same SetBought as before). Event Tick has a branch that runs BurnStep while bBurnActive. Easing curve and exact fade shape of the face and frame: open (not read from the asset). ApplyShopLook(Card, Price, bAffordable) (task-348-06, called from RefreshFromShop right after SetCard, so it overrides SetCard's old red price and "Short on ore" text): TierTab shown; TierTabImg tint = tier colour x 0.45; TierTabText = tier label, full tier colour, from labels 10 to 14 of intermission_recap by rarity Grey/Green/Blue/Purple/Yellow (COMMON, UNCOMMON, RARE, EPIC, LEGENDARY by the label map of screen_texts.json, commit cdaa459c; label order beyond "COMMON..LEGENDARY" not re-read); PriceText colour (0.85, 0.45, 1.0); BuyText = BuyLabel, or ShortLabel with {n} replaced by Price - GetOreBalance when not affordable; BuyText font size set through Appearance|SetFontSize nodes: 28 when affordable (BUY), 16 when not (NEED {n} MORE) (task-353-01; Q3 of [[task-353-shop-need-label]] decided Roboto Bold 20, measured and adjusted to 16 after the capture; commits 68d93584, dde9295e, b6341f05). BuyPlateBox and BuyTextBox keep the plate height at 68 in both states (the plate height comes from content layout, not from the brushes). ApplySoldLook (task-348-06, last node of SetBought): SoldSub = label 9 ("NO REFUNDS."), FrameGroup shown at opacity 1, FrameAccents and FrameGlow tinted grey (0.45, 0.46, 0.5), TierTab Collapsed: the burnt sold look. All task-342/343/344 variables, functions, events and animations are kept. LoadLabels(): `GetScreenText("intermission_recap")` ([[sys-text-content]]), Labels[2] -> BuyLabel, Labels[3] -> SoldLabel, Labels[4] -> ShortLabel (screen_texts.json labels: 1 CONTINUE, 2 BUY, 3 SOLD, 4 NEED {n} MORE, 5 REROLL, 9 NO REFUNDS., 10 to 14 the tiers); on failure keeps the English defaults. TintCard(Color). Labels index 2, 3, 4 of screen_texts.json `intermission_recap` are read by the card itself, so the parent does not pass them.
- dispatchers: OnBuyClicked(SlotIndex int), called after BuySlot on every click.
- logic_in_bp: display only (text, visibility, colours); the purchase itself is the C++ call BuySlot.
